## What changes

### Worth knowing (P7 + C4)
From the third day the app runs, the dock's What's new card brings one "Worth knowing" line a day. It reaches everyone, new installs included.

- **When a line goes:** each line stays until it's shown or dismissed. The next one comes the following day.
- **Every line has a real Show me:**
  - Ctrl + Click a mod: a sample check, with its mod rows flashed.
  - In Ctrl+Space: "2 div + 30c", where an item drops, a map's cards, and "keys".
  - Your own price on the Net worth Holdings tab.
  - The Hotkeys page in Settings.
- **A line is skipped when:**
  - the player already used the gesture (its tip is retired);
  - What's new is already listing the same thing;
  - its Show me can't work here (a non-English client, or the stash reader is off).
- **No line at all** with Tips off or What's new off, or in a map.
- The lines live in `tab-bar/worth-knowing.ts`, and their state is in `woke.hints` (`since`, `wk`).

### Price check tour (P6)
- It runs on the 3rd check in the window, never on the small card by the item.
- Three small cards, one after another:
  1. The mod rows: click, Ctrl + Click, right-click.
  2. poe.ninja's price.
  3. The listings.
- A step is skipped when that check hasn't got it, and the count follows ("1 of 2").
- Got it or × ends it for good. Closing the check without finishing shows it once more, at most.
- Window tip lines wait while the tour is up, so only one hint shows at a time.

### Pins (F8)
A pinned card's head gets a "?" that lists:
- drag the title (towns and maps keep their own spot);
- the wheel over the % for see-through;
- click the pin to unpin.
