## What
The window right-click menu now says **Minimize window / Hide window**, and Hide window shows a 10 s note with Undo ("Open it again from its icon or the launcher").

## Why
In the Loot filter editor the menu opens over a rule's own Show/Hide buttons. Guy clicked the menu's Hide expecting it to hide the rule; the whole window vanished, which looked like a crash. Nothing crashed: reproduced over CDP, Hide set the widget hidden with no errors.

## Review fixes
Undo does nothing if the window was removed meanwhile (would throw and leave the toast stuck) or if an exclusive window (settings, price check) opened since (would close it).
