Guards on that rule:
- The query must be at least 4 letters. "reg" stays Regal Orb.
- An item whose name is exactly the query keeps its price first. "stacked deck" still lists Stacked Deck ahead of the Stacked decks tool.
- Looser matches don't count. "eye" doesn't start "Watcher's Eye finder", so Prices stay first.

Without a lead, the order is unchanged: Tools come after the lookup cards.
