Fixes the Mod details regression from #298 (in 0.4.0) and Guy's "why don't most mods show a tier".

- **Each mod once.** Mod details listed a mod again under every line it feeds, so a hybrid or a mod in a total showed two or three times, its other lines struck through as `#%`. Now each mod shows once, under the first line it feeds, other lines dimmed with their rolls. The share box only shows on lines several mods add up to.
- **Toggle in reach.** The toggle moves from under the list to the stats header. With details on, the list could grow past the window and push the toggle off it.
- **Tiers on totals.** PoE mode's tier gutter showed nothing for any line two mods add up to (totals, pseudos). It now shows each mod's tier, `T1/2`, when all have one; gold when all are T1. Implicits and crafted mods have no tier in the item text, so they stay blank.
