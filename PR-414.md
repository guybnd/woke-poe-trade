Brings in the outside "Game look" PR (#410 by bezrq: PoE 1 / PoE 2 window art, Options-style settings). PoE 1 art is the new default, as Guy picked. It also includes the polish Guy art-directed on his dev build.

## Game look
- Roll bars: gold fill with a faint glow from about 70%. Tier tags are bevelled badges, and header chips use one dark-well style.
- Title crest fits the bar (title-fit.ts). The crest / plain-title switch no longer flickers.
- One shared look list (WOKE_LOOKS) feeds Settings, the price check palette and the rail.
- Game scrollbar in the price check. Popovers are dark game panels, and the divine splitter opens below its button.

## Price check (PoE mode)
- Ticked lines are centred with no checkbox. The tier button opens a pseudo split.
- Element words get a soft colour.
- A total from one mod takes that mod's colour; only totals of several mods keep the total colour.
- Eldritch implicits show their altar glyph and six tier pips.
- Filler mods (reflect, light radius, stun duration, attribute requirements, stun recovery) are no longer auto-ticked at T1.
- Stat header:
  - no split bar for a single damage type
  - a unique's fixed APS / crit / defences stay clickable and search at least the item's value
  - no text selection

## Listings
- Tooltip parity with the price check:
  - eldritch altar and tier, from the new `public/data/implicit-sources.json`, built by `scripts/gen-implicit-sources.py` from RePoE
  - corruption-implicit diamond
  - enchants in their own block, scourge colour
  - unrevealed veiled mods
  - Delve / Incursion / Essence / Betrayal / influence icons by the mod name
- Socket rings with a count badge, and a 6L/6S Sockets column on six-socket bases.
- Stat columns for "as Extra X Damage" are named by element (→Fire, →Cold, →Light).
