> **TL;DR** New **Fates** module for Covens (3.30). Press **Shift + F** on a Fate pick or Invoke Fates: it reads the cards off the screen, marks the Fate to take, and lists click orders (C1 › C3 › C2) with the reward icons each pays. Played and tested in game on a 3440×1100 ultrawide.

## What it does
- **Pick window:** a green **Take** mark over the best offer (with reasons, e.g. "Triples Golemancer's rewards", "Completes Gems (2/2)"), #2/#3 on the others, and a side list of what you could invoke after taking each.
- **Invoke Fates:** C1, C2… over your collected cards and every distinct outcome as a click order, best first, with reward icons and counts (×4); unfinished barred rewards show faded as 1/2.
- **Gear modifiers:** gear rewards (Boots, Wand, Axe…) share one icon whatever modifier their items get ("a Physical modifier", "no Attack modifiers"); only the tooltip says. The hotkey moves the cursor over each gear reward for ~0.1 s (no clicks or keys), OCRs the tooltips, and puts the cursor back. Only offers on a pick; already-known rewards are skipped; the auto-refreshes never move the cursor.
- **Marks go by themselves** within ~1 s of the window closing (a quick look every 400 ms, only while marks are up), and re-read when cards move into slots. No window takes focus.
- **Settings:** worth per reward and per gear modifier, highest tier you'll fight per Fate, unlocked slots, and a window-size override.

## Rules it follows (checked against in-game screenshots)
- Cards fill the slots left to right in click order, so a click order is a slot order.
- Invoking a second copy of a Fate uses a slot, upgrades the first copy's tier and **triples** its rewards; the second copy's rewards are lost. At most two copies per area.
- Rewards with a 2- or 3-part bar pay once that many parts are invoked across all Fates; a tripled copy counts 3 parts; leftovers pay nothing.

## How it reads the screen
- No OCR for the cards: zero-mean template matching against the game's own Augury sprites (card banner, art, tier feather, 47 reward icons, 2/3-part bars), the same approach as `stash-read.ts`. The UI is 4K sprites scaled by window height / 2160, so any resolution and ultrawide work.
- Reward data (25 Fates, 3 tiers, reward icons) generated from the 3.30 client tables (`AuguryCards`, `AuguryCardTiers`, `AuguryRewardOutcomes`).
- OCR (the shared worker) loads only for gear tooltips, and stops after 2 minutes idle. Cursor moves use a small PowerShell helper calling `SetCursorPos` (Electron can't move the cursor), up while used and gone after 2 minutes. Windows only.
