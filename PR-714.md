This follows up #713. Guy: "we do not have a vaal it mode for greater exceptionals? from level 3 to 4 corrupt".

- The Vaal EV used to start from 20/20 every time. It now starts from the highest clean level that poe.ninja prices at 20% quality: 20/20 for most gems, 3/20 for exceptional supports and 5/20 for awakened gems. It returns nothing when a gem is only priced at level 1.
- The chip, card and Ctrl+Space card show the level they started from, e.g. "Vaal 3/20". The search now considers gems priced at 2/20 and above.
- A low-quality result with no row of its own is priced at a version it beats on both level and quality, such as a clean 1/0. With no such version it still counts as 0, and the card says so.
- A separate reviewer looked at this change. Its findings are fixed: the price floor is now a real lower bound, the regex is tighter, and the stale comments are updated.
