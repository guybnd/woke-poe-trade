**Why it looked dead**
- The hero was a still game screenshot (hero-plaza), which reads as a paused video.
- The Price Check clip under it only played once 35% of its section was on screen. A 900px-tall window shows 31% at load, so it sat on its still until you scrolled. Reproduced on the live site and on a local build of master.

**Change**
- The hero plays a 12 s muted loop cut from the highlight clips (loot filter, Ctrl+Space, price check), built by `docs/scripts/hero-loop.mjs` (ffmpeg; rerun after new clips). Its first frame is the still for reduced motion and while it loads.
- Clips (and the hero) play once 15% of their section is on screen (`intersectionRatio`), and pause again below that.
- The hero video has no `autoplay`: the observer starts it, so a page opened scrolled down doesn't decode it off screen (review finding).
