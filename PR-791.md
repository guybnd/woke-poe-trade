## What
Guy picked A + C on the [mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/Fzrunv53XND8k44SE6tS1Q):
- **Each group can be Open, Folded or Off.** Open shows every row. Folded shows the top 3 and a "+N more" row; click it or press Enter to open the rest, and its first new row is selected. Off leaves the group out and adds a "N groups off · show" row at the end. Every group starts Folded. Answer, Prices, Uniques on a base, Recent and Things to try always show in full. In a folded group, rows whose name starts with the query come first, and they stay first when the group is opened.
- **Chip row** (All · Prices · Items · Facts · Tools · Settings · Web, each with a count). A chip shows its groups in full, including ones that are off. Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right step through the chips. A new search starts on All. A chip that has nothing for the new query goes back to All.
- **Where to set it:** a cog in the search bar swaps the results for the list of groups (Enter or Esc closes it). Settings › General › **Search** has the same list. The search hotkey's link now points to that page, and Ctrl+Space finds the page ("what search shows").
- Saved on the search widget as `groups`, which holds only the changes from the defaults.
- A jump to a row inside a fold or behind a chip (a beast's recipe, an answer) opens that group first.
- Also fixes a tooltip gap found while counting: the currency conversion heading in the detail pane had no item tooltip.

Background: across 57 everyday searches, the spam came from long groups, not unwanted ones. "chaos" gave 15 groups and 39 rows.

## Review
A separate adversarial review found these, and all are fixed in the second commit:

- With the cog list open, the keyboard still drove the hidden list.
- A jump with the same query didn't reveal a hidden row.
- The selection jumped after "groups off" and "+N more".
- All's count included rows from groups that are off.
- The page description had the wrong wording.
