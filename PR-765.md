## What
Price checking an unidentified unique jewel (or any one-slot unique) no longer asks which unique it is. The copied text names only the base, so the app guesses from the art:

- At the hotkey, if the copied text is an unidentified unique, main keeps a 3.4-cell square around the mouse from the game screenshot, before the overlay can draw over it (`main/src/woke/unid-art.ts`).
- The price check asks `unid-art-guess` with the candidates; main downloads their poecdn icons (cached) and runs a masked zero-mean colour correlation at several cell sizes, including quad tabs (`unid-art-match.ts`, pure). It yields between candidates so main isn't blocked.
- Sure (best beats every other art by 0.05): the price check opens on that unique with a "Guessed from the art" strip; Change lists all candidates, best match first, with poe.ninja prices. Not sure: the picker, best matches first. No art (ground, chat link, big items): the picker, dearest first, with prices.
- Unid unique jewels poe.ninja prices show the poe.ninja verdict and search trade (unidentified listings) only on Check live offers, as for exchange items (#761).

In a simulation with all 112 unique jewel icons drawn into fake slots (1080p/1440p/4K, normal and quad tabs, noise, brightness), 643 of 648 were right and none of the sure guesses were wrong. Replica twins share art and can't be told apart. Not yet tried against the real game.
