## Bug
Dragging a pinned panel while the game has focus lets go after moving it a bit (mostly upward).

## Cause
uiohook reports no `mousemove` while a button is held, so main's last known cursor is the press point. As the pin moves, the page resends its zone each frame and `WokeHoverTracker.update()` checks the new zone against that stale point. Once the pin has moved past it, main releases the card / gives the mouse back to the game, the window goes click-through and the drag dies.

## Fix
`main/src/woke/hover.ts`: a left press that landed on the overlay (in an activate zone, mouse taken or overlay interactable) keeps the mouse until that button comes up (still bounded by `isButtonHeld`'s 30 s hook-silence cap). Cleared on mouseup, hook stop and resume. Being in the shared tracker, it covers every game-focused drag: pins, dock, glance bar, post-map plaque.

## Tests
`testing/main/overlay-click-safety.test.ts`: new test drags a pin up with stale hook coordinates (fails before the fix), plus one that a press the game got (holding to move) still gives the mouse back. Main typecheck clean.
