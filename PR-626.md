Second part of WOKEPOET-374 (Guy's picks M and P): NeverSink's cluster jewel price rules now follow poe.ninja and live on the Cluster page.

## What changes

- **Prices from poe.ninja.** NeverSink's "cluster jewels by price" rules ([2705], `jewels->clustereco`) are each one size, passive count and item level band, and they name the passive types worth showing (`EnchantmentPassiveNode`). NeverSink ships that list frozen at its release; it is now rewritten from poe.ninja's ClusterJewel prices (`cluster-prices.ts`, one request in `cluster-ninja.ts`, refreshed every 30 minutes while the filter is loaded):
  - Types worth at least the **Top** line (100c by default) go in the t1 rules. Types worth at least the **Good** line (15c) go in t2, which also keeps t1's types, as NeverSink's does.
  - Prices with fewer than 5 listings are skipped.
  - Medium rules written `EnchantmentPassiveNum <= 5` are priced over both 4 and 5 passives.
  - A rule left with nothing to name is commented out. Rules the strictness level turns off stay off.
- **Your picks.** Any passive type, per size, can be set to always top, always good, or never.
- **Order.** It's applied after price moves and before your edits, so your own changes still win.
- **One page.** A "Worth money" box at the top of Jewels › Cluster shows each price rule (grouped when several name the same types), the two lines, the price age, and the pick list. The separate "Cluster jewels by price" group leaves the rule list. Its edits count under Cluster, and search and Go to rule still reach its tiers.
- **Saving.** Settings live in `presets.clusterPrices`, on by default. While they are at their defaults they leave older saves matching, so nothing reads "unsaved". New prices reach the file by themselves, like the safety net, when nothing of yours is unsaved.
