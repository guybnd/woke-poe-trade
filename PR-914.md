This adds Guy's audit picks to the list beside a price check.

**What a check now shows beside the item**
- **A divination card**: a "Full stack" section, when the reward is one named unique or currency. It shows the stack's price against the reward's, and the gain.
  - Example: The Doctor at 40 div gives Headhunter at 50 div, so the gain is +10 div.
  - A corrupted reward shows no Makes or Gain, because poe.ninja prices the plain item.
  - `rewardOf()` is pulled out of `cardsFor`.
- **A gem**: its base and every transfigured kind, priced at the checked gem's level and quality.
  - Not shown for Vaal or corrupted gems, because the Font takes neither.
  - Each base gem's kinds are cached.
- **A normal base**: "Uniques on <base>", the uniques an Orb of Chance can make, dearest first.
  - Only uniques that drop at random, per the wiki drop data, and never `Replica ` ones.
  - Only for normal items that aren't corrupted or mirrored.
- **A Templar Vault reward**: "Drops from <vault>" with the vault's other one-item rewards, priced.
  - A vault with no other such reward isn't listed.
  - Rows that the item's own group already lists aren't repeated (the Uber Elder fragments).
  - `vaults.ts` is imported dynamically.
- **A boss-only unique that no drop group lists**: "Drops from <boss>" with the boss's other uniques.
  - The data is in `related-family/boss-drops.ts`: 62 uniques under 20 bosses (Delve, Abyss liches, Harvest, Expedition, Settlers, Uber Atziri, the Incarnations).
  - Each was checked on poedb, from its "Limit: … Dropped by" line or the boss page's unique list.
  - Six that the wiki names but poedb doesn't confirm are left out: Curiosity, The Bandit Lord's Band, Tecrod's Gaze, Mantle of Dreams, The Draugur's Lantern and Oni-Goroshi.
  - Shown only when getSources finds no drop group.
- **Oils** list their 3-to-1 ladder.
- **Ritual Splinters**: 100 make a Ritual Vessel.
