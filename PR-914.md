This builds Guy's audit picks for the list beside a price check. It covers every pick except the boss-only uniques table, which gets its own PR.

**What a check now shows beside the item**
- **A divination card**: a "Full stack" section, when the reward is one named unique or currency. It shows the stack's price against the reward's, and the gain.
  - Example: The Doctor, 8 × 900c = 40 div, gives Headhunter at 50 div, so the gain is +10 div.
  - `rewardOf()` is pulled out of `cardsFor`, so both read a reward the same way.
  - The card's "Drops in" no longer repeats the reward unpriced.
- **A gem**: its base and every transfigured kind, priced at the checked gem's level and quality. The Divine Font keeps those, so the prices compare. Vaal gems are left out.
- **A normal or magic base**: "Uniques on <base>", the uniques an Orb of Chance can make, dearest first.
  - Only uniques that drop at random, per the wiki drop data (`randomDropsOnBase`). No Heist replicas, and no league or boss-only uniques.
  - Each is priced on this base.
- **A Templar Vault reward**: "Drops from <vault>", with the vault's other one-item rewards, priced the way the vault table prices them.
  - A vault with no other such reward isn't listed (Machinist's only has Stacked Decks).
  - `vaults.ts` is imported dynamically, so startup JS doesn't grow.
- **Oils** list their 3-to-1 ladder.
- **Ritual Splinter**: 100 make a Ritual Vessel.
