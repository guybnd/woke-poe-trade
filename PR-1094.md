## What
The M8 proof (our own filter, no NeverSink file) found about 170 valuable white or blue exotic bases hidden at area 45 that NeverSink shows. Examples are talismans, Cord Belt and Basemetal Treads. #1091 adds an exotic Show to the skeleton. Two levelling Hide rules sit above it and still caught some of these bases:

- **White and blue gear for other builds:** now has `BaseType != <x1+x2 bases>`.
- **White and blue armour in other defences:** its base list now leaves out the x1 and x2 bases.

With this, exotic bases fall through to the rules below: NeverSink's, or the skeleton's exotic rule from #1091.
