This fixes Guy's feedback on the Exalted Orb price check. Only one exalt showed, only The Hoarder showed as a card, and Farric Lynx Alpha's tooltip said nothing.

**Cards that give a stack.** `cardsFor` only matched a reward of exactly one of the item, so it skipped `<currencyitem>{2x Exalted Orb}` and rewards wrapped in a size tag.
- It now reads the `Nx` count and the size wrapper. That adds 39 cards: 6 for Exalted, 5 for Divine, 4 for Chaos, and the rest across Alteration, Fusing, Mirror Shard and others.
- Each row shows what a full stack gives ("10 × The Saint's Treasure → 2").
- Rows are sorted cheapest per item first.
- Random rewards are still left out, e.g. "Elderslayer's Exalted Orb".

**More than one family per item.** `family.ts` let an item be in only one family. Rules can now be `also`, which adds a family on top of the item's own:
- Exalted Orbs: every `* Exalted Orb`, plus Awakener's Orb and Orb of Dominance. This replaces the old "Influence orbs" family.
- Chaos Orbs.
- Orbs of Annulment.

So Exalted Orb now lists Eldritch, Veiled, Tainted, the six influence exalts, Awakener's and Dominance. Tainted Exalted Orb keeps its Tainted currency list and gets the exalts as a second section.

**Beast tooltip.** Hovering a captured beast showed only its price. It now lists its Bestiary recipes:
- e.g. Farric Lynx Alpha: "Modify Mods on an Item: Add a Suffix, Remove a Random Prefix / Only works on rare items".
- That explains why it's beside Exalted Orb.
- The text is from poedb's Bestiary Recipe page, for 41 beasts.
- Aspect and portal recipes are left out, because that part of the page couldn't be read word for word.
- `beast-crafts.ts` loads on the first beast hover, so it adds nothing to startup JS.
