WOKEPOET-515, part 2a. Guy's rule from the thread: **profit counts only what the stash sees**. Faustus gets its own line, and the run shows **Total = profit + In Faustus**. Nothing needs ticking ("too manual").

- **In Faustus** holds two things:
  - Listings made during the run, at your asking price. Listings over a day old also show poe.ninja's price.
  - Sales during the run that haven't been collected yet.
- **Collected, automatically:** each stash read records the currency that came in (`arrivals`, net per currency). An arrival collects whole sales made before it in that currency, oldest first, as long as it has enough for the next one. So a 5c drop never collects a 10c sale.
- **From before, automatically:** Start reads your Faustus listings (the newest 100). A sale of one of those items is from before the run. It isn't counted, and once it's collected it comes off profit. A chip overrides this. A listing from the start that was repriced during the run isn't the run's either.
- **Listing reads:** Start and Stop each read your merchant listings: one trade search on your own account (`securable`, newest first), plus up to 10 fetches. These reads skip the price check's 5-minute cache (a `fresh` flag on `requestTradeResultList`/`requestResults`, only the woke change in that file).
- **Rare and magic maps** (Nightmare Maps drop rare) were unpriced. They are now priced at their base's poe.ninja price ("Blighted" kept) and grouped into one row. `BASELINE_V3` stops the first read after the update from booking them as a gain.

**Known guesses (in the code comments):**
- Matching is by amount, so loot currency can collect a waiting sale early. The real collection then counts as new, so the run comes right once you collect. It only stays too low if you never collect during the run.
- Arrivals are net per read: collecting and spending the same currency between two reads hides the collection.
- With over 100 listings at Start, a sale of an older one counts as the run's.
