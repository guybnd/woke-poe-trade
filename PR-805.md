WOKEPOET-515, part 2a. Guy's rule from the thread: **profit counts only what the stash sees**. Faustus has its own line, and the run shows **Total = profit + In Faustus**.

- **In Faustus:**
  - Listings made during the run count at your asking price.
  - Sales during the run whose currency isn't collected yet count at their price.
  - Listings over a day old show poe.ninja's price next to yours.
- **Reading the listings:**
  - Stop reads your merchant listings: one trade search on your own account (`securable`, newest first), then a fetch per 10 listings until one is older than the start. At most 10 fetches. The price check's rate limits apply.
  - A live run also has a "Read Faustus listings" button.
- **Sale marks:**
  - "Collected" (live run only) means the currency is in the stash now, so the sale leaves the Faustus line.
  - "From before" means the item wasn't from the run. Once collected, it comes off profit.
  - A "Mark all sales collected" link sets the mark on every sale.
- **Run totals and Ctrl+Space:** the totals show "Sold from before the run". Ctrl+Space shows In Faustus and Total.
- **Rare and magic maps** (Nightmare Maps drop rare) were left unpriced. Guy's maprun tab showed only the Zorath's Eyes.
  - They are now priced at their base's poe.ninja price ("Blighted Strand Map" keeps Blighted).
  - They show as one row, e.g. "Nightmare Map (T17, rare)".
  - A new baseline marker (`BASELINE_V3`) keeps the first read after the upgrade from booking them as a gain.
