## What

A **Gear** tab in the loot filter editor. One table replaces NeverSink's tiered gear rules (3.30 drop tiers) and, optionally, its over-quality rules. Guy tried each step on the dev build.

- **Table:** rows are Build bases, Exotic bases, Rank 1-3 and Unranked (trash). Columns are Magic and Rare. Each cell is the lowest drop tier shown: Off, Always (no tier too), or 2-5. The Exotic row's Off is "NS" (only NeverSink's exotic rules decide). Quick buttons: Magic off, Magic like rares, Only my build's bases, Stricter, Looser.
- **Looks:** the game's rarity colour for the text (on by default, since NeverSink paints magic and rare alike), and a look of your own for magic and for rare.
- **Over-quality:** a per-row quality line (Off, 21, 24, 28, 30%).
- **How much gear this shows:** bases shown per tier and rarity, split by row. Click a cell for its bases.
- **Test drops:** 36 random gear drops with the decorators you pick (influenced, fractured, synthesised, corrupted, over-quality), run through the whole filter as built. Shown and hidden groups, and each drop links to the rule that decided it (Rules, or the table's Looks).
- **Base ranks:** lanes for Build (from the PoB build's rare bases), Rank 1-3, Exotic, Unranked and Never. Kind filters use item art and weapon subtypes, defence filters use the game's colours, plus search and drop level. Multi-pick and Move to.

Off by default: the filter only changes once you touch the table, and "Back to NeverSink" undoes it.

## How

- `gear.ts`: `applyGear` runs in `compose.ts` after your edits and before the presets. It comments out NeverSink's `gear->tiered` rank/any rules (and over-quality ones when that table is on), and writes `woke->gear` rules after NeverSink's stranded fractured/veiled rules, with NeverSink's looks per rank and tier (your restyles included). White gear follows the rare column, as NeverSink's rules took all three.
- Settings live at `presets.gear` (`normalizeGear`, which also drops base names with quotes or line breaks from share codes). "Hide low item tiers" and quiet/beams treat the table's rules as NeverSink's.
- UI in `GearTab.vue`, `gear-bases.ts` and `gear-pile.ts`. `StyleEditor` gets a `lookOnly` prop.
