## What

Guy hid every map tier but still saw maps drop. The edits were unsaved: the only sign was a small grey "· unsaved changes" in the footer. This PR makes unsaved changes as obvious as the Atlas tree does (#524):

- The editor window gets a gold frame while changes are unsaved.
- The footer turns into a gold strip with a pulsing dot, an **Unsaved** stamp, "Not in the game until you save", and Save + reload with a `Ctrl S` hint.
- **Ctrl + S** saves and reloads. It works only from inside the editor, including from a field or after clicking something that then disappears (a chip's ×), and only when there's something to save. It doesn't fire from other widgets or on key repeat, so it never types `/itemfilter` for nothing.
- "Reload now" and its hint hide while there are unsaved changes, because saving reloads anyway.

## Checks

- vue-tsc, eslint, build and perf budgets all pass.
- e2e: the rules test now checks the Unsaved stamp shows, then saves with Ctrl+S and checks the stamp goes away. The loot-filter spec passes apart from `look strip` and `stack sizes`, which fail the same way on master.
- The adversarial review found Ctrl+S firing from other widgets and with nothing to save, plus a short strip and a stale hint. All are fixed in the second commit.
