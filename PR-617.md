## What

A **Megalomaniac searcher** window. It reads your PoB build (the one in use, or the one whose page you opened it from) or your synced character, gives it build tags, scores every notable Megalomaniac can roll, and opens a trade search for jewels with the notables you tick.

These follow Guy's picks on the mock page (WOKEPOET-372):
- **Build source D:** the PoB build or character fills in the tags. You can drop any tag or add one from about 110 tags in 11 groups, with a search box. Edits are saved per build.
- **Standalone panel:** open it from Ctrl+Space "megalo", or from a PoB build page › More › Megalomaniac searcher.
- **Layout A:** notables show as chips grouped by the build tag they fit best. Hover a chip to see the game's notable text, its tags and why it got its weight. Click it to tick or untick it. Its suggested weight (0-100) can be edited, and ↺ resets it.
- **Search:** Count (need 1, 2 or 3, default 2) or Weighted sum (the trade site's weight group, with a suggested minimum total), plus an optional divine price cap.

## Scoring

Each notable line is matched against the tag regexes in `megalo/tags.ts`:
- Main-skill tags weigh 3, other tags 2.
- On a minion build, your own damage lines count ×0.25. On any other build, minion damage lines count ×0.25.
- Defence lines (your resistances, avoidance, block, suppression) don't count as a damage type or skill kind.
- The best fit gets 100. Ticked by default: weight 40 or more, at most 12 notables.

The 9 new 3.30 notables that the trade data doesn't know yet are left out, and the panel says so.
