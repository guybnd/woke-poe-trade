## What
- **Tool icons:** `moduleIcon` looked up `icon.item` only among base items (`ITEM`), so tools drawn with a unique or a gem fell back to their Font Awesome glyph. It now also tries `UNIQUE` and `GEM`. This fixes the Watcher's Eye finder (Guy's report), the Forbidden pair finder (Forbidden Flame) and PoB builds (Enlighten Support). The Ctrl+Space Watcher's Eye cards use the jewel art too.
- **Match mode (Guy's ask):** "Has all / Any N" is replaced by three toggles: At least 1, 2 and 3. At least 2 is the default, and an option is disabled when there are fewer picks than it needs. When the toggle covers every pick, the search is an `and` and Price check works. When it covers fewer, it is a count search for the trade site, and Price check says why it is off.

## Tests
- New `renderer/test/woke/module-icons.test.ts`: every tool with an item icon resolves art. It fails without the fix for watchers-eye, forbidden and pob-builds.
- `testing/e2e/watchers-eye.spec.ts`: checks the default is At least 2, that 3 is disabled with 2 picks, and that At least 1 turns Price check off.
