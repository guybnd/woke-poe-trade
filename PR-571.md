## Bug
Dragging the Atlas strategy list's bottom edge stopped growing after a few cm.

## Cause
`WPanel.startResize` capped growth at the room below the whole widget. The widget's bottom is set by the tall tree and loadout panels beside the list, so the list could only grow by the small gap under them.

## Fix
- `edgeSlack` (panel-fit.ts): the gap from a panel's moving edge to the widget's edge, when a bigger neighbour beside it left that gap. It is 0 if another panel sits past that edge (the next panel in a row or column), or if the gap is just a few px of padding, so single-panel widgets behave as before.
- `resizeAxis` takes `slack`: the panel can grow by room + slack, and the anchor moves only for growth past the neighbours.

## Review
Adversarial review caught that the first version also counted the neighbours' width as slack on the right edge of the row; fixed with the "panel past the edge" check and tests.
