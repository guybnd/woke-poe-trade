Overhauls the tools launcher (the rail's grid button) to make it easier to read. Guy picked A, 3, 1, 3, 1 on the mockups page and then asked for it bigger. Board ticket: WOKEPOET-373, "Tools launcher overhaul".

## What changes
- **Five columns, one per section.** Overlay now gets its own column. The section names sit on the game's heading bar (`w-category`) in the game look and stay on one line.
- **Bigger:** tool names use `--w-fs-xl`, icons are 28px, rows are 42px and the launcher is 1360px wide.
- **Never covers the rail:** the launcher opens on whichever side of the rail has room. When neither side fits the full width, it takes the roomier side and gets narrower.
- **Hover line:** the foot says what the hovered tool does (`toolDescription`: module descriptions, plus short ones for the overlay's own tools). For a switched-off or icon-row tool, it adds the hint after the description.
- **Pins:** a pinned tool keeps its gold pin and its name turns gold. An open window now shows a small blue dot instead of a gold name. An unpinned row's pin floats over the end of the row, so names keep their room.
- **Switched-off tools** stay faded in place, as before.
- **Sections:** Lab gems moves to Campaign & lab, Farming to Mapping and Wickerman to Economy (`modules.ts`). The module tables in `README.md` and `docs/tools.md` are regenerated to match.
