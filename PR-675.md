Net worth's profit per hour now counts only play time, and shows the last 1, 2 and 3 hours (WOKEPOET-414, layout A from the mocks).

- **Play time**: hideout, town, Rogue Harbour/Menagerie etc. (`isTownLike`), game closed and AFK are left out. They come from Client.txt on demand through `game-log-range` with a new `sessions` flag (game start, the line before it, AFK on/off). There's no always-on tracker; marks are read incrementally at each stash read.
- **Profit**: the refreshes' net gains (items in minus out), so price swings and tab changes don't count. A read that straddles a window's start counts its share of play inside the window.
- **UI**: summary row `Per hour 1h · 2h · 3h`, with hover detail (play time, what was left out). Today's rate (tooltip on "today") and the dock glance use play time too. Ctrl+Space "per hour" / "profit" shows a card from the saved glance.
- Needs 10 min of play in a window before it shows a number.

An adversarial review found three bugs, all fixed with tests: time before the game started counted as play; a crash in a map dropped that map's play; AFK in the hideout returned as play after trimming.
