_Requested by **Guy**_

First rollout step of the game look (WOKEPOET-183), on top of #296. Hybrid and Native only; Modern and Classic don't change.

- **Settings:** bronze frame with corner marks, dark header with a gold fade line, small caps group labels at 13px, the open page pressed in, a recessed search well, square link chips, gold marks on APT's radios, checkboxes and toggles (disabled ones stay grey).
- **Verdict strips:** Flips and mechanic prices share `.w-verdict`, a gold wash with a small caps headline.
- **Dock cards:** bronze frame, corner marks that stay put while a card scrolls, a centred small caps title in gold over a gold fade.
- **Charter leftovers:** the dock badge, offer chip, flip weight chip and link chip arrow no longer go under 11px; NewItemSearch uses tokens instead of Tailwind greys.

## Review
An adversarial bug review found that the corner marks scrolled away on tall dock cards, that disabled APT radios showed as enabled, and that the dock card title stayed in the accent colour. All three are fixed in the last commit, along with a broken title icon the shots showed.
