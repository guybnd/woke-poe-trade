This fixes two bugs Guy found in Loot › Jewels › Unique jewels on master.

- **Prices:** the Loot tab's `dropLookup` used the `market` variant rule, which takes the most valuable variant with a real market. A unique that poe.ninja lists only by variant was therefore priced by its rarest roll. Voices showed 164,040c (the 1-passive line, about 1,200 div), along with a Timeless jewel and some Foulborn rolls. A new `drop` rule takes the cheapest variant with a real market instead, since that's what most drops are. The safety net and the Prices tab are unchanged.
- **Rows:** WItemRow's art takes a grid cell, so the Unique jewels grid gains an `auto` art column. The price column also widens so a 5-digit price and its pill fit.
