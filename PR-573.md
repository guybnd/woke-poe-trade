## What
The Prices tab's "Not moved" list said "132" but showed only the first 20 rows, had no bulk actions, and gave no way to tell why items were held back.

- **Every held-back item is listed**, 40 at a time with "Show all N".
- **A short line explains the list**: prices say these items belong in another tier, but they stay put. It names each reason that applies (kept by you, thin market, price jumped) and what you can do about it.
- **Filter chips** by reason and by group, for example "kept by you · 90" and "Currency · 40".
- **Checkboxes**: pick all or none, shift + click for a range, and an "N of M picked" counter.
- **Bulk "Unlock N"**: one undo step. It also drops moves made by hand in Rules, and the note says so.
- **Bulk "Move N anyway"**: one undo step. The button shows "· K would hide" when some of them would hide.
- **Apply bar**: "N held back" jumps to the list. "Nothing to move: tiers match today's prices" no longer reads as if all was fine. An **Undo [Ctrl][Z]** button uses the existing filter history from #566.
- Skipped rows now carry `hides`, worked out in `planRetier`, so they get a "would hide it" chip.
- Picking for the would-move list moved into a shared `usePicks` composable. Its behaviour is unchanged.
- Each row's look comes from a per-doc map instead of searching the blocks for every row.
