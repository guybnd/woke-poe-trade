## Why
#796 didn't catch the real case. Rain Tempter was **98 div** (20,140c), not 98c. Guy's saved daily prices had it at 0.11–1c every day from Nov 17 to Nov 22. poe.ninja's 7-day line showed only +2,362% because that line started from an earlier fake (818c). The card is thin (5 of 7 days empty), so the 6-hour jump hold let it through, and #796's line-based check was anchored to the fake.

## What changed
- **Main:** a new `market-daily-prices` op (`dailyPrices`) returns the saved daily chaos price of the named stash items for the last 14 calendar days. It reads only that window's day files.
- **`net-worth/history.ts`:** loads those prices once per UTC day and name set. A row is used only if its newest saved price is at most 2 days old, because the collector runs only while a market widget does. Two types that fold into one key (a relic and its unique) are dropped. The cache is cleared when the league changes.
- **`valuation.ts` `priceStep`:** the step check runs on the saved prices when there are 2 or more (3 or more for gear), and on poe.ninja's line otherwise.
  - A step of 5x or more counts at the usual price until it has held 3 priced days, or until poe.ninja's line has held it 3 days.
  - A step of **50x or more** never confirms on time alone.
  - Rain Tempter now counts at 1c.
- **Hover:** the tooltip says when a step is big enough to wait about 2 weeks.
- **Tests:** Guy's real numbers, the main op, the freshness rule and type clashes.

## Review
A separate adversarial review found:
- Saved days were counted instead of calendar days, and stale history could hold a real rise indefinitely.
- History from the old league was used after a league switch.
- Type collisions within one key.
- Every day file of the league was read.

All are fixed in the third commit.
