Stacked on #434. Retarget to master once that merges.

## What
`apply` added junk items from the trade item list, and the hourly upstream job runs `apply`. In that list, map, Scrying Orb and Mercenary Warrant variants carry a `disc` field, and their `type` is an internal key ("43432", "AbyssalTemplar"), not a name. `diff()` now:
- skips nameless `disc` entries. A unique's variants carry its name, and the existing name check catches them.
- skips any name without a letter.
- puts new gems (Arcane Link) under "Left out" instead of adding them as plain items without gem data.

## Duplicate stats
#434 covers these: `remap` runs before `diff`, so a moved id is ours before `diff` sees it, and it isn't added again (`check` reports 0 to move). The roughly 90 grant and support texts `apply` would still add are ids the trade site keeps live *beside* our old ones, for example "Grants Level # Lightning Warp" (effect id) next to our "...Lightning Warp Skill" (old id, still live). They are different texts on different ids, so they don't clash. I tried folding them into our stats, and it also caught the Forbidden Shako random-support stats, which are a different stat. So they stay as new stats.

The hourly job already opens a pull request and never pushes to master.
