## What

Guy's pick **F1** for the stat block at the top of the price check, in PoE mode (Tooltip lines). Ticket WOKEPOET-212.

- **Weapon:** a big DPS number with a thin roll bar, a bar split by damage type (phys grey, fire red, cold blue, lightning yellow, chaos purple), a legend of phys / ele / chaos DPS (each with a roll bar), and APS and crit chips on the right.
- **Armour:** one big number per defence (armour, evasion, ES, ward), each with a roll bar in its colour. Block and base percentile are chips.
- **Clicking:** a number picks its filter and gets a gold outline, and its usual row opens under the header in click order. Clicking it again unpicks it. The header never changes shape. Clicking never searches.
- **Rows under the header:** unpicked property lines aren't listed any more, because the header shows them. Every DPS part shows even when today's row would be hidden (pDPS under a 67% share). A part picked from the header always shows its row.
- **Roll bar:** fill = (value − min) / (max − min). The bar is grey when nothing on the item can roll it. A fixed unique roll can't be clicked.

## Chaos in Total DPS

The trade site's `weapon_filters.dps` counts chaos. On a live Rebuke of the Vaal listing, `extended.dps` 352.07 = pdps 166.85 + edps 140.39 + chaos 44.8. Total DPS now includes local chaos damage ("Adds # to # Chaos Damage" × APS) in its value and its range.

- The pDPS/eDPS share rules that pick and hide rows still leave chaos out, so default picks don't change.
- A weapon with phys + chaos only or ele + chaos only now gets a Total DPS row, unticked.
- There's no chaos DPS trade filter, so the chaos legend entry toggles the chaos mod's own row.
