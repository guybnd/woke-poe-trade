## What

The mouse wheel now steps every number box in the app without clicking into it first. Before, only price check's number chips/tiles, affix caps and Hide < had their own wheel code; the other ~60 boxes (price check's mod row min/max among them) relied on Chromium, which only steps a focused box.

## How

- `renderer/src/web/woke/ui/number-wheel.ts`: one shared behaviour. A passive `pointerover` on the window hooks a wheel listener onto each number box the pointer reaches (no blocking wheel listener on the window, so scrolling elsewhere isn't slowed). Loaded as its own chunk from `main.ts` so startup JS stays in budget.
- Steps by the box's `step`, keeps to `min`/`max`, snaps to the step grid without float drift; `step="any"` adds or takes 1; a box with no `min` stops at 0.
- A scroll already going on (a wheel on something else in the last 500 ms) carries on over a box instead of being taken by it; non-cancelable events are left alone.
- `input` fires each notch, `change` once the wheel rests (300 ms, or pointer leaves), so savers and the loot filter rebuild once per spin.
- Boxes stepped by their wrapper are marked `data-own-wheel` (FilterBtnNumeric, FilterAffixCaps, HideChip) and keep one step a notch. `data-no-wheel` opts a box out.
- Price check roll boxes: an empty box starts from the item's roll (`data-wheel-from`), and wheeling switches the row on.
- `WField` now passes `min`/`max`/`step` to its input (they used to land on the wrapper span).
