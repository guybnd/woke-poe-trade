_Requested by **Guy**_

After a streamer visit, visitors scrolled past the tabbed slides and the stills didn't land. Guy picked looping clips (M1) and a picture strip under each shot, with the name plates kept as tabs (C), plus a new section order.

## What changes

- **Clips.** Each highlight opens on a short loop of the real overlay in use, recorded headlessly over saved game frames. Nothing runs over the game. The loops are price check (whole screen, Ctrl+D, then hovering listings), PoB (loadout, tabs, passive tree), filter (Alt+F, then re-tier), market (the Flips icon outlining a Delirium tab), the League mechanics tabs, and quick search typing. Each comes as 1080p WebM and MP4, 6 MB in total. A clip loads only when it is on show and its section is on screen, and only one plays at a time. With reduced motion, visitors get the still and no clip loads.
- **Picture strip.** A row of thumbnails sits under each shot, so it's clear there's more to see. A clip's thumbnail has a play mark. Thumbnails are made at build time, alongside the 1280 px and phone copies (`docs/scripts/phone-shots.mjs`).
- **Order.** Price check, PoB, filter, market, on screen, quick search, then the rest.
- **Recorder.** `testing/e2e/clip.ts` takes a screenshot each step at device pixels, through a camera that eases between areas. It draws a game cursor and keycaps, and encodes with ffmpeg. Record the clips with `WOKE_LANDING_CLIPS=1 npx playwright test landing-highlights -g "clip:"`.
- **Site check.** `docs/scripts/check-phone.mjs` now checks that the thumbnails load and pick their slide, that the first clip plays and only one plays, and that reduced motion loads no clip.

## Checks run locally

- `npx vitepress build`, then `node scripts/check-phone.mjs`: OK at phone 390, phone 360, desktop, and with reduced motion.
- `npx playwright test --list e2e/landing-`: 52 tests load.
- All six `clip:` tests pass and record.
