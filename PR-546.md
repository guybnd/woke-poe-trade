## What

Price checking a bulk item now lists the rest of its family in the side panel beside the price check: all fossils, resonators, catalysts, oils, delirium orbs, essences of the same tier, omens, astrolabes, threads, scarabs of the same mechanic, influence orbs, and more. Rows are sorted by poe.ninja price, and your item is marked. Long families scroll inside the panel.

Clicking a row opens that item's price check in place. That is the only trade search. The list itself uses the item data and poe.ninja rows already in memory. Each family is read from the item data once.

The panel that already existed for items in a drop group keeps working. An essence still shows its other tiers below the family, and "Drops in" leaves out items the family already lists.
