## What
Maps where you go into a Starfall Crater (the Black Knight's fallen star) get a **Starfall Crater** chip in the after-map toast, Map history (row text and filter chip) and the Ctrl+Space "maps" card, the same way Mirage does (#700).

```
Generating level 83 area "SettlersBossFallenStar" with seed 3511095274
: You have entered Starfall Crater.
The Black Knight: Only the worthy may pass!
Generating level 83 area "MapWorldsFortress" with seed 3822646249
```
