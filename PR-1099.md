- "Any unique, 6-link" used to be priced at the best 6-linked unique on poe.ninja, so every 6-linked unique was Tink. It is now priced at 20 Orbs of Fusing, with a Big floor.
- 6-link rows, the any-base one and Tabula, move to a new **6-links** section (`uniqlinks`). It has its own look below Tink: white on unique brown, a white edge, a white beam and a star.
- Tabula is priced by its Fusings, or by its base's plain unique when that is worth more, never by its 6-link market.
- A base whose plain unique is worth more than the row gets its own rule at that price (`splitPer`), so a 6-linked chase unique keeps Tink. Alt+F's card prices that base the same way (`rowForBase`).
- The floor stays on when Big is turned off for the page, the way Collecting does.
- Review fixes: the section id has no digit, because normalizeCurrency drops keys that have one. A bias edit no longer stops the split.
