When the campaign card is on "Complete Trial of Ascendancy", Izaro's trial-finished line in Client.txt now ticks the step.

**Signal.** Guy's own Client.txt (10 characters) shows Izaro says exactly one line per trial, said when it is finished. The line is the same every run, and the same in the act 6-10 and endgame lab copies of that trial. Nothing is logged when you only enter: a 2-second visit to an endgame trial logged nothing.

| Trial | Line |
|---|---|
| Lower Prison | Belief is the strongest metal of them all. |
| Chamber of Sins 2 | There is a fine line between consideration and hesitation. ... |
| Crypt 1 | Shine boldly so that all might find you when the night falls. |
| Crematorium | Allow your wisdom to be tempered by the flames of the past. |
| Catacombs | An emperor must bear two blades. Hope in the left hand. Surety in the right. |
| Imperial Gardens | An emperor must know precisely where he stands. |

**Change**
- `log-parse.ts`: `isTrialFinished(line)` matches `] Izaro: ` plus one of the six exact lines. Lines from chat channels (#, @ and similar) don't match.
- `route-parse.ts`: `advanceOnTrial` moves past the next trial step in the current zone. It stops at the first step in another zone, so town hand-ins are never skipped.
- `WidgetCampaign.vue`: this counts only in campaign zones and while the route is awake. The lab and maps are ignored.

**Checks:** lint, renderer typecheck, all 3654 unit tests (9 new) and the build pass. An adversarial review found no defects.

**Limit:** if you finish the act 1 Lower Prison trial on your first visit, before the town hand-ins, the line ticks nothing. The step is passed later as you walk on into the Upper Prison.
