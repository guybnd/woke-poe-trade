## Why
A Discord user's deck session listed The Inoculated, Thunderous Skies and Glimmer of Hope at the bottom with "—" and the note "cards have no poe.ninja price and count as 0".

Checked live (Covens): poe.ninja's dense card list has 403 cards and leaves out 25 deck cards. Together they're about **12% of deck draws** by the Prohibited Library weights (The Carrion Crow, More is Never Enough, Rain Tempter, The Arena Champion, Glimmer of Hope, Thunderous Skies, Her Mask, Lost Worlds, The Inoculated, Last Hope...). They aren't on the exchange overview either, and the old stash item overview is gone (404), so poe.ninja has no other source for them. They trade too rarely to price. There was no fallback for this anywhere: the deck tracker has always counted them as 0 and kept them listed (`isCheap` returned false for no price on purpose).

## What
- `totals()` takes a `common(name)` predicate. A card with no price that is common folds into the "under Xc" line with the cheap cards. That only happens once some card in the session has a price, so nothing folds while prices load. With the cut-off at 0, every card is still listed. It counts as 0 either way, so Got, Result and the chart don't change.
- `isCommonCard`: the card drops in at least 1 of 2000 decks by the weights. All 25 cards poe.ninja leaves out pass that test. A rare card with no price (a new card, say) stays listed with "—".
- The note now says "N cards aren't on poe.ninja (too few trades) and count as 0". Hovering the note or a "—" explains why. In the folded list, a card with no price shows "—" instead of "0 c".
- `deck.ts` still doesn't import the weights JSON (Config.ts imports deck.ts). DeckSummary passes the predicate in.
