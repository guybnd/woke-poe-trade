## Why
Several users say deck sessions show inflated card prices. In a Discord screenshot, Thirst for Knowledge shows at 47 div. On live poe.ninja (Covens) it reads 14,139c (72 div) with graph `[null×6, 0]`, so it had a price on one day only. It's a common card (1 in about 200 decks) that's really worth a few chaos. Net worth (#769, #796, #797) and the price check (#838) already catch fakes like this. The deck tracker still used poe.ninja's raw price everywhere: Got, Result, the big-hit popup, Expected and luck, and saved sessions.

The same screenshot also lists cards poe.ninja leaves out entirely (The Inoculated, Thunderous Skies, Glimmer of Hope). Those are 25 deck cards, about 12% of draws. They trade too rarely for poe.ninja to price, and there is no other poe.ninja source for them.

## What
**Fake prices (guard.ts)**
- Each card price goes through `ninjaDoubt` from ninja-trust.ts, the price check's checks, using poe.ninja's 7-day line.
- A price poe.ninja has on one day only, at 10c or more, counts as 0, since there's no earlier price to fall back on.
- A sudden 5x step counts at the usual price until the price holds.
- A thin market on its own is kept, because most cheap cards are thin.
- These rules apply everywhere `price` is used: totals, chart, big-hit popup, Expected and luck, and the record saved at the end.
- A held card shows a pause icon. Hovering it shows poe.ninja's number and why it isn't counted, and a note says how many cards are held.
- Past sessions: `withoutHeld` drops a saved price that today's check still calls fake. It only does that when the saved price is at least half of poe.ninja's held number, so an older real price stays.

**Cards poe.ninja doesn't list**
- A common card (1 in 2000 decks or more) with no price folds into the "under Xc" line once some card has a price.
- A rare card with no price stays listed.
- The note says "aren't on poe.ninja (too few trades)" only once prices are in. Before that it keeps the old wording (review finding).

## Tests
- Unit: stacked-decks.test.ts, 32 tests. They cover a one-day fake, a spike, real/thin/cheap one-day prices kept, `withoutHeld`, and folding.
- e2e:
  - A one-day fake Thirst for Knowledge counts as 0. It shows the held note and the pause icon title, and never pops up as a big hit.
  - Her Mask, a card poe.ninja doesn't list, folds in with the cheap cards.
- vue-tsc and eslint are clean.
