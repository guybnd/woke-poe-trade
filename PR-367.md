WOKEPOET-90, Guy's pick A: switches by itself.

- **Per-character state.** Each character keeps its own route step, gems, step times and asleep state (`chars` on the campaign widget). The one you follow lives in `step` and `state`, as before.
- **When it switches.** The log names a character only when it levels, and party members' level-ups are logged too. So the tracker switches to another character only after two of its level-ups in a row while the one you follow hasn't levelled for 20 minutes. In a party you both level, so it never fires. It never switches while a name is pinned in Settings. The one-click "follow" button switches at once.
- **Coming back.** A saved character comes back with its own route whenever its name returns: after a pin change in Settings, or when it levels first after a new character's Strand.
- **New character on the Strand.** A new character entering the Twilight Strand still resets the route. The character before is saved first, with its step times and gems.
- **Limit.** Up to 10 other characters are kept; the longest unseen one goes first.

An earlier version also switched at once to characters at level 70 or more and kept them asleep. The review showed that this breaks parties and real act 10 characters, so it's gone.
