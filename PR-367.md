WOKEPOET-90, Guy's pick A: switches by itself.

- **Per-character state.** Each character keeps its own route step, gems, step times and asleep state (`chars` on the campaign widget). The one you follow lives in `step` and `state`, as before.
- **When it switches.** The log names a character only when it levels up, and party members' level-ups are logged too. So the tracker switches:
  - at once to a character at level 70 or more (an alt in maps);
  - to a levelling character after two of its level-ups in a row with none from the one followed;
  - never while a character name is pinned in Settings.
  The one-click "follow" button uses the same switch.
- **Endgame characters.** While you follow a character at level 70+, campaign zones no longer wake the route (the "72 wakes" case).
- **New character on the Strand.** A new character entering the Twilight Strand still resets the route. The character before is saved first, so its gems and step come back with it.
