## What

A **Tier** line now sits at the top of the price check side list for uniques.

- **Drop T0–T5** shows how rare a drop the unique is. T0 is the rarest and T5 the most common. The numbers are community estimates from PoE Wiki's "Analysis of unique item tiers" (CC BY-NC, credited in the UI). Only relative rarity is shown, because the real drop weights aren't known. Open the line to see where the unique ranks among the uniques on its base.
- **Loot filter tier** shows the tier your active loot filter puts the unique in, drawn in that tier's own label look. Open the line to see:
  - the drop's look on the ground;
  - Go to rule;
  - a by-base note;
  - the dearest unique on the base, with its price.
- The filter tier handles Foulborn and replica tiers, your own rules and the safety net.
- **Chance it** rows now carry each unique's drop tier.
- New shared data file `tips/unique-drop-tiers.json` (670 uniques). The data version goes to 36.
- The line is lazy-loaded, so startup JS stays at 1638.9 KB against the 1640 budget.
- NEWS line, Show me demo (Carcass Jack, spot `unique-tier`) and release notes added.
