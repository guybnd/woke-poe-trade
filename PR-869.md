Test-only fix for the master flake in `net-worth.spec.ts`, where the dock card's Stop check, `[data-card-run]` `toHaveCount(0)`, sometimes fails.

**Cause:** this one test walks through reads and two runs. Since #847, each run's Stop also reads the stash and the Faustus listings, and then the summary is checked. Alone the test takes about 35 s, and on a loaded runner it took 40 to 45 s. The default 45 s test timeout then ran out inside the second Stop's wait. I reproduced it with 4 workers plus 4 busy CPU loops: 2 of 6 runs failed with the test timeout at that line.

I timed each Stop with log lines: about 4 to 5 s from the first click until the run tab is gone, in every run. So Stop itself never hangs, and its 30 s count wait was never hit.

**Fix:** `test.setTimeout(90_000)` for this test, as `atlas-strategies.spec.ts` does for its long tests. With the same load, `--repeat-each 8 --workers 4` gave 8 of 8 passing.
