## What
A player asked on Discord that Ctrl+Space keep their last search. Until now only a *priced* item you opened was saved, so a quick look that opened nothing left no trace.

- **Typed searches are kept.** When the palette closes, what you typed (2+ letters) is saved, even if you opened nothing. Up to 3 are kept, newest first (`queries` on the widget config, `addQuery` in `widget.ts`). A search that grows or shrinks from an older one ("tab", then "tabula") replaces it.
- **The empty box lists them first under Recent**, as "Search again" rows, above the items you opened. Enter or a click puts the text back in the box; ↓/↑ move through them. Typing hides Recent. A past search that is exactly an item's name shows that item's tooltip.
- **Save fix:** the overlay saves the config as the palette hides, *before* the palette's close watcher adds the recent (Vue runs pre-flush watchers by component uid, parent first). Closing on blur or into a tool brought no later save, so the newest recent could be lost to a restart. The close watcher now saves once if either list changed.

NEWS line `search-recent-queries` and a release note are included.
