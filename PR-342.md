Stacked on #341.

Standard collects every old league's stash tabs as "… (Remove-only)", which is how they show on the real tab list (for example `~b/o 2 chaos (Remove-only)`). In Standard the stash reader now leaves them out:
- They aren't read, so they don't use up the rate limit.
- They aren't kept in the cache, so they're left out of totals and "since last refresh".
- A remove-only tab 0 brings no items. The first call always returns tab 0's items along with the tab list, and those are dropped.
- The Net worth footer shows "N remove-only tabs skipped". The count is also saved in the cache, so it's still there after a restart.

Other leagues work as before. This adds no other tab limits.

Tests: Standard reads and caches only the other tabs, a remove-only tab 0 keeps no items, the count survives a restart, and other leagues are unchanged.
