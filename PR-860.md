## Why
Several users reported inflated prices. #858 fixed Stacked decks, but only Net worth, the price check and decks checked for fakes. Every other window read poe.ninja's raw price, so one fake could set a loot filter tier, inflate a map's card value, a farming session or a boss run. Today's example is Thirst for Knowledge at 14,139c on the one day poe.ninja had it. Guy picked "one central guard".

## What
- **`shared/price-trust.ts`**: the one-day and step checks now live here, moved unchanged from net-worth/valuation.ts and ninja-trust.ts, which re-export them. It is small and pure, so windows loaded at startup can use it.
  - `heldPrice` returns what to count instead.
  - `heldText` gives the line that explains it.
- **Saved prices (market snapshot)**:
  - `parseDenseChunks` marks a held price. It keeps poe.ninja's `chaos` and adds `held`.
  - The price index's `snapshot` now counts a held price at the usual price, or leaves it out when there is no usual price (`countedPrices`).
  - That covers every window that reads the snapshot: loot filter tiers, Boss runs, Nightmare strip, Farming, Mechanics, Atlas, Essences, Disenchant, Forbidden, Trades, Session analytics and the Drop counter.
  - The DB keeps the raw price.
- **Live prices**: `usePrice().priceOf` (card drops, item tooltips) returns the guarded `chaos` plus `held`.
- **Shown, not hidden**:
  - The item tooltip still shows poe.ninja's price, plus a line in the warn colour: "poe.ninja: 72 div, on the one day it had a price… it counts as nothing until the price holds".
  - Ctrl+Space reads the raw snapshot and marks held rows with its existing step icon and that text.

The rules are the same as the price check's: a one-day price of 10c+ (not gear) or a 5x step that hasn't held 3 days. A thin market on its own is kept.

## Not covered yet
Some windows read `usePoeninja` directly for gear and gems: gem versions, PoB unique prices, Forbidden's live path, Wickerman/Viscera, Harvest swap and Beast altar. The one-day rule doesn't apply to gear anyway. They can move to `priceOf` later.
