WOKEPOET-450. Guy's picks: opt-in reader (A), Net worth A + C, per-type summary layout 1 (ranked bars).

## What
- **Main:** `personal/trade-history.ts` reads `GET /api/trade/history/{league}` with the stash reader's sign-in (POESESSID in Electron's cookie store). The site returns the last 100 sales and limits each account to 5/min, 10/10 min and 15/3 h. The reader keeps every sale it has seen in a per-league file. Quiet reads run at most every 30 min on entering the hideout, and manual reads at most every 5 min. The limiter uses half of each window. Only a 401 or a redirect counts as signed out. Registered as reader op `trade-history`, with a broadcast after a quiet read.
- **Net worth (A):** a Sold section under Details (latest sales, today/7 days totals, vs poe.ninja), Sold today on the summary line, gold sale dots on the chart, and sale rows in the change list. These take the sold items out of their removed lines, matched by item key and count.
- **Net worth (C):** a Holdings / Sales switch. The Sales view has a range (today/7d/30d/league), ranked bars per type that filter a searchable table, and every kept sale.
- **Ctrl+Space:** "sold" answers with totals and the latest sales; an item name finds past sales.

## Review

- Sales were matched to removed lines by their display label.
- A manual Refresh followed by a hideout entry made two reads.

- Stale banners.
- Duplicate chart keys.
- Ctrl+Space priced currencies differently from the window.
