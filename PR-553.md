## What

Guy wanted to work through the Prices tab in batches and save once. He also pointed out that the switches there don't look like PoE 1.

- **Apply, don't save:** "Apply N and save" is now **Apply N**. Price moves (Apply, Move anyway, Put back, Back to NeverSink's tiers, the menu's Move) join the editor's unsaved changes, with the gold Unsaved frame. The window's **Save + reload** (Ctrl+S) writes them all once. Toasts say "not saved yet" and keep Undo.
- The Prices tab's quick steps ("Show it", "Not for me") still save on their own, but only when nothing you changed is waiting unsaved. They never sneak your staged moves into the file. `store.userDirty` is exported for this.
- **Switches in the game's art:** inside the editor, the sliding switch from #547 is now a recessed well in a bronze frame. It has On/Off in the game's small caps, and the game's checkbox coin slides across as the knob. Plain checkboxes in the editor (price move rows, the beam "temporary" box) use the coin too. This is only under the art look (`data-woke-art`). Themes without art are unchanged.
