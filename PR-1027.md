Loot filter bug hunt, batch 3.

- **Uniques always priced by the best unique on their base.** The Loot tab used the Prices tab lookup, which follows "cheapest unique". With that set, a base like Heavy Belt was priced by its cheapest unique and the Loot tab could hide a valuable one. New `lootLookup` (always 'max') feeds the written rules, the Uniques page and the Alt+F card.
- **Card folds.** `cardCategoryOf` stripped a `<size:N>{...}` wrapper that, for 9 cards, closes on a later line (\r\n data), so they fell to Other. It now strips only the leading wrapper.
- Tests in filter-loot-bughunt.test.ts; useFilterPrices mocks gain `lootLookup`.
