## What

**Tooltips.** Rows under "Not moved" and "Moved so far" now show the item tooltip on hover, the same as the "Would move" rows. Those rows never had `v-item-tip`. That includes the rows #573 made reviewable.

**Move to any tier.** Right-click a "Would move" or "Not moved" row to get "Move to Tier N" for every tier in the group:
- The tier the price picks is marked "· price says" and makes a price move, as before.
- Any other tier is a move by hand (`useTierMove`). That locks the item, so prices leave it there. It then shows under Not moved as "kept by you".
- On a row that is already locked, every tier is a move by hand. A price move under your own move would do nothing.
- Tiers that hide their items are marked "· hides it".
- The tier the price picks is always offered, even when it isn't one of the editor's move targets.

**One tier list.** The tier list moved into a shared `tierMoveEntries` in `tier-move.ts`. The filter editor's "Move to" menu uses it too, and behaves the same as before.
