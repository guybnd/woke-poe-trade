_Requested by **Guy**_

WOKEPOET-154, the launch-day data patch for the 3.30 readiness epic WOKEPOET-149. It streams to players as data version 7, with no release needed. Guy pre-approved shipping launch fixes.

**What `trade-data-patch.mjs apply` added, against the live 3.30 trade lists:**
- **80 new stats.** Mostly "Grants Level # X" and "Socketed Gems are Supported by Level # X" lines without the old "Skill" suffix, plus Area contains the Viridian Wildwood and Delirium Reward Type: Currency.
- **2 new ids** on stats we already know.
- **221 reworded mods**, now read under their 3.30 text. Examples: "Nearby Enemies are Blinded" becomes "Enemies within 3.5 metres are Blinded". "#% increased Rarity of Items found" becomes "+#% to Item Rarity". Scarab atlas lines become Thread lines. "Damage #% faster" becomes "#% Faster".
- **29 new items:** the 10 Threads and Frayed Thread, the Soul Cores of Intellect, Finesse, Might and Instability, Royal Visage, Cursed Poppet, the Immortal, Feral, Royal and Cryptic Astrolabes, the Allflame Embers of the Cloister, the Multitude and the Divine, the Afarud Enshrouding Crystal, Threaded Delirium Orb, Maven's Chisel of Threads, Essence of Trickery, and Shield Crab (beast).

**Script fixes found on the real data:**
- Our matchers carry no sign on rolled numbers ("+15%" is parsed as "#%"). The first run's reworded matchers kept the "+" and never matched, so the patch now drops it. The ref keeps it.
- The exchange list is read before the item list, so new currency keeps its trade tag and art instead of being added name-only.
- A map mod's id is shared with its atlas passive ("... in your Maps"). That wording isn't added to map mods, because it crowded the map mod checker's token search (caught by `map-mods.test.ts`).

**Not in yet:** the Wickerman Viscera and the 11 new uniques aren't in the trade site's lists so far. The hourly upstream job reruns the patch whenever upstream ships data, and I'll rerun it once the trade site adds them.

New `data-330.test.ts` checks that "+15% to Item Rarity" parses to the rarity stat and that the new items resolve.

Checks run locally: eslint, test typecheck, all 3256 unit tests, build. All passed.
