Stacked on #342.

Two Net worth windows were open at once. Guy's live config had two `woke-net-worth` widgets (ids 129 and 130).

**Cause:** turning the stash reader on places a Net worth widget in the live config. An open Settings window works on its own copy of the config, which didn't have that widget yet. So the Net worth settings page showed "Add Net worth", and clicking it created a second widget.

**Fix:**
- In Settings, adding a woke widget that's already placed now adopts the existing one instead of creating another. Woke tools never allow copies.
- Extra Net worth widgets are dropped (the oldest stays) when the reader turns on and once config has loaded at startup, so configs that already hold two are cleaned up.

Tests: turning on with an extra in config keeps one (the oldest), and extras are removed after load.
