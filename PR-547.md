Six of the ten loot filter editor clarity fixes Guy picked (1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10 in the review artifact). Names, conditions in words, Sockets and links by size and example labels follow in a second PR on top of #545.

**Off rows.** A rule that's commented out shows a "Turn on" button with the reason on hover: NeverSink leaves it out at your strictness, or you turned it off. The side panel says the same thing and has its own Turn on button. A tier whose items were all moved out shows no button, since turning it on would write an empty item list.

**Marks key.** A key line above each tier list says what the beam bar, map icon and sound number are. The orange "changed" dot is now an "edited" tag.

**Group list.** Endgame / Campaign / Both fits on one line. Counts say "N tiers", "N groups" and "N edited". Influenced maps get the chapter name "Influenced maps" (it was "Influence and eldritch").

**Switches.** Inside the editor, on/off switches are a track with On or Off in it, so they no longer look like the "?" coins. They keep a keyboard focus ring.

**Side panel place.** "line 646" is now "Rule 82 of 640 · 3 rules above list some of these items and are checked first". The line number moved to the hover.

**History.** Changes tab steps name the items and tiers, for example "Prices moved Exalted Orb Tier 4 → Tier 3" and "Kept Divine Orb where it is". Saved steps are described again from what they changed, so older history reads the same way.
