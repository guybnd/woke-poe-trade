- The map strip gets a **+ Add** tile. Its menu lists the missing numbers with their icons. Picking one adds a tile that takes any minimum (it isn't capped at the map's own value). Clicking that tile takes it back out.
- Tick prefs and the default-disable loop skip `mapExtra`.
