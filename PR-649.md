## What

Price check accounts for socket colours where they can't be changed (Guy's pick B):

- **R / G / B / W tiles** next to Links, each "at least this many", sent as `socket_filters.filters.sockets.{r,g,b,w}`.
- **On by default** for items with "Sockets cannot be modified" (Skin of the Loyal / Lords). Shown off on corrupted 5L/6L. A colour the item lacks waits at 1, dashed.
- **Right-click Links** on any linked item shows the tiles (off); again hides them while all are off.
- Listings' Sockets column shows each listing's colours as dots when the tiles are shown; your row shows your layout.
- poe.ninja's price (any colours) stops counting as a fit while a colour tile is on; colour tiles count as narrowing a rare search.

No extra trade searches: the tiles only change the one search.
