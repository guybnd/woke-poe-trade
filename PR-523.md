Guy found the short rail tags (DLV, EXP…) jarring and asked for a way to expand them to full names.

- A » / « button sits at the foot of the League mechanics rail (sticky while the list scrolls). It switches the tabs between short tags and icon + full name rows.
- The choice is saved in the widget config as `railNames`, which is off when unset, so no migration is needed. While names show, the panel widens from 40rem to 45rem so the guide columns keep their room.
- The button has an `aria-label`, because the Font Awesome glyph would otherwise become its accessible name.
