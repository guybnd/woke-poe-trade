The Net worth window redesign. Guy's picks from the [mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/E5ZMem9viTeQbAFbvQj2Cj): A1 Ledger, B1 + B2, C2, D1, D2, D3, D5, D6.

## Top to bottom
- **Header:** read N min ago, Refresh, and a gear button that opens the tab picker (#345).
- **Hero:**
  - The total in div, with ≈ chaos and **liquid** (currency + fragments) under it (D1), plus the **goal** and its ETA at the current rate (D6).
  - On the right, the chart: a point per refresh. Hover shows its time, total, change and what changed. Big gains are marked, and ranges are 24h / 7d / League (B1).
  - A **Total | By category** toggle switches to the stacked view (B2). The goal draws as a dashed line.
- **KPIs:** since yesterday, per hour today, this session, best item. Below them, today's 3 biggest **price moves on what you hold**, from poe.ninja's 7-day line (D3).
- **What changed | Where it is:**
  - What changed: with a Farming session running, gains are grouped **per map** ("Map 9 · Crimson Temple · 4:12"), with the session total, per hour and **End session** (C2). Otherwise it groups per refresh.
  - Where it is: category bars. Clicking one filters the table; clicking again clears it.
- **Stash tabs** in each tab's own colour, with value, and the change since the read before in their tooltip (D5). Clicking one filters by tab.
- **Chips:** search, All and the categories, and **Hide < N div**, with the threshold in the widget settings (D2).
- **Item table:** Item with art, Qty, Each, Total, Share, 24h, Tab. Sortable by mouse or keyboard. Rows get the game tooltip (with the tabs that hold the item, and poe.ninja's 7-day line) and the right-click item menu.
- **Footer:** tabs read, remove-only skipped, unpriced count, poe.ninja age.

No trade searches anywhere; rares stay unpriced. Everything loads in the opt-in Net worth chunk only. Startup JS is 2201.8 KB and all JS 4755.7 KB.

## Limits from the real data
- Per-refresh chart points and what changed at each are kept **from now on** (a `<league>-ledger` doc, kept small). Earlier ranges show the daily snapshots.
- Net worth has no session of its own. C2 uses the Farming window's running session, and End session ends that window's session.
- Moving an item between tabs isn't a change in the diff, so "moved tab" isn't shown.

## Fixes from the bug review
- Gem, card and currency rows open the right item menu.
- The session total counts every refresh in the session, and its clock moves while you're in the hideout.
- End session ends only the Farming window shown, and only when its module is on.
- A one-point chart no longer draws a wedge, and the ledger doc stays small.
- Hide small needs a divine price, missing 24h values sort last, and the table headers sort from the keyboard.

Also: `WTabs` takes an optional colour and title per tab, and `farming.ts` has a small end-session hook keyed by widget id.
