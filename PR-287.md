_Requested by **Guy**_

The top of the price check took two rows for the name and its chips, plus a tall poe.ninja card that couldn't be collapsed. That card is upstream APT code and never had a collapse; only the verdict row after a search does (#178, #180), so this isn't a regression.

- **One row**: the name, base and chips share a row. Chips wrap only when the window is too narrow, and they wrap under each other beside the title instead of dropping the whole chip block below it.
- **poe.ninja card**: before a search it's now one slim row (price, sparkline, 7-day change). Its chevron folds it into a `poe.ninja 2.5c ▾` chip on the name row, which costs no height. Folded is the default; the choice is kept in `localStorage` (`woke.ninjaCardOpen`).
- The folded chip keeps its space while the price "loads", so nothing beside it moves.
