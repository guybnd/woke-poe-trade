**Checked game fact (poedb 3.30.0 notes):** Atlas Drops replaced Item Quantity on map mods. It raises Maps, Threads and league resources (Splinters, Hiveblood, Lifeforce) plus a few boss drops. It does not raise currency in general, and a map's Atlas Drops also count as Item Rarity. Item Rarity decides how good monster currency drops are.

## Farming goal (G1)
- New `map-mods/goal.ts` adds a goal (`any` / `currency` / `atlas`), stored on the Map mods widget. Any is today's defaults.
- `pseudo/maps.ts` builds the map's number filters on or off from the goal. Currency turns on Rarity, Pack and More Currency. Maps & Threads turns on Pack, More Maps and More Threads. Atlas Drops stays off because the trade site has no listings for it.
- On `MapModStrip.vue`, a Farming switch above the chips flips the current map's chips and sets the goal for the next maps. The goal's numbers get a gold ring, and off chips that don't matter fade. Every chip's hover now says what it raises.
- The Planner's "I want" starts on the goal until you pick one there.
- Ctrl+Space: "farming goal", "atlas drops", "farm currency" and "map goal" show an Atlas card that explains it.

## Planner trade-first (P1)
- Trade search pane first, stash search after. When the stash text is too long, one dim line replaces the red meter and error.

## Div card chance per map (D1)
- `card-drops.ts` adds `mapPool`, `cardChanceIn`, `valuePerCardDrop` and `mapsByChance`. The chance is the card's weight over the map's pool: its own Divcord cards plus cards with no known area that its level reaches. It is marked as an estimate everywhere.
- Drops in chips are sorted likeliest first, each with "1 in N". The map hover and the "Div cards by map" card show each card's chance and "about Xc per card drop". `LookupRow.note` is new: a dim note before the price.
