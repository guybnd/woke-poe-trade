## What

Dragging a big panel, such as the loot filter editor, was laggy because the overlay composites in software:

- The `.w-enter` opening animation held its last frame (`fill: both`), so it stayed a live animation. Every open panel kept its own layer, and everything drawn over it was split into overlap layers, about 50 on the loot filter editor.
- `will-change: translate` during a drag did the same thing.

The fix:

- `.w-enter` now fills `backwards` only.
- Drags no longer set `will-change`.

In a local test, the worst drag frame dropped from 83 ms to 17 ms.
