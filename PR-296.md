_Requested by **Guy**_

The game look Guy picked in WOKEPOET-181 (S3 + T1), as shared tokens and parts (WOKEPOET-182).

- **Hybrid and Native** get the game look: bronze frames with lit corner marks, a dark header with a gold fade line, the title centred between diamonds, bevelled buttons, pressed-in active tabs and segments, recessed fields, square small-caps chips, 2px corners, Fontin text. `theme.ts` sets `data-woke-look="game"`; every look rule is gated on it.
- **Modern and Classic** keep their look (`data-woke-look="plain"`, neutral token values); only small size steps move onto the token scale (buttons and fields 12-12.5 to 13px, segments 12 to 11.5px, Modern's title follows Text size).
- **Readability rules from the pick:** numbers, prices, fields and key caps stay Inter; text under 13px stays Inter; small caps labels never go under 13px; Settings hints are upright. The price check keeps Inter for its tables and verdict.
- **One owner per control:** `ui/controls.css` styles WButton, WChip, WSegmented, WField, WSelect, WToggle, WKbd, APT's `.btn`, and the price check's buttons, chips, segments and roll inputs. OfferRow, PriceDrivers, FilterBtnLogical and WCorruptedFilter join by class.
- **Price check:** `poe-mode.css` colours are tokens, the look follows the theme in both layouts, Settings "Window style" is now "Filter layout" (Tooltip lines / Rows), `.w-poe-line` / `.w-poe-qtag` scoped to `#price-window`.
- **Token clean-up:** `--w-text`, `--w-text-sm`, `--w-head-size` retired; literal radii and most literal font sizes in woke on the scale. README updated; charter P5, P14, P19 updated.

Panel-specific polish (verdict strips, Settings nav, dock cards) comes in WOKEPOET-183.
