## What
The after-map toast in the middle of the screen, redesigned in the PoE 1 look (Guy's pick B from the mocks: carved title bar with map and tier, bronze edge on stone, gold time beside the run bars and numbers, encounters as chips).

- **Click to dismiss:** a click on it closes it. It takes the mouse only after the pointer rests on it for 250 ms, so click-to-move across it still reaches the game. A toast that comes up under a still cursor doesn't arm until the pointer leaves and returns.
- **Resizable:** drag the bottom-right corner; the size is saved as `chipScale` (60% to 200%).
- **Auto-hide:** still goes after the set time, waits while the pointer is on it, and goes when you enter a map.
- **Close key:** shown as a key chip with "Dismiss" beside it; "Click to dismiss" when none is set.
- The "Click closes it" toggle is gone: clicking always closes it now.
