## What

The price check's stat header (armour and weapon items) now shows each big number's range under its roll bar: the lowest this item could be on the left, the max on the right (gold when the item is at it). Chips (aps, crit, block) and the weapon legend give the range in their hover text only. Guy picked option B.

The max is what the roll bar already measured against: the base at its top defence roll plus every local mod at the top of the tier it rolled, at 20% quality (property filter `roll.bounds.max`, or the computed weapon roll for the chaos part).

No max is shown when:
- the number is a unique's fixed line, or nothing on the item can roll it (grey bar);
- the copy has no roll ranges (a plain paste into Ctrl+Space search), since every mod would read as maxed;
- the value is above what the base and mods allow (unknown source or stale base data), rather than a wrong max.

Mockup option B from https://claude.ai/artifact/XLdTTCJWWVZjdpn5pqgDpp . Ticket WOKEPOET-468.
