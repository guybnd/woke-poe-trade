Requested by Guy: step 1 of moving the whole loot filter onto scores (plan picks: A, B4, M1, L1, P1, O1; top tier red on white).

## What
A new **Currency weights** tab in the Loot filter. It shows when the experimental tools are unlocked, and it's off by default and per profile.
- **Items covered:** in maps (AreaLevel >= 68), endgame currency, essences, fossils, oils, delirium orbs, omens, tattoos and ducats show by pile worth, which replaces NeverSink's tiers for them.
- **Pile worth:** poe.ninja chaos × √stack × a full-stack bonus of `min(2.5, 1 + log10(full stack chaos)/2)`.
- **Line and steps:** one line slider from 0.1c to 5c sets where Small starts; anything under it hides. Normal, Big and Beam start at fixed prices: 5c, 30c and 1 divine.
- **Look:** Beam is red text and border on white, with a red beam and a red circle on the minimap.
- **Supplies and pins:** a supply shows from a set stack size, and a pin always shows.
- **No price:** an item with no price gets a guess from NeverSink's tier, marked est. A thin price is held within ×10 of that guess. Chaos Orb counts as 1c.
- **Size:** about 20 rules and about 26 KB on Semi-strict.
- **Stack sizes:** full stack sizes come from game data (`stack-sizes.ts`, RePoE).

## Checks
- Full renderer unit suite passes (6,072 tests).

## Known / open
- In maps these rules replace NeverSink's for these items, so price moves and tier edits for them apply only below area level 68. The info text now says so.
- Quiet, Colour-blind and Fewer beams skip `woke->currency`, the same as rare weights.
- The RePoE stack data may be stale, and the in-game load time is unmeasured.
- The tab has no fake-game spec yet. Its rows use `WItemRow`.
- `FilterEditor.vue` has small edits that may conflict with #978.
