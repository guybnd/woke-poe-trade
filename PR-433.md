Price-checking a 3.30 Viscera (a Ghostly Claw) showed Unknown Item. When the 3.30 data patch ran (#423), the trade site had no Viscera yet. It has them now: 133 bases, searched by slot as Head, Arm, Body or Leg Viscera.

## Changes
- **Data (version 8):** adds the 133 bases, 60 new implicit stats, and 41 implicit ids on stats we already had. These come from `node scripts/trade-data-patch.mjs apply --group viscera`.
- **Data patch script:**
  - Viscera bases now get their category.
  - `--group <id>` patches one trade item group plus the implicit stats it carries.
  - It skips skill grants and socketed supports, which the trade site moved to new `effect_` ids in 3.30.
- **Parser:**
  - Adds `ItemCategory.Viscera`.
  - Reads the slot line (Head, Torso, Upper Limb, Lower Limb) and the Wickerman help line.
  - A Viscera copy has no Rarity line, so it is read as Normal. Without this, its implicit was dropped.
- **Price check:** exact search by base. Relaxed search covers every Viscera of that slot (`viscera.head`, `.body`, `.arm`, `.leg`). A Viscera searches as soon as it opens.
- **Tests:**
  - The fixture `viscera-ghostly-claw.txt` follows Guy's copy from his screenshot.
  - Adds `viscera.test.ts` and a scope test for the patch script.
  - Viscera joins the loot filter matcher's item corpus.

## Checks
- Adversarial review found no confirmed bugs. It confirmed the slot categories against the live trade site: Ghostly Claw returns 44 results under `viscera.arm`, and Primate Foot returns results under `viscera.leg`.
- vitest passes (158 files, 3676 tests), and so do typecheck, lint and build.

## Open
- 30 boss heads aren't on the trade site yet, so they still come up as Unknown Item. Examples: Sirus' Head, Shaper's Head, the Nightmarish ones, and Coven Queen's Crown.
- Two Viscera implicits span two lines. The patch skips those, so they show as unknown mods.
- Not in this PR: 365 of our stat ids are no longer on the live trade list. "Grants Level # X Skill" moved to `effect_` ids. This is reported to the data-patch thread.
