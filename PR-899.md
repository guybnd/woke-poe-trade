## Why
A player's Stacked Decks session showed Loyalty ×1 = 53 div. Their build is v1.330.19 or older (the "cards have no poe.ninja price" wording), which predates the fake-price guard (#858/#860). But that guard only reads poe.ninja's 7-day line, so it also misses a fake that has sat there for days, or one with no earlier price.

## What
- `guard.ts`: `dropRateHeld` — a card whose price × its share of deck draws (Prohibited Library weights) is over 2× the Stacked Deck price counts as 0 (`kind: 'drop-rate'`). Loyalty (1 in ~45 decks) at 10,000c would be ~220c a deck alone; The Doctor passes up to ~950,000c at a 3c deck.
- `prices.ts`: the live table applies it after the line checks.
- `DeckHistory.vue`: saved sessions apply it to their saved price too.
- `price-trust.ts`: `HeldPrice.kind` gains `'drop-rate'`, `heldWhy` shared text.
- Tests in `stacked-decks.test.ts`; release note line.
