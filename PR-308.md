## What
Price check now gives a Quality box to every item that takes quality, even when the item has none (Guy's pick B from the mocks).

- Gems, weapons, armour, flasks, tinctures and maps under 20% quality get a box waiting at **20**. It starts off and dashed, so one click searches for 20%+.
- Items at 20% or more keep their own value, off, so you can push it to 27 on a Twilight Regalia.
- Rings, amulets, belts and quivers are left out because their quality is catalyst quality, which is searched on the mod. Empower, Enlighten and Enhance (Awakened ones included) take no quality, so they're left out too.
- The listing's quality column appears only once a suggested box has been searched with.
