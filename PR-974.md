Type part of a mod ("item rarity", "onslaught") and a new **Uniques with this mod** group lists every unique with a line that has it.

- Rows are priced and sorted dearest first, closest match first. The line that matched is shown with the typed words lit.
- Foulborn mods match under the Foulborn row and its price. Vestigial implicits are tagged Vestigial. Jewel pools (Watcher's Eye and similar) are tagged "Can roll" and sort after.
- Every row has the item tooltip, and Enter price checks it. The details pane lights the matched line.
- Uniques poe.ninja has no price for come after the priced ones (unique colour, no price).
- The group is folded to 3 rows by default, sits under the Prices chip, and can be set in the cog.
- Matching starts at 4 letters, needs at least one word of 3 or more letters, and every query word must start a word. Numbers are ignored. At most 30 rows show.

**Perf:** the index is about 14,900 lines and takes about 45 ms to build once, the first time a query is long enough. It is built from the tooltip files Search already fetches for unique details. Each keystroke adds about 1–2 ms. `mod-index.ts` is its own lazy chunk (1.8 KB), so startup JS doesn't change.
