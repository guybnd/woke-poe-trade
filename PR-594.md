Saved lists parity, step 3 of 4 (WOKEPOET-364): Trade searches move onto the shared saved tree (#587, #590).

## What
- **The list** is `SavedTree`, as Atlas strategies and the Regex library:
  - nested folders (a search's folder "a/b") that fold, with their icons on the folder row;
  - drag reorder of searches and folders; Ctrl / Shift picks; Alt+arrows;
  - F2 rename; Move to folder; new folders; Delete (one or several) with Undo; the filter.
  - Folder menu: new folder inside, rename, change icon (the picker opens under the folder), move, A to Z, fold / open all, delete folder (its searches go to the top level, with Undo).
- **The rail** keeps its views: All, Pinned, Recently opened, Live searches. They now filter the list, showing those searches with their folders. Plus New folder.
- **Sort**: a new "My order"; drag and Alt+arrows switch to it, as in Atlas. The other sorts (Last opened, Name, Times opened, Newest) still put pinned first.
- **Kept**:
  - a click opens the search here; the external-open button; hotkeys; the live search.
  - A folder move or rename keeps PoB builds' Trades tab links and the save box's last folder, and folder icons go with the folder.
- New optional setting: `closedFolders`. Folder records (`folders`) are now keyed by full path and also keep the folder order.

## Behaviour changes
- Folders moved from the rail into the list, so a search is no longer dragged onto a rail folder; it's dragged into a folder row.
- Searches with no folder sit at the top of the list rather than in an "Uncategorized" folder. A saved "folder:X" view opens as All.
