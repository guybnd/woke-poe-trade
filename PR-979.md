## What

A new tool, **Watcher's Eye finder** (module `watchers-eye`, under Builds & trade, and in the Mapper and Trader presets). Guy picked option A1 in the mockup and left two comments, both built here: a type-to-pick aura box, and saved setups.

- **Auras.** The PoB build's aura gems start out added; Vaal auras don't count. Only the auras in use show as chips, and clicking one drops it. The **Add aura…** box filters as you type and lists the build's auras first, marked "In your build".
- **Mods.** It lists every aura mod Watcher's Eye can roll: 87 across 17 auras. They are read from the unique tooltip data, and the list matches poedb's current one. Rows are grouped by aura, with ranges, and each row has a hover card. A picked mod can take a minimum roll.
- **Price check.** Opens the normal price check for a Watcher's Eye with the picks, so it costs one trade search, and only when clicked. All 87 mods read back as ticked filters; a unit test checks each one. This needs the English client.
- **Trade site.** Opens the same search on pathofexile.com without searching from the app. With 3 or more picks it can ask for any N of them.
- **Save.** Stores the auras, picks and minimums under a name; the list next to it loads one back.
- **Ctrl+Space.** Typing an aura name ("grace", or "purity" for all four) gives a card that opens the finder on that aura. Words from a mod ("suppress spell") find the mod, and Enter picks it in the finder. Results go in a new "Watcher's Eye mods" group.
- A NEWS line (`watchers-eye-finder`, since 1.330.24, whole tool), a line in release-next-notes and a README/docs row.

## Review

A separate reviewer found these, and all are fixed:
- Trade ids failed on non-English clients. They are now looked up by the stat's English ref.
- Price check is disabled on non-English clients, because its text is English.
- "Any N" could be wiped while the build was still loading. It is now derived, not stored.
- Vaal auras were counted as the aura.
- Enter in an empty Add aura box picked an aura.
- The NEWS `since` was stale.
