Price check showed "Goreblossom has +3(1-3) to maximum Blooms" as *Not recognized modifier*. The trade site writes that stat "Goreblossom has +# maximum Bloom"; the game writes "+# to maximum Blooms" from 2 up. Every stat our 3.30 trade data patch added knew only the trade site's wording, so the game's plural, reduced/less and 100% wordings were unreadable.

- `scripts/trade-data-patch.mjs`: `gameWordings` reads RePoE's `stat_translations.json` (3.30.0.2.2), finds our stat by trade id and adds the game's other wordings: plural, negated (`negate`), and numberless fixed values (`value`). It runs as `node scripts/trade-data-patch.mjs wordings` and as a best-effort step of `apply` (skipped on `--group` runs).
- 78 wordings added to `stats.ndjson`. Examples: Goreblossom Blooms, "# Prefix Modifiers cannot be changed", "#% reduced Effect of Cruelty", "Melee Critical Strikes Poison the Enemy".
- Reworded lines now keep the old line's negate flag. Hindered and Stun/Block Recovery "within N metres" lines are fixed by hand: a "reduced" roll was read with the wrong sign.
- Data version 22.
