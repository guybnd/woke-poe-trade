Guy's glance bar showed "Run 1 map" after about 5 maps. The run glance's map count is only recomputed by `saveGlance` after a stash read, and quiet reads happen at most every 10 minutes on entering hideout.

## Fix
- `saveGlance` now saves `lastMapAt`, the entry time of the newest map it counted.
- The glance bar adds the maps in Session story's map log that were entered after that time and left after the run's start, the same rule `mapsIn` uses. A map is logged only once the next one starts, so cutting at the save time would always miss one map.
- Old glances without `lastMapAt` fall back to their save time.
- Profit still comes from the last read. Hovering it shows that read's time.
- `useNights()` is now declared before the immediate watch, which had been calling `loadMaps()` before it existed. The review caught this.
