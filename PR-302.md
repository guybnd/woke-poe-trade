_Requested by **Guy**_

Rollout part 3 of the game look (WOKEPOET-183): the small heading labels.

- The 27 one-off uppercase labels now use three new tokens: `--w-caps-face`, `--w-caps-case` and `--w-fs-caps` (plus `--w-caps-weight`). These are the column heads, section labels and stat keys in mapping, campaign, builds, economy, search, the tab menu, mechanics and Settings sections.
- Modern and Classic: unchanged, uppercase Inter at 11px.
- Hybrid and Native: 13px Fontin small caps, at regular weight, because Fontin small caps has no bold.
- After this, the shared parts from #296 already carry the mapping, campaign and lab, and builds panels. Their shots look right in the game look.

## Review
An adversarial review found four problems, all fixed:
- One label stayed at 11.5px.
- Bold small caps were smeared.
- A fixed-height grid head overflowed at 150% text size.
- The run timer's keys switched face in the plain look.

Known and accepted: numbers that sit inside caps labels (e.g. "after 7d") use Fontin digits in the game look.
