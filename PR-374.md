## Problem
On a Q20 catalysed Auxium, the "Elemental Damage with Attack Skills per Power Charge" slider ran 24 to 30. The catalyst scaled the whole tier (20-25 × 1.2), but items on trade with less or no catalyst quality roll as low as 20.

## Fix
`calculatedStatToFilter` now widens the slider bounds to the union of the uncatalysed tier and this item's catalysed tier (`rollWithoutCatalyst`). Pseudo totals keep their source multipliers. Roll value, goodness ("max") and the default min/max are unchanged.

Side effect: Low rolls / High rolls presets on a catalysed row now use the wider range (Low rolls on that belt searches 20+).
