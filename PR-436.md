The campaign card often showed a town step, such as "Hand in The Eternal Nightmare", while Guy was in a zone, such as The Belly of the Beast.

**Cause.** The fault is replayed from Guy's Client.txt of 2026-11-14.
- Towns come up many times on the route. Entering one matched a later town step (a logout, portal or waypoint into town) up to 14 steps ahead.
- So a portal home mid-zone, or doing zones in another order, jumped the route past whole zones.
- In act 4 he did Daresso before Kaom, with Highgate trips in between. The route went Grand Arena → Highgate to step 199, skipping Kaom. Then Kaom's Stronghold → Highgate went to step 216, "Hand in The Eternal Nightmare". From there the Belly and the Harvest were behind the route, so it stuck.

**Fix** (`advanceOnEnter`)
- A town entry no longer moves past a step that enters a zone with monsters.
- After the last such zone, the epilogue's town-only steps still move on, because nothing later could catch up.

Replaying the whole day's log, act 4 now shows the right step in every zone. Acts 1-3 track as before.

**Adversarial review:** it walked the real route with three option sets and found no stalls. It also skipped each area change in turn: a town visit right after a missed zone now holds, and the next zone entry catches up. The one stall it found, the epilogue tail, is fixed and has a test.
