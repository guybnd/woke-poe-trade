Requested by **Guy** after his in-game walk on our own rules only. Part of NeverSink retirement M8 (WOKEPOET-721).

## What was wrong
Several items fell to the skeleton's pink catch-all:
- 6x Alchemy Shard
- 16 and 387 Gold
- Provisioning Wombgift
- the div card Thirst for Knowledge

On the own skeleton, anything no Loot row took had nowhere to go: unpriced currency, cards and gems, stacks under the line, maps with no tier, and rares the weights don't score. The proof only failed pink uniques and maps, so it missed all of these.

There was also a bug for everyone: the name guard (`dropUnknownBases`) removed `BaseType "Gold"`, because the game data has no Gold item. So the Loot tab's Gold rows never reached the filter.

## Fix
- New rest rules in the skeleton, above `unknown`:
  - `mapsother` (Valdo, Nightmare, Shaper guardian and Vaal Temple maps)
  - `rares` (rare gear, in the rare look)
  - `cheapstack` (low shards and essences hide in maps, as NeverSink Very strict did)
  - `gold` (small piles; no minimap icon)
  - `wombgifts`
  - `currency` (currency-like classes)
  - `cards`
  - `gems`
  - `other` (flasks, tinctures, heist, league items and the like)
- `gameNameCheck` knows `Gold`.
- Proof changes:
  - it walks stackables at stack sizes 1, 6 and 40, and gold piles from 1 to 20000
  - it adds every base NeverSink names by class that the game data has no class for
  - it now fails on any pink item, real prices or none
- Enthalpic and Cryonic Ring in pink on black are not the catch-all. That is the rare weights' exotic x2 text (the E1 pick).
