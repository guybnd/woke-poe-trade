## What players get

- **A, first-open tour.** The first time the loot filter window shows our own filter, six cards walk through it, one after another: All loot, Section rulers, Steps, Section choice, Gear, Preview. A gold ring marks what each card is about and the rest of the window is shaded. A card whose target isn't on screen is skipped. Got it, × or reaching the last card retires it for good. It starts only once All loot is on screen.
- **C, How the filter works.** A `?` in the title bar, lit gold until first opened, opens a page over the window with 7 tiles: All loot, Section rulers, Steps, Section choice, Alt+F, Gear, Preview. Each tile has a small picture (the real step labels in their looks, the section chip, the key chips), one line, and Show me. Show me closes the page, switches tab, and scrolls to and flashes the part. Alt+F's Show me opens the Alt+F card on a sample Chaos Orb. The page also has Replay tour. Close, × or Esc closes it.
- **F1, the first Alt+F.** Three cards on the Alt+F card (the Loot card and the rare score card): Rule path, Needle, Ctrl+Z. They come once and then retire for good.
- **What's new:** one line, `loot-filter-tutorial`, whose spot flashes the `?`.

## Code

- `shared/TourCard.vue` and `shared/tour.ts` are the price check tour's card made generic: step skipping, the "n of m" count (now counts only the steps actually shown), placing inside zoomed panels (it was off at 4K), an optional ring, `needFirst` and `replay`. `PriceCheckTour.vue` is now a thin wrapper, and its e2e selectors moved to `data-tour-next` and `data-tour-done`.
- `loot-filter/filter-tour.ts` holds the steps, tiles and hint ids. `FilterHelp.vue` is the page.
- `goto-flash.ts` gains `flashAt(selector)`, which `flashSpot` now uses. `WTabs` buttons carry `data-tab`.
- The rare score card now has Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y and Ctrl+S like the other Alt+F card, so the Ctrl+Z tour card is true there too.

## Wording changed from the mock, to match the code

- All loot: "Open it and drag a handle…", "Click a step's dot…". The ruler has a handle per step and folds by default. There is no single needle.
- Section rulers: "Each page has its own, and a section can too (Own ruler)… Its undo arrow follows All loot again." There is no button called Reset; it's an undo icon.
- Steps: "Decent and up get a beam." Not "Tink gets the beam": Decent, Big and Tink all have beams.
- Row choice is now **Section choice**: "…sets the whole section, whatever the price." The chip is per section. Only the Alt+F card sets a single item.
- Rule path: "…this item or its whole section. Loot is All loot: change it on the Loot tab." The crumb reads Loot › section › item, and Loot is read only.
