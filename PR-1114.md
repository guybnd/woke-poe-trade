## What was wrong
Two items hid on his strict line:
- a fractured rare Searching Eye Jewel (abyss, ilvl 85, unidentified)
- a magic Superior Divine Life Flask at 26% quality

Jewels were judged only by their rarity and item-level rows, so fractured made no difference. The flask over-quality rows covered only the top bases at ilvl 82+.

## Fix
- New Loot rows, on top of each section's other rows, at Decent. A `free` flag shows them at least Small whatever the section's mode or line (Guy: "fractured jewels of any type should have a always on default"):
  - Fractured abyss jewel
  - Fractured jewel
  - Fractured cluster jewel
- New flask row: "Life or mana flask, quality 21%+". It covers any life, mana or hybrid base at any item level, normal or magic, not corrupted or mirrored. It sits at Normal and always shows at least Small.
- Proof:
  - The catalog now includes fractured jewels, fractured clusters and magic 26% flasks and tinctures.
  - New test: a strict line (line 50, jewel sections set to By price) still shows the fractured jewels and the 26% flask. It fails without this change.
