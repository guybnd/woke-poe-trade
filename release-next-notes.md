# Next release notes

- After-map toast: redesigned as a small game window (carved title bar, stone, bronze edge). Click it to close it, drag its corner to resize it (the size is kept), and it waits while your pointer is on it. It shows your close key with Dismiss beside it, and it only takes the mouse once you rest the pointer on it.
- Session story: vaults (Zealot's, Scholar's, Arkhon's, Machinist's) no longer count as maps in the night summary, map toast, times and averages. Change the list in Settings › Farming & sessions.
- Farming sessions, Session analytics, Net worth's map count, the drop counter and death recap no longer count vaults as maps either.
- Ctrl+Space search: a calculator. Type a sum such as 70 * 38, (12 + 8) x 1.5 or 200 + 10% and the answer is the top row; Enter copies it. Currency works too: 50d / 7 or 2 div + 30c use poe.ninja prices.
- Ctrl+Space search: every keystroke picks the top result, so Enter does what the first row says (a sum's answer, the best item match) instead of the web search row you had a moment before.
- Net worth: buying something no longer reads as a loss. A refresh where 10 div or more, mostly currency, left your stash counts as a purchase: it adds 0 to profit per hour, the session total and the session story's profit, and shows as a dashed blue line on the chart. A gain of about the same size within 2 hours (the currency back from a trade that fell through, or the bought item put in your stash) cancels it instead of reading as profit. You can also mark any losing refresh as a purchase yourself, from the change list or by clicking a point on the chart and typing how much you spent, and dismiss a purchase that wasn't one.
- Net worth: the chart has a 3h range, for a closer look at the last few hours.
- Ctrl+Space search: gems the campaign doesn't give now say where they drop. Exceptional gems name their boss or vault (Greater Spell Echo: Uber Atziri), Empower/Enhance/Enlighten say where they drop, and old Awakened supports say they no longer drop and name their Greater replacement.
- After-map toast: maps where you stepped into a Mirage get a Mirage chip, also shown in map history and Ctrl+Space search. A Mirage you skip isn't logged by the game, so it can't show.
- Ctrl+Space search: Templar Vaults. Type "vault" for one card with all four (Zealot's, Scholar's, Arkhon's, Machinist's): each one's quadrant, the bosses and Nightmare maps that spawn it, and every reliquary reward at poe.ninja prices, with an estimated typical pick. A vault's name ("zealot") puts that one first.
- Nightmare map price check: each map shows the vault it can spawn (Fortress and Citadel: Zealot's, Abomination: Arkhon's, Ziggurat: Scholar's, Sanctuary: Machinist's), and the big view has a Vaults tab with each vault's rewards and prices.
- Session analytics › Map history: a Vaults chip lists vault runs too (marked "not counted"); they stay out of the count and average.
- Price check: listings now sort by real value. Chaos and divine prices (and other currencies) are compared at the current poe.ninja rate, so a 150c listing no longer shows after a 1 div one.
- Session analytics: tonight's strip and totals (from Session story) now sit at the top of the window on every tab, and the window opens on Map history first. First step toward one Session window.
- Session analytics › Map history: each Net worth stash read is its own row between your maps, with what came in since the read before, how many maps that covers and the rate per hour. Purchases show as spent, not as a loss.
- Session analytics: click a map on tonight's strip to jump to its row in Map history (the row and the strip segment light up together; clicking a row marks it on the strip). Map history has chips for the mechanics you met (Delirium, Expedition, Zana…) and for maps with a death; click one to list only those.
- Session analytics: a Nights tab lists every night with its own strip, time, maps and profit. Click one to show it at the top of the window, then click its maps to find them in Map history.
- Session story: an optional bar at the top of the screen while you're in a map, with the map, its time so far and tonight's maps and profit. It never takes the mouse and sits alongside the after-map toast. Turn it on under the map settings (dock card › After a map, or Settings › Farming & sessions).
- Ctrl+Space search: "maps" opens Map history, and each map in the card has a Show button that opens Map history at that map's row. The tonight card can open Map history too.
- Session story: tonight's night carries on after the app restarts. Before, a restart rebuilt it from the end of the game's log only, so the map count dropped back to about a dozen maps.
- Session story no longer has a window of its own. Its dock icon is now called Session analytics and works the same way (tonight's card, the after-map line), and clicking it opens Session analytics. Its right-click menu has Map history and All nights.
- Incarnation of Fear, Neglect and Dread fights (normal and Uber) no longer count as maps in Session story, the map toast, Map history, Farming and Net worth's map count. Boss runs now knows their arenas.
- Ctrl+Space search: the tonight and "maps" cards only offer their Session analytics buttons while Session analytics is on, and the maps card says "Your last N maps" instead of claiming the last 2 days.
- Session story's glance bar: drag it anywhere with the overlay open (it shows then even outside a map); its spot is kept. While you play it still never takes the mouse.
- After-map toast: drag it by its title bar to put it anywhere; its spot is kept (Show me with your last map in its settings brings it up to place).
