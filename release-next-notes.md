# Next release notes

- Search (Ctrl+Space) is easier to find and to start. With the overlay open, just start typing and search opens with what you typed. The right-click strip of any item has a More button that opens search on that item (where it drops, recipes, cards that give it, your sales). The empty box suggests a real search each time ("Try: 2 div + 30c"), Things to try lists the newer answers (map cards, Valdo rewards, vaults, sales), and typing "keys" lists every hotkey you have set and every mouse trick. The welcome card for new installs has a Try it button.
- Opening a tool from the rail that has its own key (Map mods, Loot filter, Challenges) says the key under the title for a moment ("Next time: Alt + M on a map checks it"), until you've used it. Clicking into a number box says once that the wheel steps it.
- Price check: Searing Exarch and Eater of Worlds implicits now have a roll bar like other mods. A tier that rolls a range (Fortify 6-7%) shows that range; a tier with one fixed number (an Endurance Charge every 14 seconds) shows where it sits from Lesser to the best tier. Hover the line for every tier, Lesser to Perfect, with yours lit.
- Ctrl+Space pressed in the first second or two after the app starts now opens the search. It used to be lost while the search was still loading.
- Price check's "Related to" list is tighter: it no longer lists the item you checked, and no longer groups things that only go into the same recipe or share a theme (Exalted Orb listed the Eldritch orbs and two Farric beasts; Orb of Fusing, Watcher's Eye, Goldrim and the item quantity uniques had the same kind of list). Families and fragment sets still show your item in its place.
- Tips for keys and clicks you might not know. The price check's mods, Net worth's rows and the Regex library get one short line at the foot until you've used what it shows (Ctrl + Click a mod to search only it, click a dashed number for your own price, give a regex a hotkey). Item tooltips end with "Right-click: wiki, trade, copy, price check" until you first right-click an item. Each tip goes for good once used or closed with ×, shows one at a time, and never in a map. Switch them off, or bring them back, in Settings › Look & icon row › Tips.
- Every window's "?" now lists its mouse tricks too: double-click the title to minimize or reset the size, hold Alt while resizing to stop snapping. The Regex library, Trade searches, Atlas strategies and PoB builds list their row keys (F2 rename, Delete, Ctrl + C, Ctrl / Shift + Click to pick several, Alt + arrows to move). The loot filter's icon card names Alt + F (the rule that shows or hides the item under the mouse) and the wheel over its levels. Dock icons say what a right-click and the wheel do.
- Price check: the poe.ninja price you can click to switch chaos and divines has a dashed underline, like other numbers you can click. The Links tile shows three small socket dots when a right-click adds the socket colour tiles.
- Price check, few listings: the headline price is now the cheapest real instant buyout (bait and stale listings left out, and only when it sits close to what the other listings ask) instead of a blend of listings, poe.ninja and poeprices, and the line under it says "cheapest instant buyout". Your own past checks of the item still show on the hover, but no longer move the price.
- Session analytics: the Sessions and Maps tabs only show if you have sessions from the retired Farming tool. Map history and Play sessions cover the same ground.
- New installs start with the Mapper preset on instead of every tool. Nothing changes for an existing install.
- More fixes: the map scan counts a rating you saved under another wording of a mod (Mirror of Delirium), the run hotkey holds an unidentified unique as its unique when only one unique uses that base and refuses it otherwise (it was priced as a white base), and Undo after setting the map check key in Settings takes the Map mods window back out.
- Bug fixes (about 40). Net worth: an unidentified unique that isn't a rolled one is priced as that unique (when its art matches), so identifying it no longer books its value as profit; a file locked by antivirus or a sync tool no longer wipes past runs, the chart or listings on the next save; listings and sales you left out stay out of every total; unnamed map runs no longer mix with crafting runs; old runs keep the time Stop saved. Sessions: a side area you portal back into counts as map time, Starfall Crater from a map isn't a second fight, map encounter areas aren't new maps, and the glance bar's total stops while AFK. Maps: the stash search for Originator no longer hits Elder/Shaper maps, the scan can call an Elder/Shaper map Safe, the Planner reads T17 "less" wordings, ratings cycle back to unrated, and Mirror of Delirium is matched. Price check: no crash on a unique missing its socket mod, Bound By Destiny counts as rolled, a rare searched with only an added mod gets a price, and New item search finds Foulborn/Vestigial items. Also: missing keys on the Hotkeys page, Ctrl+Space finds sales without apostrophes, Valdo map results can be folded, the icon row stays on screen, finished trades don't come back after a restart, stacked deck luck on saved sessions, PoB "6 link" titles, and small number formats (199.6c reads 1 div, "1 map").
- Essence scan: CORRUPT now means the monolith has a Misery, Envy, Dread or Scorn (or the essences you ticked), as before when prices were missing. The line under it shows the best case after corrupting, not an average: the odds aren't published, and the old average counted four guessed outcomes as equally likely.

- The Mapper preset no longer turns on Drop counter. It's still in All tools if you want it.

Type how many you want in Buy and a line adds up the total and the average each, buying from the cheapest up, and gold marks the prices it takes. Click a price to see its sellers: each has an amount box (starting at what your Buy needs from them) and a Whisper button. Whisper next whispers your Buy's sellers one after another. The currency switch now says Chaos 93 / Divine 64. The duplicate pay/get and Fulfill columns are gone.
