# Next release notes

- After-map toast: redesigned as a small game window (carved title bar, stone, bronze edge). Click it to close it, drag its corner to resize it (the size is kept), and it waits while your pointer is on it. It shows your close key with Dismiss beside it, and it only takes the mouse once you rest the pointer on it.
- Session story: vaults (Zealot's, Scholar's, Arkhon's, Machinist's) no longer count as maps in the night summary, map toast, times and averages. Change the list in Settings › Sessions.
- Farming sessions, Session analytics, Net worth's map count, the drop counter and death recap no longer count vaults as maps either.
- Ctrl+Space search: a calculator. Type a sum such as 70 * 38, (12 + 8) x 1.5 or 200 + 10% and the answer is the top row; Enter copies it. Currency works too: 50d / 7 or 2 div + 30c use poe.ninja prices.
- Ctrl+Space search: every keystroke picks the top result, so Enter does what the first row says (a sum's answer, the best item match) instead of the web search row you had a moment before.
- Net worth: buying something no longer reads as a loss. A refresh where 10 div or more, mostly currency, left your stash counts as a purchase: it adds 0 to profit per hour, the session total and the session story's profit, and shows as a dashed blue line on the chart. A gain of about the same size within 2 hours (the currency back from a trade that fell through, or the bought item put in your stash) cancels it instead of reading as profit. You can also mark any losing refresh as a purchase yourself, from the change list or by clicking a point on the chart and typing how much you spent, and dismiss a purchase that wasn't one.
- Net worth: the chart has a 3h range, for a closer look at the last few hours.
- Ctrl+Space search: gems the campaign doesn't give now say where they drop. Exceptional gems name their boss or vault (Greater Spell Echo: Uber Atziri), Empower/Enhance/Enlighten say where they drop, and old Awakened supports say they no longer drop and name their Greater replacement.
- After-map toast: maps where you stepped into a Mirage get a Mirage chip, also shown in map history and Ctrl+Space search. A Mirage you skip isn't logged by the game, so it can't show.
- Ctrl+Space search: Templar Vaults. Type "vault" for one card with all four (Zealot's, Scholar's, Arkhon's, Machinist's): each one's quadrant, the bosses and Nightmare maps that spawn it, and every reliquary reward at poe.ninja prices, with an estimated typical pick. A vault's name ("zealot") puts that one first.
- Nightmare map price check: each map shows the vault it can spawn (Fortress and Citadel: Zealot's, Abomination: Arkhon's, Ziggurat: Scholar's, Sanctuary: Machinist's), and the big view has a Vaults tab with each vault's rewards and prices.
- Session analytics › Map history: a Vaults chip lists vault runs too (marked "not counted"); they stay out of the count and average.
- Price check: listings now sort by real value. Chaos and divine prices (and other currencies) are compared at the current poe.ninja rate, so a 150c listing no longer shows after a 1 div one.
- Session analytics: tonight's strip and totals (from Session story) now sit at the top of the window on every tab, and the window opens on Map history first. First step toward one Session window.
- Session analytics › Map history: each Net worth stash read is its own row between your maps, with what came in since the read before, how many maps that covers and the rate per hour. Purchases show as spent, not as a loss.
- Session analytics: click a map on tonight's strip to jump to its row in Map history (the row and the strip segment light up together; clicking a row marks it on the strip). Map history has chips for the mechanics you met (Delirium, Expedition, Zana…) and for maps with a death; click one to list only those.
- Session analytics: a Nights tab lists every night with its own strip, time, maps and profit. Click one to show it at the top of the window, then click its maps to find them in Map history.
- Session story: an optional bar at the top of the screen while you're in a map, with the map, its time so far and tonight's maps and profit. It never takes the mouse and sits alongside the after-map toast. Turn it on under the map settings (dock card › After a map, or Settings › Sessions).
- Ctrl+Space search: "maps" opens Map history, and each map in the card has a Show button that opens Map history at that map's row. The tonight card can open Map history too.
- Session story: tonight's night carries on after the app restarts. Before, a restart rebuilt it from the end of the game's log only, so the map count dropped back to about a dozen maps.
- Session story no longer has a window of its own. Its dock icon is now called Session analytics and works the same way (tonight's card, the after-map line), and clicking it opens Session analytics. Its right-click menu has Map history and All nights.
- Incarnation of Fear, Neglect and Dread fights (normal and Uber) no longer count as maps in Session story, the map toast, Map history, Farming and Net worth's map count. Boss runs now knows their arenas.
- Ctrl+Space search: the tonight and "maps" cards only offer their Session analytics buttons while Session analytics is on, and the maps card says "Your last N maps" instead of claiming the last 2 days.
- Session story's glance bar: drag it anywhere with the overlay open (it shows then even outside a map); its spot is kept. While you play it still never takes the mouse.
- After-map toast: drag it by its title bar to put it anywhere; its spot is kept (Show me with your last map in its settings brings it up to place).
- Stacked decks: the big-hit popup can be dragged anywhere with the overlay open, and its spot is kept. Right-click the dock icon › Place the big-hit popup shows a sample to place.
- Session analytics nights use the game's AFK mode, like Net worth: 15 minutes AFK ends the night (chat no longer keeps it going), a shorter AFK is left out of the time played, and the night card shows it. The night card has Net worth's "Leave out hideout" box for its per hour and maps per hour.
- Nightmare map price check: hovering a map's vault shows its rewards as a list, one line each with the item's picture and price.
- Net worth: your sales from pathofexile.com's trade history (stash reader only, using the same sign-in). A Sold section under Details lists your latest sales with a Sold today total and how each price compares with poe.ninja, sales show as gold dots on the chart, and a refresh that holds a sale shows "Sold Headhunter" in place of the item leaving. A Holdings / Sales switch at the top opens a Sales view: totals for today, 7 days, 30 days or the league, a ranked bar per type (uniques, gems, rare gear, currency, fragments & scarabs, div cards, maps; heist blueprints and contracts go under Other) that filters a searchable table of every sale. pathofexile.com sends only your last 100 sales and allows few reads, so the app keeps every sale it has seen and reads at most every 30 minutes on its own (Refresh in the Sales view reads now).
- Net worth: hovering a sold item shows its tooltip with its own mods (gear) or the usual item tooltip (gems, currency, cards). Sales read before this update show the plain tooltip by name.
- Ctrl+Space search: type "sold" for today's and this week's sales, or an item's name to see when and for how much you sold it.
- Price check: a Bulk button above the listings shows only sellers with 2 or more copies at one price. If fewer than 10 show, the click loads more listings (up to 100) right then; opening a check never loads extra.
- Price check: Search bulk, next to Search, does both in one go: it searches with Bulk already on, loading more listings if fewer than 10 bulk sellers show.
- Price check: the hideout and stash icons come first in each row, before the ×count, so they sit in one column.
- Every list that names an item now shows the game's item tooltip on hover, including Wickerman's Viscera, Disenchant bases, Scarab flip, Boss runs entry items, the Drop counter, the Trade log, the build compare, the loot filter's drop preview and item search, the Ctrl+Space launcher's prices and search cards about an item.
- After-map toast: maps where you went into a Starfall Crater (the Black Knight's fallen star) get a Starfall Crater chip, also shown in map history and Ctrl+Space search. The crater stays part of the map's run and time.
- League investments and Farming are retired for now: they're gone from the tool launcher, the icon row, Ctrl+Space, Settings and the presets, and their hotkeys are off. Your saved sessions and settings are kept, so they can come back later. Session analytics' Strategies tab is retired with it (boss profit per hour is in Boss runs, trade totals in Trades); the Settings page is now called Sessions. Net worth's session view now follows tonight's night from Session story (it starts and ends by itself), so it no longer needs a Farming session.
- Settings › Look & icon row: a new UI size makes everything bigger or smaller in one go: panels, the price check, pinned things, menus and tooltips. Map check and the settings window keep following Price check window size. Text size, Panel size, Price check window size and Pinned size now sit under Fine-tune each part. Ctrl+Space: type "bigger" or "smaller" for one step. The price check's palette menu also has a Size for that window.
- Toolbar settings (the cog on the icon rail): now split in two. Toolbar layout and size covers only the toolbar; under a separator, Tools look and size holds the theme and a UI size −/+ that sizes the panels, price check and pins, the same as in Settings.
- Ctrl+Space search: settings are findable. Type "settings" for Open settings and every settings page, or a setting's name (such as account name, panel opacity or map regex) to jump to its page with the row highlighted.
- Flips for 3.30: the Scarabs tab is gone and a Threads tab prices the new 3 for 1 Thread vendor recipe. Nobody has measured its odds yet, so every Thread counts as equally likely, the tab says so, and it stays out of the dock card's best flip; set your own weights in Settings. It also shows what a Frayed Thread opens to on average.
- Flips: the Delirium tab uses the 11 orbs that drop in 3.30 (Threaded keeps the old Skittering odds), so removed orbs no longer skew the average. Catalysts leave out Sinistral and Dextral, which a recipe can't give.
- Ctrl+Space search: type a flippable item ("legion thread", "fine delirium") to see what flipping it makes per item at today's price, or "flips" for the best flip right now. Vendor recipes add the Thread recipe and drop the removed 3 maps for a higher tier one.
- League mechanics: each mechanic's Thread shows with its price again. poe.ninja moved Threads to their own list, so the card had stopped finding them.
- Ctrl+Space search and item menus: Trade site now opens Instant buyout listings, following the price check's setting, instead of anyone online. A plain unique's search leaves out Foulborn copies (a Foulborn one searches Foulborn copies only), and unique maps such as Cortex open a working search.
- Map scan is now part of Map mods: one tool instead of two. Start and stop it from the new Scan tab in the Map mods window, which also shows the tally, rolling mode and every map read with the mods that flagged it, and set its keys under Settings › Map mods. Your Alt+S and Alt+R keys, the on-screen verdict and the dock icon carry over as they were. Ctrl+Space "map scan" finds it in Map mods.
- Ctrl+Space search: the item buttons have icons and are grouped. Trade site comes first and is what Enter does; Market moved to the end. Shortcuts: Ctrl+Enter price check, Ctrl+F find in stash, Ctrl+Shift+C copy the name. Hover a button to see its shortcut.
