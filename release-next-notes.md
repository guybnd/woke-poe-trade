# Next release notes

- After-map toast: redesigned as a small game window (carved title bar, stone, bronze edge). Click it to close it, drag its corner to resize it (the size is kept), and it waits while your pointer is on it. It shows your close key with Dismiss beside it, and it only takes the mouse once you rest the pointer on it.
- Net worth: buying something no longer reads as a loss. A refresh where 10 div or more, mostly currency, left your stash counts as a purchase: it adds 0 to profit per hour, the session total and the session story's profit, and shows as a dashed blue line on the chart. Currency coming back within 6 hours (a trade that fell through) cancels it. You can also mark any losing refresh as a purchase yourself, from the change list or by clicking a point on the chart and typing how much you spent, and dismiss a purchase that wasn't one.
