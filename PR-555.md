## What

The Ctrl+Space search now knows the Bestiary's Blood Altar recipes (Guy picked A + C from the [mocks](https://claude.ai/artifact/QqRVt8DFxGrJYUiynYLUr3)).

- **A, under a beast's price:** a Blood Altar table lists each recipe the beast is in: what it makes, the other named beasts it needs (with prices and a trade search button each), and what the named beasts cost together. Clicking a beast looks it up. Clicking a recipe opens its card.
- **Reverse, under a spirit boss's unique:** Farrul's Fur and the other den uniques show the portal recipe that opens the den (Farric Tiger Alpha + 3 any rare).
- **C, recipes as results:** recipes come up under a Blood Altar heading when you type what they make or a beast's name: "farr", "aspect cat", "unique ring", "6 link", "fracture". A recipe's card lists its beasts with prices, a trade search per beast (row button and Trade buttons), the named beasts' total, and a portal's den drops with prices.
- Search now ignores accents on Latin letters, so "black mor" finds Black Mórrigan (the price list missed it before too).

Data: `public/data/bestiary-data.json` (9 KB, fetched when the palette opens), built by `npm run bestiary-data` from poedb's Bestiary Recipe page and its boss drops. 78 recipes, current patch. Flask recipes need one red beast, and that slot is kept apart from "any rare".
