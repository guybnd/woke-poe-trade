Redesigns the beta Currency weights tab using Guy's picks A, S2, G1 and E1 (mockup https://claude.ai/artifact/FVdpDGiSu7XjwiBboFsqoL).

- **Five steps (S2).** Small (quiet), Normal, Decent, Big (yellow beam) and Tink (the shared top-step look: red on white, red beam, big sound). The defaults are 1c, 3c, 10c, 30c and 90c, and the line under Small hides the rest. Each step is a card with its own "from" input, and the steps stay in order.
- **Categories (A).** Items are grouped into 14 categories that fold open: Orbs, Influence and Eldritch, Catalysts, Atlas and Maven, Expedition, Tainted, Essences, Fossils and resonators, Oils, Delirium orbs, Omens, Tattoos, Ducats and Other. Each category header shows how many items show, a row of step dots, and See more / See less (the line × 2^n, up to ±3). You can search the list and filter it with chips: All, Can show, Always hidden, Edited.
- **Item editor (E1).** Click an item row to open its editor. You can set its step by hand, enter your own price, set a stack it always shows from, or give it its own look (StyleEditor, applied on top of its step's look). "Let the price decide again" clears all of it.
- **Ground pile (G1).** One ground at the top of the tab drops 24 random piles, with cheaper items more likely. Hidden piles are struck through. Clicking a label opens that item in the list.
- **Engine.** Settings now hold `from[4]`, `nudge` and `items`. Own-look items compile first, then the grouped blocks loudest first, then the final Hide.
- **Old saves.** Saves from the first beta migrate: normal and big become the matching step prices, supplies become per-item supply, and pins become supply 1.
- **Release notes.** The currency line in `release-next-notes.md` is updated. There is no NEWS line, since the feature is still beta and experimental, like #997.

## Tests
- Typecheck and lint are clean. The full unit suite passes: 6,079 tests.
- 14 currency weights unit tests, including hand-set steps, own price and own look (the filter validates), category nudge, category coverage and migration.
- New fake-game test in `loot-filter.spec.ts`. It covers the tab, folding a category open, the item editor (Tink, own look, reset), search, and a ground label opening an item. Item rows go through `WItemRow` and ground labels through `v-item-tip`.
- A separate adversarial review found one bug: pins migrated to a forced Small step. That is fixed, and refused numbers no longer stay typed in inputs.
