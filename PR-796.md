## Why
Rain Tempter read 98.5c each (76% of a tab, +2,362%) while it's worth about 4c. The two earlier guards both missed it:
- the one-day rule only fires when poe.ninja has no earlier day (Rain Tempter had a full week);
- the jump rule only fires on thin markets (3+ empty days) and compares with the last read, for 6 hours at most.

## What changed
- New `graphSpike` (valuation.ts): turns poe.ninja's own 7-day line into chaos per day. A price 5× or more over the median of its earlier priced days (and 1c+ over it) counts at that median, marked `spike`. Needs 1+ earlier day for cards/currency/fragments, 3+ for gear (sparse lines).
- It lets go once poe.ninja has had the price 3 days in a row (today and the 2 before within half of it), or when the price falls back.
- Stateless: works with no last read and even when an earlier read already stored the fake as the solid price. Runs for every category, thin or not.
- Shown, not silent: the pause icon, a hover naming poe.ninja's number and why, and the 24h change shows — while held.
