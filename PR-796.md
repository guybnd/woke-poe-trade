## Why
Rain Tempter read 98.5c each (76% of a tab, +2,362%) while it's worth about 4c. The two earlier guards both missed it:
- the one-day rule only fires when poe.ninja has no earlier day (Rain Tempter had a full week);
- the jump rule only fires on thin markets (3+ empty days) and compares with the last read, for 6 hours at most.

## What changed
- New `graphStep` / `graphSpike` (valuation.ts): turns poe.ninja's own 7-day line into chaos per day. A **step** of 5x or more over the last priced day before it (and 5x / 1c+ over the median of the days before the step) counts at that median, marked `spike`. Needs 2+ earlier priced days (3+ for gear).
- A steady climb (under 5x a day) is never held, so league-start ramps stay correct.
- Released once poe.ninja has had the new price on 3 priced days; gear released after 6 hours, like the jump rule. A confirmed step never starts a thin-market jump hold afterwards.
- Stateless: works with no last read and even when an earlier read already stored the fake as the solid price. Runs for every category, thin or not. Ignored items are never held.
- Shown, not silent: the pause icon, a hover naming poe.ninja's number and why, and the 24h change shows — while held.
- Tests: `renderer/test/woke/net-worth-spike.test.ts` with the Rain Tempter line, climbs, gear and release cases.
