Guy picked B (steps table), S (slider) and W1 (replace the card) on the item card mock.

When Currency weights decides an item, Alt+F now shows `CurrencyCard.vue` in place of the quick keys, tier ladder and look editor:
- **Category chip.** Shows the item's category in its colour. Clicking it opens the Currency weights tab.
- **Show.** Off / Weighted / Always. Off sets step 0, Always sets supply 1, and Weighted clears both.
- **Worth ×.** A per-item multiplier from ×0.25 to ×4 in 0.05 steps, applied when the slider is released. It is saved as `ItemEdit.mult` and applied in `currencyItems` and in a new `withEdit`, which the tab uses too.
- **Steps table.** Each step's label, the stack it starts at and its price, with the item's current stack marked and a one-line summary.
- **Fold for the rest.** Own price, supply stack and own look, via the existing `CurrencyItemEditor`. The Rule and Also-listed folds stay.

The card is lazy-loaded (`defineAsyncComponent`), so index JS stays under budget: 1678.40 kB vs 1678.51 kB before.
