## What was wrong
Valdo maps parsed fine (area, reward, numbers), but the map mods strip treated their fixed "Unique Modifier" lines as mods it couldn't read ("Couldn't read every mod", "6 mods I couldn't read"), offered no Void or reward control, and showed a pointless Quality 20 box.

## What changed
- New Valdo strip (`renderer/src/web/woke/valdo/ValdoStrip.vue`) on the price check for Valdo maps:
  - **Void**: Any / Void / No void at the top, defaulting to the map's own (one stat filter, `valdoVoid`, made in `pseudo/maps.ts`).
  - **Reward**: this map's foil reward by default; click to pick another unique (no replicas) or Any reward; "This map's" goes back.
  - The map's mods as a folded list, Void first.
- Map mods strip no longer shows for Valdo maps; the empty stats block under the strip is hidden.
- `mapCompletionReward.disabled` leaves the reward out of the trade query.
- No suggested quality box on Valdo maps.
- Header reward chip hides on Any reward.

Ctrl+Space search: nothing new to add. This change adds search controls, not new information.
