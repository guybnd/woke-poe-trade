_Requested by **Guy** · [project thread](https://claude.ai/code/project/chan_01TFAVQEVFL8KKQ8fhVfpfJo?thread=cmsg_01TFAVQEVFL8KKQ8fhVfpfJoXmX2ctxMAqEb58UonVgqyd)_

NeverSink 8.21 (3.30) removed Scarabs and Vials, moved Allflames into a new "Allflames and Map Enchantments" list, and added a Tiered Gear matrix (`t5r1` = item tier 5, rank 1, the best). This brings the loot filter editor in line. WOKEPOET-266.

- **Quiet mode and fewer beams** read the tiered gear matrix by rank. Before, `t5r1` read as "tier 5" and lost its sound and beam, the opposite of what NeverSink means.
- **Price tiers**: Scarabs group removed; Allflames and map enchantments re-tiered from prices and covered by the safety net (embers stay in the fragments net for pre-8.21 filters).
- **Rule list**: "Scarabs and fragments" is "Fragments and map items"; Viscera, Allflames, special map mods and item tiers get categories and names.
- **Fixtures** are NeverSink 8.21.0a at every strictness plus the Vaal style (8.21 styles ship with LF; the parser already keeps a file's own line ends).

Every preset was compared rule by rule on 8.20.2 and 8.21. Apart from the tiered gear fix, the only difference is gear-in-maps leaving `t3_84` alone, because 8.21 put rings in it and the preset never narrows jewellery.

Open: the matcher can't judge `UnidentifiedItemTier`, `Fated` or the Viscera class until a real 3.30 copy shows how the game writes them (WOKEPOET-153). Viscera have no poe.ninja category yet, so they aren't price-tiered.
