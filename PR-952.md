Follow-up to #950, from Guy's report that Market tracker › Prices rows (Forbidden jewels and others) had no item tooltip, and his ask for a corpse quick card.

**Prices tab tooltips**
- New `ui/tooltip/ninja-row-tip.ts` (loaded lazily) turns a poe.ninja row named after its price key into the item it stands for:
  - Forbidden jewels: Flame or Flesh with only that notable and its class.
  - Cluster jewels: size, enchant and item level.
  - Maps and blighted maps: their tier.
  - Temple rooms: the Chronicle.
  - Valdo maps: their Foil reward.
  - Filled Coffins: their mod.
  - Shrine belts and imbued gems are covered too.
- `ninja-row-tip.test.ts` checks every Prices category against sample rows. The rows from the deleted League investments dump now live in `test/helpers/ninja-rows.sample.json`.

**Corpses**
- Corpses (Raise Spectre corpses from Ritual) open the quick poe.ninja card, like currency. Item level isn't shown, and the trade search starts with the item level filter off, because it doesn't change a corpse's price. That filter change is a one-line, marked change in `create-item-filters.ts`.
- The tooltip and quick card show "Raise Spectre corpse", the monster type, the spectre's abilities, and "Mirage areas only" where it applies. The data comes from poewiki's Corpse item table (102 entries) and is in `public/data/tips/corpses.json`. It loads only on a corpse, and the data version goes to 35 so installed apps fetch it.
- Corpse tooltips also show "You have N".
- What's new: `price-check-corpse-card`, with a Show me sample.

Invitations, fixed-roll uniques and unique maps stay on the full price check, as Guy picked.
