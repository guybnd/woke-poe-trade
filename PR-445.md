_Requested by **Guy** · [project thread](https://claude.ai/code/project/chan_01TFAVQEVFL8KKQ8fhVfpfJo?thread=cmsg_01TFAVQEVFL8KKQ8fhVfpfJo8BeQs26UY2WUBCPe8hRwXS)_

Guy: "why cant i get any of the stats to filter when i price check from a searched item". A unique priced from Ctrl+Space search or the right-click item menu had no game text, so it had no stat rows.

**What changes**
- The unique's mods come from the tooltip data (`tips/uniques.json`). They're written as a Ctrl+Alt+C copy with each roll at the bottom of its range (`75(75-100)% increased Physical Damage`), and the real item parser reads that text. Rows get the right ranges and trade ids, the same as a copied unique.
- Every row starts unticked (`ParsedItem.rollsFromData`), so the first search is still the name alone. The lowest roll of a "lower is better" stat would otherwise count as a perfect roll and get auto-ticked.
- The variant comes from the price row when it's known. Otherwise only the mods every variant has are used.
- These are left out:
  - another base's mods (Grand Spectrum, Doryani's Delusion)
  - choices the data lists in full (1 or 2 abyssal sockets, increased or reduced)
  - jewel seeds
  - negative ranges
  - mod pools
  - lines the parser can't read
- English only, since the data is English. The loot filter verdict chip stays hidden for these items, as before.
- 1087 uniques get rows.

**Checks**
- New unit test `unique-mods-by-name.test.ts`: every unique is unticked and no range spans zero, plus the review's cases.
- New e2e `price-check-unique-by-name.spec.ts`: search, then Price check, tick phys %, Search, and the stat goes out with min 75.
- Lint, vue-tsc, the test typecheck, vitest (3720) and the build all pass.
- 67 e2e specs: 2 loot-filter specs failed once under load and passed on rerun.
- JS budgets: startup 1470 KB of 1640, all 5036 KB of 5120.
