Guy's follow-up picks after #974 (mockup ideas 1, 2 and 4):

- **A number is the least roll in mod search.** "item rarity 50" keeps only uniques whose matched line can roll 50 or more. A line's roll is the top of its largest range ("Adds 1 to (650-850)" counts as 850). If it has no range, it's the line's first number.
- **Passives by what they do.** "onslaught" or "life leech" lists the keystones, notables, ascendancy and cluster jewel notables whose stats match. They come after any passive named like the query, up to 8, with the matched stat shown under the name. The word matching (every query word starts a word of the line, numbers ignored, 4+ letters) moved to `mod-words.ts` and is shared with the mod search.
- **Div cards by what they give.** This already worked for priced rewards. Now a card also comes up when its reward is a real item that's corrupted or rolled: "fertile mind" lists A Mother's Parting Gift, and "headhunter" also lists The Demon and The Fiend.

What's new: new line `search-tree-by-stat` (Show me searches "onslaught"). The `search-unique-mods` note now mentions the number. Both lines are also in the release notes.

Tests:
- Unit tests cover numbers and rolls, tree stat hits, and the corrupted card reward. The full renderer suite passes (6,001 tests).
- Fake-game: `search.spec.ts` adds "onslaught 100" (Watcher's Eye's "4 seconds" drops out) and Dance of Blades under Passive tree. 81 search-related fake-game tests pass locally.
- In `search-groups.spec.ts`, a check for "no +N more row" matched the word "more" in a passive's stat line. It is now anchored to `+N more`.
- Type check and lint pass.
- A separate adversarial review found one bug, now fixed: a line's roll used to be its first number, so "Adds 1 to (650-850)" counted as 1. It also asked to accept "50%." with trailing punctuation, which is done.

Perf: the tree stat scan costs about 2 ms per keystroke over the 2,389 stat lines, plus about 25 ms once to build its word cache. Startup JS is unchanged.
