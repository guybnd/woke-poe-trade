This is the rest of Guy's tooltip audit picks (G2, G5, I1 to I9); PR 915 had G1 and G3. Everything here is added to tooltips that already exist and loads on first hover. Nothing loads at startup.

## What changes
- **Mod rows in the price check (G5, I1).** Hovering a tiered mod shows:
  - every tier on that item class, with the item level each one needs;
  - the row's own tier lit, and "Item level 84: T2 is the best it can roll (T1 needs 86)";
  - the bench craft for the same line, with its cost and where it unlocks.
  - This works on picked rows and on PoE mode's unpicked lines.
  - Show me opens a sample check.
- **Uniques (I2, I3, G2).** A unique's tooltip adds:
  - the cards that turn into it, with the full set's cost and how much cheaper or dearer the set is. No % is shown for rolled or corrupted rewards.
  - its Vestigial implicit;
  - on a Foulborn copy, the line its mutated mod replaces;
  - on Timeless and Abyss conquest jewels, each conqueror's keystone.
- **Bases (I7).** A gear base shows the item level its best tiers need, for the mods that matter. It also lists the uniques it can be, with prices. Uniques that never drop at random are marked, with "a Chance Orb can't make them" (Guy's note on I7).
- **Maps (I8).** Any map name shows its tier and its cards, dearest first.
- **Divination cards (I4).** A card shows its set cost against the reward's price, and the three maps where it is likeliest.
- **Gems (I5).** A gem shows each poe.ninja version's price, plus one line on where to get it.
- **"You have N" (I9).** Currency, fragments and cards show the count from Net worth's last stash read, and its value.
- **Essences (I6).** An essence shows the mod it forces on each slot. This comes from a new shared data file, `tips/essences.json`, built by `npm run essence-data` from Path of Building's data. Item data goes to version 34.
- What's new has one line per feature, and release-next-notes.md is updated.
