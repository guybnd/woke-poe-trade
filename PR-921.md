## Sessions
- **Character:** after the game starts (log opening or login line), the first death or level-up decides which character is yours. Other names after that are party members, ignored in maps and in the hideout. The night tracker keeps its own decision across restarts (my-char.ts).
- **Hideout + Other:** the session card and Ctrl+Space split real hideout time from other time out of maps. A night saved before hubMs existed shows one "Out of maps" line.
- **Map-less sessions:** an evening of endgame play with no maps saves a session. That means boss arenas, or areas of level 68 and up that aren't hub, town, campaign or Labyrinth areas.
- **Analytics:** a session saved without map detail counts as its N maps (profit/N each) in typical map, best map and losing share.
