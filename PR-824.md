Bug hunt over the features merged in the last few days. Five area reviewers found the bugs, and each one was confirmed (most with a repro test) before it was fixed. A separate adversarial review of the fixes found 2 more, which are fixed here too.

## Price check
- Bulk checks fired a second exchange search on their own to compare chaos with divine offers (#774). That broke the no-search-without-a-click rule. Now divine leads when chaos was held back, with a note saying so, and clicking Chaos loads it.
- A bulk search still waiting in the rate-limit queue was sent after you'd moved on to another item. Now it's dropped.
- Enter on a focused button in the small poe.ninja card opened Live offers, which runs a search.
- The age colour at exactly 1h and 1d didn't match the "1h" and "1d" labels.
- A Divine Orb bulk check could end up with neither side loaded.

## Net worth
- A gear spike priced from poe.ninja's line alone flipped between held and spiked every 6h (20 → 500 → 20). Review fix: a later, 5× bigger spike is still held.
- Sales and Faustus listings were priced without links, so 6L items got the unlinked price.
- Magic maps never got the saved-price spike check: history was requested by the affixed typeLine.
- After a league switch, the old league's sales stayed if the new read failed.
- The Best chip and the chart's "Sold:" names had no item tooltip.

## Strategy runs
- A rare or magic map held with Alt+H and then listed or sold counted twice. Review fix: the match also checks the map tier.
- With more than 100 Faustus listings, sales of older listings counted as run profit.
- After a restart, the dock card, Ctrl+Space and the glance bar had different profit and Faustus figures from the Runs view.
- The Runs view's per hour was divided by the time to now; the card divides by the time to the last read.

## Atlas, Map history, Ctrl+Space, What's new
- A tree link followed by `.`, `,` or `;` in pasted guide text failed to import.
- In a hypostasis link, an empty pick (`3:`) picked mastery type 0.
- Unique maps in Map history got a tooltip for a non-existent "<name> Map".
- The "Listed …" and "Sold …" results in Ctrl+Space, and the rows inside "Sold" cards, had no item tooltip.
- Show me on a `menu::skip` tool opened All modules instead of the tool's own Settings page.
- Reopening search with the same text kept previously expanded groups.
- Hovering a tool in All tools cleared all of its What's new lines but showed only one. Guy picked: clear only the line shown.
