New module **Session story** (Mapping, on with Farming): a card of the night, read from Client.txt. Guy picked option B (night strip) from the mocks.

- Profit and best drop come from the stash reader's gains and ledger diffs (Net worth) when it read during the night; without it the card says so.
- A night ends after 15 quiet minutes or a new `LOG FILE OPENING` section. The game logs no logout line (checked in Guy's real Client.txt).
- Dock icon with the night so far, a peek when a night finishes, a panel listing the last 20 nights (saved in woke-docs/session-story/nights.json), and Ctrl+Space "tonight".
- Map instances count once (portal re-entries and side areas fold in), as Farming counts them.
