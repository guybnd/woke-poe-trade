The always-on campaign pill now shows the current step's area hint ("Usually ↗", "Loop around counter-clockwise") on a second, smaller line under the step. That's Guy's pick B from the [mocks](https://claude.ai/artifact/MQcQW44AsoHTUT2iZpHMUM).

**Change**
- **New pin "Area hint for the current step".** It can be switched off in the campaign settings. The migration adds it once, after "Current step", for anyone who already pins the step; turning it off stays off.
- **`areaHint(step)`** picks the first sub line that's a where-to-go hint. It skips vendor, buy, chat-command, "Put currency" and "Farm Level" lines.
- **Dock:** a readout can be `below`. The pill then grows downward on a second line while the icon, ring and badge stay put. The horizontal dock aligns icons to the top, so the other icons don't move. A below readout with nothing above it stays on one line.
