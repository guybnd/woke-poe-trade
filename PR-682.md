This PR reworks the Net worth window layout under Details. Guy tried each round on the dev build (WOKEPOET-414).

- **Summary row:** total and today, plus liquid, best item and session while Details is open.
- **Two halves:**
  - The left half is the chart. It fills its half, and its height can still be dragged when it is alone.
  - The right half is the per hour box:
    - a 1h/2h/3h/Today picker and a themed "Leave out hideout" checkbox;
    - one row with Per hour, Maps, Median map and Played;
    - a time bar with a hover per colour, and a one-line legend (Maps, Other, Hideout, Off).
- **Lighter controls:** small text tabs with a gold underline (new `TextTabs.vue`) replace the big segmented buttons.
- **Columns below:** Since last refresh sits beside Where it is plus price moves. Price moves show item icons, and each category shows its top item's icon. Text sizes match the rest of the panel.

**Review:** a separate reviewer went over each round. Its fixes covered a class clash with the tab strip, a lint failure, a drag handle placed off the chart, a dim legend, and the name column overflowing.
