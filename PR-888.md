## What changes

**Net worth › Sales and Listed**
- Hover a row for ⊘, or right-click it for the same menu with the item header. Delete works on a focused row.
- A row you leave out stays in place, struck, with a "left out" tag. It no longer counts in:
  - the head total
  - the type bars (Sales)
  - the stale chip (Listed)
- A **Changed N** chip lists the rows you left out.
- Left-out ids are saved per league in a new store, `net-worth/line-out-store.ts` (doc `line-out:<league>`).
- New `saleMenuRef` gives the menu its item header: uniques by name, rares by their base with no price.
- Not in this PR: the dock icon's stale count (the pending glance) still counts left-out listings.

**Farming**
- Loot rows are now `LootRow.vue`. The always-on − and + buttons are gone.
  - Click the count or the value to change it.
  - ⊘ leaves the line out.
  - ⋯ gives your price, Back to poe.ninja's price, Change how many and Remove, with Undo toasts tied to the session.
- `LootLine` gains `out` and `each`; `lootUnit` uses them in the totals and the last map's result.
- `toSessionRecord` saves your price (with poe.ninja's kept as `read`) and `out` to Session analytics, per map too.
