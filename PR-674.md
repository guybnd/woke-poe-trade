Instant buyout (merchant tab) rows in the price check get a door icon that sends you to the seller's hideout, like the trade site's own Travel to Hideout button. Stash listings keep the whisper. WOKEPOET-413; mocks: option A.

**How it works**
- A signed-in `/api/trade/fetch` gives instant listings `listing.hideout_token`, a JWT that expires about 5 minutes after the fetch. Signed out there is no token, so no door.
- The door posts `{ token }` to `/api/trade/whisper` through the local proxy. The proxy adds the site's `Origin`/`Referer` for that one path on official hosts.
- The age cell shows the outcome: Travelling…, In demand: click again (the next click sends `continue: true`, as the site does), Too old: search again (expired token, nothing sent), or the site's own error.
- State is kept per token, so a new search of the same listing starts clean. After the site accepts, further clicks send nothing.
