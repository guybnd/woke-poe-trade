## What
Alt+F on a magic Crimson Jewel showed the unique Crimson Jewel card (its uniques, average price). The filter rule that caught it was right (Loot › Jewels, "Magic or white jewel"); the card looked up the wrong Loot row.

## Why it happened
The jewel rows (and special gear, league rows) cover several bases but their `bt` names only the first one (Cobalt Jewel). `itemFor` matched rows by `bt` only, so for Crimson/Viridian (and abyss bases past the first) no row fit, and its fallback picked the plain row named "Crimson Jewel": the unique base row. `rowFor` would also have picked it (no conditions, dearest).

## Fix
- `itemFor` also matches a row whose `bts` list the base (rule conditions still have to match exactly).
- `notItsRarity`: a `uniques` row is never picked for a non-unique item, in the card and in Loot place (Open on the Loot tab).
- Test: every jewel row on every base it lists finds itself, never the unique row.

Checked against Guy's live filter: magic/rare/white plain and abyss jewels, with and without corrupted implicits, all land on their own jewel row.
