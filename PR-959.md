## What was wrong
Guy's "sanc" run showed Profit +7.4 div; the real number is +27.4. The Runs view passed `outsideTotals()`'s full totals straight to `runTotals()`. Both shapes have a `leftOutChaos` field, but in `runTotals` it meant "left-out sales already collected (off profit)", while in `OutsideTotals` it is *everything* left out. So the 20 div Behemoth Tread listing Guy left out came off Profit, Per map and Per hour, and the top "Left out" figure disappeared. Stop, the dock glance and saved `final` totals went through `outsideSums()` and were right.

Checked against Guy's stored data: stash change +48.3, minus 16 div of maps listed before the run that sold during it, minus 4.9 from his 17-maps edit = +27.4.

## Fix
- `Outside` uses `OutsideTotals`' own field names (`leftOutCollectedChaos`, `leftOutChaos`), so either shape means the same thing.
- RunsView passes `outsideSums(outside)`.
- Regression test: a left-out unsold listing takes nothing off profit and shows as left out (fails on master).
