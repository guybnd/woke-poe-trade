## What

Price checking a captured beast now lists the Blood Altar recipes it's used in (option A of the [mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/NFLczRhQWZFcBkPGkLrws9), WOKEPOET-485).

- **BeastAltar.vue**: a "Blood Altar" panel under the instant poe.ninja card and beside the full window, the same column as the related items. Each recipe shows what it makes, the other beasts it needs (with poe.ninja prices and item tooltips) and the total; a click on another beast price checks it. Loaded async, so it adds nothing to startup JS.
- **3.30 data fix**: 3.30 took yellow beasts out of every recipe ("they are no longer a requirement for any Beastcrafting Recipes"), but poedb still draws the old "any rare creature" slots and the app showed "+ 3 any rare". The build script drops them (the `a` field is gone); flask recipes keep their one red beast slot. Ctrl+Space's recipe table and cards follow.
- Adds the Black Mórrigan "Create a Unique: Talisman" recipe (3.29 patch notes, wiki table), which poedb's table lacks.
- `search/lookups/bestiary-load.ts`: one shared fetch of `bestiary-data.json` for the search and the price check.

Data checked against the wiki's 3.30 recipe table: every named-beast combination matches. Craicic Vassal alone corrupts a map to have an implicit modifier ("Gruffmauler the Rumbling" in Guy's example is just the rare name on a Vassal).
