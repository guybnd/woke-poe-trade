Part of WOKEPOET-385, about the top e2e flakes.

`tab-rail.spec.ts:280` ("a pinned icon drags to a new place, and a plain click still opens it") was flaky in 4 of 69 runs and failed outright in 1. Each time the moved icon never got its `open` class after the click.

After an icon drop, `TabBar.vue` swallowed any click for 50 ms, to stop the drop's own click from opening the tool. A real click that soon was eaten as well. A test can click that fast, and so can a person, rarely. A new `mousedown` now ends the swallowing. The drop's own click never has a press of its own, so that click is still swallowed.
