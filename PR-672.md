## What
- Local vitest runs (renderer and testing) are capped at 4 forks, and local Playwright at 2 workers. CI keeps its own settings (`CI` set). `WOKE_TEST_WORKERS` changes the local cap.
- New root `CLAUDE.md` tells every thread to run checks through `scripts\woke-check.cmd` (cloud while PoE is open), to keep full suites, builds and Playwright off the PC during play, and to stop the servers and monitors it starts.

## Why
Measured on Guy's PC while he played: one `vitest run` of a single file forked 23 workers, Playwright defaults to half the cores, and 16 orphaned `tail -F | grep` monitors plus a 4-day-old vite server were still running. woke-check already existed, but its rule only lived in DEVELOPING.md, which threads don't load.

## Checks
CI only, because PoE was running. CI sets `CI`, so the cap doesn't apply there. The cap itself was not measured locally.
