Part 3 of the security audit fixes (item A).

- Electron 40.10.6 → **43.8.7**. 40 is out of support and `npm audit` listed 14 advisories (all fixed after 42.11.15), including `<webview>` enabling Node in Web Workers, which matters here because lab, Wealthy Exile, the trade site and the challenge guide are webviews.

- `npm audit` in main: only a moderate dev-only `minimatch` left.

**Why 43 and not 45:** from Electron 44, `clipboard.readText`/`writeText` return promises and `readImage`/`writeImage`/`availableFormats` are gone. That breaks `HostClipboard.ts`, the price check's clipboard polling from Awakened PoE Trade (upstream is also still on 40). 43 is a supported line, so it gets security fixes without touching price check. Moving to 44+ is worth doing together with upstream.
