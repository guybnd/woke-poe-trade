WOKEPOET-89: Guy picked B, with the note "Show run time and town time separate and show total of both".

- **Auto-pause:** after 20 minutes with no game activity in Client.txt (the game closed or left alone), the run pauses as of its last activity, so the idle time never counts. The next zone resumes it, and the card says "paused: no game activity". A gap the app slept through, like a night with the app closed, is dropped the same way when the next line arrives. The mock said 10 minutes; I used 20 because a long campaign zone can go 10 minutes without a log line, and 10 broke an existing split test.
- **Town time:** the run tracks time in towns and hideouts (`isTownLike`). The card shows Run (the total), Out of town and Town & hideout. The HUD strip adds a Town figure. Splits and PBs still use the total, as before.
- A manual pause stays manual: only an idle pause resumes on its own.
