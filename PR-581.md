One-off features audit, finding 6 (WOKEPOET-360).

## What
- **`shared/on-demand.ts`**:
  - `inLane(work)`: one trade search at a time across Boss runs / Search quotes, gem versions and Harvest swap, so clicks and background quotes never fire together. A search that doesn't answer in 2 minutes gives up its place.
  - `askOnce(key, work)`: each answer is kept by its request for 15 minutes and shared while running, so the same search again (the same gem copied again, a swap mode tried twice) costs nothing.
  - `useOnDemandQuote()`: one loading / done / error state for rows that search on a click; a kept answer shows without a click.
- Gem versions use `useOnDemandQuote` (request = the version's filters and trade settings). Harvest swap's Check and refine go through `askOnce` (request = league + its trade body). `trade-quotes` runs each lookup in the lane.
- Fix: `trade-quotes` `request()` never cleared names asked for in another league (the check ran after the league was overwritten). Now it does.

## Behaviour changes
- The same gem copied again shows the versions searched in the last 15 minutes at once. Before, the rows started empty and a click was answered by APT's 5-minute request cache.
- Two clicked searches run one after the other instead of side by side.

## Left as is
- Price drivers (multi-search batches with their own retry and budget UI), the new-item search (runs on its own, shows raw listings) and the Claude link's trade search (its own 10 s gap, answers to Claude) don't fit a single quote and keep their paths.

## Tests

- Then it expects the answer at once with no new search; `test/woke/on-demand.test.ts` covers the lane order and failure, keeping and sharing answers, row states and retry; `boss-trade-prices.test.ts` adds the league switch case.
