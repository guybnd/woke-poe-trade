## What was wrong
Net worth rows showed no item tooltip, or one half hidden behind the window. Bringing a panel to the front set its `wmZorder` to the top number plus one, and nothing ever brought the numbers down. In Guy's live config Net worth was at z-index 1004 (checked over CDP), above the item tooltip (1000), menus and toasts (1000–1002) and the dock (900). Not caused by a recent merge; the window just crossed 1000 with use.

## Fix
- `overlay/zorder.ts`: once any panel passes 800, renumber all numbered panels 1, 2, 3… keeping their order and ties. Runs after each bring-to-front and in `upgradeConfig`, so an existing config is fixed on load.

## Why the gate missed it
`tip-audit.ts` checks that item names sit inside a `data-item-tip` element. It never checked the tooltip is painted on top, and test configs start with low z-orders. Now the market hover test asserts the tooltip is topmost at its centre and corners, and a new test boots a panel at 1004.
