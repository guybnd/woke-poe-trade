_Requested by **Guy**_

The bookmark next to Trade now opens a small box: a **name**, prefilled with what you'd call the item, and a **folder** (the existing category), prefilled with the last folder saved to. Enter saves; saving a link that's already saved renames or moves it.

Proposed names: a unique's name (+ `6L`), a gem's level/quality, a map's tier, otherwise the base plus the first two ticked stats (`Two-Toned Boots: Fire Resistance, Movement Speed`). Property filters (Total DPS, Armour) are skipped.

Before, a link from a finished search held only an id, so most saves were named "Saved search".

Checks run locally: `vue-tsc --noEmit`, eslint, full vitest (2866 pass), new `test/woke/save-search-name.test.ts`.

Known limit: Esc is eaten by main's `before-input-event` (closes the whole price check, as it already did); Cancel or clicking outside closes just the box.

Folders/organisation of the Trade searches window is a separate change, waiting on a design pick.
