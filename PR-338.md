## What changes
- **Pins** (`woke/pins/`): pinned things stay on screen while you play. You drag a pin by its head. Scroll over the ◐ to change how see-through it is (20–100%). A pin goes solid while the mouse is on it, and takes the mouse only after the cursor rests there for 250 ms. Each pin keeps one spot for towns and hideouts and another for everywhere else. Pins are saved in localStorage, like the theme.
- **Dock cards**: every card has a pin button. A pinned card comes off the dock and shows as a pin, and its icon gets a small pin mark. `holdOpen` ref-counts the widget's open state, so the dock and a pin can both show a card.
- **PoB**: you can pin the passive tree, a gear slot, a gem chain, or the Next step. Pins follow the build file and your level. The tree gets a `bare` mode for the pin.
- **Text**: Text size now goes down to 70%, and a new Pinned size setting (50–100%) sits in Settings › Look.

## Known limit
Cards that mark things seen when they open (market and essence alerts) only do that once while the card stays pinned.
