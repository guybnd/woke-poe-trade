**WOKEPOET-310 (idea 1):** Guy asked for a compare tooltip next to the item icons on the Gear tab.

Hovering an item on the Gear tab, or a build item on the Character tab, now shows its tooltip with the price check's swap compare underneath. The compare is against the item your synced character wears in that slot.

Special cases:
- **Rings and flasks** can be in any order. One you wear anywhere says "you wear this".
- **Rings** are otherwise compared against the ring you'd lose least by replacing.
- **Flasks** get no compare, because there are no flask stats to compare.
- **Empty slot:** the tooltip says so.
- **Off-hand next to a two-handed weapon:** the tooltip says the main hand would need to be one-handed.

The compare only shows when the stash reader is on and a character read exists.
