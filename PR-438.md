_Requested by **Guy** · [project thread](https://claude.ai/code/project/chan_01TFAVQEVFL8KKQ8fhVfpfJo?thread=cmsg_01TFAVQEVFL8KKQ8fhVfpfJoW9FtdVATJw6MNwjadWuQzn)_

Scroll the mouse wheel over the campaign dock icon, or over the steps on its card, to move the route. Wheel down marks the step done and wheel up steps back, the same as the Step done / Step back buttons.

**Behaviour**
- One notch is one step. A touchpad's small deltas add up to a notch first, and a fast spin moves at most one step per 120 ms (`dock/wheel.ts`).
- If the card is taller than its frame and scrolls, the wheel scrolls it instead, so the lower part stays reachable.
- Nothing moves once the route is asleep (past the campaign), like the step hotkeys.
- Only the campaign icon takes the wheel: there's a new optional `wheel` prop on DockItem.
- While you play, the wheel reaches the overlay when the cursor is over the icon or a pinned card, because the overlay takes the mouse there.

**Checks:** lint, renderer typecheck, all unit tests (4 new) and the build pass.

**Adversarial review:** it found two issues, both fixed. Scrolling a tall card ticked steps instead of scrolling it. And with Windows set to scroll 1 line, a single notch never stepped, so the notch threshold went from 50 to 25 px.

**Not tried in game yet.**
