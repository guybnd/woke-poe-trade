## What
A **Bulk** chip on the price check results bar. On: the table keeps only sellers with 2+ copies at one price (or a stack over 1). If fewer than 10 such sellers are loaded, the click pulls further pages of the same search (10 listings each, up to 100), then stops. Off again for each new search. Foot reads "N bulk sellers in X of Y listings".

## Trade budget
Opening a check fetches exactly what it did before. Extra fetches only run from the Bulk click, each checked against the fetch limiter first (no queueing; a note shows instead). No new searches.

The extra pages feed the verdict too (more listings priced), the same as a longer automatic fetch would.
