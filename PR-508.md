## Resistances and attributes (311)

The Character tab sums your resistances and attributes. Resistances start from the acts' penalty and are checked against each maximum.

Sources counted:
- worn gear
- allocated passives, masteries included
- socketed jewels
- the auras you socket, at their level: Purity of Elements, Fire, Ice and Lightning, and the like

The same totals are also shown with the build's gear in place of yours.

Not counted, and the tab says so: pantheon, flasks, charges, abyss jewels in gear, and other buffs.

## Can't use yet (312)

Lists build gear and gems that your level or attributes don't meet, with what's short.
