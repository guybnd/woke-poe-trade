Typing a sum in the Ctrl+Space search shows the answer as the top row, and Enter copies it.

- Plain sums: `70 * 38`, `(12 + 8) x 1.5`, `2 ^ 10`, `2k * 3`, `200 + 10%` (220, as on a phone calculator).
- Currency words price at poe.ninja: `50d / 7` answers in divines with chaos beside, `2 div + 30c`, `10 ex * 3`. Adding a plain number to currency, or multiplying currency by currency, is not an answer.
- A small recursive-descent parser in `lookups/calc.ts`, never eval. It answers only when the whole query parses and has an operator, so item searches such as `+1 gems`, `4-link`, `6l`, `Maven (1/10)` and the existing `3.5 div` answer are untouched.
- `currencyNamer` moved out of `currencyLookup` unchanged, plus "chaos orb" as a whole name (poe.ninja has no Chaos Orb row).
- The search widget is lazy-loaded and `calc.ts` imports only types, so the startup bundle doesn't change.

Look: option A of the mockups (answer row with a card); Guy can still pick B or C.
