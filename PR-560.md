## What
Price check showed both lines of every timeless jewel (Brutal Restraint, Lethal Pride, Militant Faith, Elegant Hubris, Glorious Vanity, Heroic Tragedy) as "Not recognized modifier".

## Why
The stat matcher index only held a matcher's advanced form when it had one. Timeless jewel advanced forms end the keystone name in an option range (`...akhara of Asenath(Asenath-Nasima)`), and 3.30 copy text leaves that out (`Denoted service of 7993(500-8000) dekhara in the akhara of Asenath`), so nothing matched.

## Change
`buildStatIndexes` and `make-index-files.mjs` also index the plain text of matchers that have an advanced form, unless another line already owns that text or two lines share it. No stat data change, so no data version bump.
