## What
poe.ninja priced two thin div cards wrongly for about two hours on 2026-11-22: The Witch at 7,075c (really about 0.2c) and Rain Tempter at 857c (really about 0.01c). One user's 16:00 read had Div cards at 107k c and net worth at 196k instead of about 100k. Both cards were thin: poe.ninja had no price for them on 6 of the last 7 days.

- **Hold a thin price jump** (`valuation.ts` `holdJump`): a thin item (3+ gap days of 7) whose poe.ninja price is 5× or more the last counted read's, and 1c or more higher, keeps the last read's price for up to 6 h. It takes the new price sooner once poe.ninja's falls back or the item stops being thin. `heldSince` is kept in the baseline so the hold carries across reads. Your own price, a just-removed own price, and ignored items are never held.
- **Chart**: `priceSpikes` finds a read whose price move in and the next read's move out go opposite ways, each over 25% of the value, with the second undoing at least half of the first. That read is charted at the read before's prices plus what came in, so its loot and spends still show. On the user's real ledger (336 points) it flags only the 16:00 read.
- The Holdings row shows a pause mark, and its hover gives poe.ninja's price and why it's held.

## Backtest (3 days, 211 poe.ninja pulls, every item listed)
With 5× on thin items only: 438 jumps. 142 crashed back within a day, 191 stayed up, 105 unclear. For stackables only: 22 jumps (6 fake, 8 stayed up, 8 unclear). Fakes last a median of 3.6 h, and 66% are gone within 6 h. A real rise shows up at most 6 h late.

## Open

- Display only: the table switches from the held price when prices or the stash next change, not exactly at 6 h.
