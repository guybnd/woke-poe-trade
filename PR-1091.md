NeverSink retirement M8 (WOKEPOET-721). Blocker for the full cut (own skeleton by default).

## What was wrong
With `ownBase` on (no NeverSink file underneath) many Loot pieces vanished, because they insert above a NeverSink rule and returned nothing when it wasn't there:
- every unique of every class fell to the pink catch-all (applyUniques, Foulborn, special rolls)
- every map was pink; the map floor had no anchor
- jewel, special map, gold and league rows were only built when NeverSink's blocks existed
- special gear rows and recipes went below the skeleton's catch-all, so they never applied
- with no prices the Loot tab wrote nothing, so the whole filter was pink

## Fix
- `ownBottom(doc)` in own-base.ts: on the skeleton, missing-anchor sections go just above its `woke->rest` rules. On NeverSink's file nothing changes (the reviewer confirmed byte-identical output in 24 configs).
- Skeleton rest rules: hide plain/unidentified-magic life, mana and hybrid flasks and plain generic jewels in maps; show rare jewels, magic abyss jewels, uniques, blight-ravaged and blighted maps, then plain maps by tier band in the Loot map look. The map floor goes above the plain tiers, below uniques and blighted maps.
- Our own special unique rules in place of NeverSink's (6-link, synthesised, unique jewels, idols, heist, replicas, double corrupted, abyss sockets, over-quality, 5-link, Vestigial).
- No prices on the skeleton: the Loot tab runs on last league's guesses.

## Proof
`renderer/test/woke/filter-own-proof.test.ts` + `test/helpers/proof-items.ts`: every base, unique, gem, map and stackable in the game data (4 rarities/variants), plus one item built from each of NeverSink 8.21 Very strict's 631 shown rules (457 reachable), at area 83 and 45, through NeverSink, today's filter (Loot + Equipment over NeverSink) and the skeleton, with real (day-1) prices and none. Fails if anything valuable (20c+, or unpriced and NeverSink sounds/beams it) that today's filter shows hides on the skeleton, if any unique or map is pink, or if the map floor hides unique/blighted maps. `WOKE_PROOF_OUT=<file>` writes the full diff (hostile prices and 4 area levels too).

Not in this PR (handed to their threads): Rare weights hides loud NeverSink exotic gear today (M6), levelling bottom rule (1090), heist/corpse/cluster sections (1083).
