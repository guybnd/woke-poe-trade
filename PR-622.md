Loot filter editor: rules that differ by numbers (cluster jewel passives and item level, flask area level and quality, gem level and quality…) now read as those numbers, and the numbers can be changed. First part of WOKEPOET-374 (Guy's picks A, 1+2, All).

## What changes

- **New `number` edit**: sets or removes one bound (at least / at most / exactly) of a number a tier checks: item level, passives, map tier, quality, area level and more (`numbers.ts`). Stored like every other edit, so it reapplies to each new NeverSink version. Setting a tier back to NeverSink's value drops the edit.
- **Rows lead with their numbers** when a group's tiers differ by two numbers: "Passives up to [8] · Item level [84]+", with the values in boxes you can type into. The tier's name sits under it; a rule with no numbers among them reads "Everything else".
- **Headings by item** when such a group splits cleanly by base (Large / Medium / Small Cluster Jewel).
- **No single-number order there**: Jewels › Cluster used to be sorted by passive count across three sizes, hiding item level 84 and dropping Small and the catch-alls into "Special rules". Groups ordered by one number (maps by tier, stacks) keep their ladder and "Show from".
- **Side panel "Numbers it checks"**: every number the picked rule checks, with how it compares, a remove button, and "+ Check another number". Works for every NeverSink group, ladder or not.

Groups that switch to the numbers-first list at NeverSink 2-SEMI-STRICT: cluster jewels (both groups), endgame flasks and tinctures, gems (generic, special), quality perfection, exotic corruptions, the hide layer, and the levelling rare/weapon progression groups.
