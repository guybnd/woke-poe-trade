_Requested by **Guy**_

## Why the ES mods were missing
The armour and weapon property totals (Energy Shield, Armour, Evasion, Ward, Physical DPS, APS, crit) remove the local mods they are made of, so a body armour showed `Energy Shield: 491` but never `+57 to maximum Energy Shield` or `110% increased Energy Shield`.

## Change
- Those local mods come back as unticked rows (`inTotal`), in the item's mod order, one row per stat. Picking one searches its local trade stat.
- PoE mode lists them as tooltip lines. Standard keeps them with the hidden rows.
- They are added after auto-ticking, so nothing starts ticked. The worthless verdict and mod worth ignore them. An uncorrupted fractured local mod replaces its hidden Fractured twin, so it is listed once.
