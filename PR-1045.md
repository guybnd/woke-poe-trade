- **Average drop:** `dropLookup` (economy.ts) prices a unique base by the drop-weighted average of its world-drop uniques. The weights are wiki tier estimates. - **Chase uniques:** a base with a T0 unique goes to `uniqchase`, which is Always and has its own look. Its style is picked on the section header (`CHASE_LOOKS`, default Ice blue). The drop pool always shows a chase base, and the ruler samples skip it.
- **Boss split:** a base with no world-drop unique at all goes to "Boss and special drops" (Always), whatever its NeverSink tier, and is priced by its best unique (Voices on Large Cluster Jewel). A NeverSink boss base that also has world drops goes to a new `uniqbossmix` section.
- **Always overrides off steps** in every section (`offSteps`).
- **Checkbox per unique row:** unticking it sets that item's step to 0.
- **One 6-link row:** "Any unique, 6-link" (`bts`).
- **Size cut:** 2x2 counts 75% of its price, 2x3 60%, 2x4 50%. It's Auto by default (on from ×1.25), or On/Off. Always sections skip it (`sizeCut`, `sizeCutNow`, `CurrencyItem.size`).
