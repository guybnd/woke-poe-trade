## What it does
For a checked unique, the side list beside the price check gets:
- **Foulborn**: each Foulborn kind, the mod it adds (Foulborn colour) and the one it loses (struck out), its poe.ninja price and how that compares to a plain copy (×1.7). A checked Foulborn copy lights its own kind ("this one"). Hover a kind: the Foulborn copy's tooltip. Click: the trade site's search for Foulborn copies with that kind's mods (one search, only on the click; follows the built-in browser setting).
- **Vestigial**: the mod the unique leaves on another unique of its slot.
- **Vestigial mod from**: an item carrying a Vestigial implicit lists the unique(s) that leave it, priced and clickable.

Data: tips/uniques.json Foulborn entries (poe.ninja) via search/unique-details.ts, tips/vestigial.json (PoB). Logic in `related-family/unique-info.ts`, loaded with the first unique checked; the trade link code loads on the click. No new static imports beyond what the price check already loads.

NEWS line `price-check-unique-foulborn` with a Show me demo (Carcass Jack, spot `foulborn`); release-next-notes line.
