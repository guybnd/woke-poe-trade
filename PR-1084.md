## What it does
- **Mod table.** A filter only sees a mod by its **name** (`HasExplicitMod`), and the name gives the stat and the tier. `scripts/gen-affix-tiers.mjs` builds `renderer/public/data/affix-tiers.json` from RePoE: each gear class's prefix and suffix names, with stat text and tier. The `affix-tiers.yml` workflow refreshes it weekly, by hand, and on PRs that touch the script. If two kinds of mod share a name, that name gets its worst tier.
- **Grades.** `id-mods.ts` grades each mod from the price check's valuable mod list (`price-insight/seed.ts`, moved out of drivers.ts unchanged):
  - **Great**: a top 5 mod at T1.
  - **Good**: a top 5 mod at T2, or another listed mod at T1.
  - **Fair**: one tier below Good.
  
  The points for each grade, plus Identified rare and Identified magic, are new rows under Equipment › Rare gear (Identified mods).
- **Rules.** A filter can't add points, so each score is written as the fewest counts over nested name lists that reach it (`HasExplicitMod >=N ...`).
  - An identified rare under the line hides; this is a new scope in "the rest".
  - Identified magic never scores lower than its base: under the line it goes on to its base's rule, and its rules keep the crafting-base ilvl split.
  - A name that sits inside a lower-worth name on the same classes is left out, because the game matches part of a name.
- **Marks.** An identified item with a mark that counts (links, influence, fractured, quality, synthesised...) is scored by that mark, as an unidentified tier 1 one would be. It is not scored by its mods, so a 6-link never hides because its mods are plain.
- **Base rank and item level** count only through marks. Counting them on every mod rule would multiply the rules several times over.
- **Alt+F** on an identified rare or magic item lists its mods, each with its grade and points. Show me focuses Rare gear.
- **What's new**: NEWS line `loot-filter-identified-mods`, plus release notes. Three expired NEWS lines were taken out.
