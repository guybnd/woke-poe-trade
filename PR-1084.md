## What it does
- A filter only sees a mod by its **name** (`HasExplicitMod`), which gives its stat and tier. `scripts/gen-affix-tiers.mjs` builds `renderer/public/data/affix-tiers.json` from RePoE (each gear class's prefix/suffix names, stat text, tier). The new `affix-tiers.yml` workflow refreshes it weekly, by hand, and on PRs that touch the script. **The committed table comes from an older RePoE copy, so the workflow run on this PR should replace it with the current export.**
- `id-mods.ts`: each mod gets a grade from the price check's valuable mod list (`price-insight/seed.ts`, moved out of drivers.ts unchanged): **Great** is a top 5 mod at T1, **Good** is T2 (or another listed mod at T1), **Fair** is one tier lower. Points per grade, plus Identified rare / Identified magic, are new rows in Equipment › Rare gear (Identified mods).
- A filter can't add points, so a score is written as minimal counts over nested name lists (`HasExplicitMod >=N ...`). The rules go before the unidentified rules. An identified rare under the line hides (a new scope in "the rest"). Identified magic under the line goes on to its base's rule. Names that sit inside a lower-worth name on the same classes are left out, because the game matches part of a name.
- Alt+F on an identified rare or magic item lists its mods, each with its grade and points.
- Base rank, item level and the other unid terms don't count for identified items. Counting them as well would multiply the rules several times over (about 340 rules, ~320 KB at defaults now).
- NEWS line `loot-filter-identified-mods` (Show me opens Rare gear, spot `id-mods`) and release notes. To stay under the startup JS budget I took out three expired NEWS lines (since 1.330.21/22, no test references): conquest keystones, rename runs, Atlas Tree Planner links.
