Guy reported that the see-through control on a pin "doesn't work at all".

## Cause
A pin goes solid while the mouse is on it. You set the level by hovering the ◐ button, so the pin was always solid while you changed it, and you never saw a difference. Clicking the ◐ also did nothing.

## Fix
- While you set the level, the pin shows the level you pick: when the mouse is over the ◐ or its slider, and for 1.5 s after a change.
- Clicking ◐ opens a slider (20–100%) under the header. Scrolling over ◐ still works.

## Checks
- vue-tsc and eslint pass.
- `pins.spec.ts` passes. It now checks the pin's computed opacity while you scroll and after you set the slider.
