## What changes
- **`shared/hints.ts`**: the rules every tip follows.
  - A tip retires for good the first time you do what it shows, or when you close it with ×.
  - Otherwise it gives up after 3 overlay opens. Each open counts as one showing.
  - Only one window tip shows at a time. Tooltip lines don't take the slot.
  - Never in a map unless the overlay is open.
  - State lives in `woke.hints`, bound from OverlayWindow, so the file imports no Config.
  - One switch: Settings › Look & icon row › Tips, with "Show tips again".
- **`WTipLine`** tip lines at the foot of three places:
  - The price check's mods: Ctrl / Alt + Click and right-click. Retired in `stat-actions`.
  - Net worth's rows: click a dashed number, ⊘. Retired in `WLineNum` and `lineToast`.
  - The Regex library: give a string a hotkey. Retired once any string has one.
- **Item tooltip** ends with "Right-click: wiki, trade, copy, price check" when the row's right-click opens the item menu. It goes after the first item right-click, and shows for 20 opens at most.
- **WPanel "?"** now also lists double-click the title (minimize or reset size) and Alt while resizing (no snapping). This means resizable windows get a "?" now.
- **Saved lists** (Regex, Trade searches, Atlas strategies, PoB builds) list their row keys.
- **Loot filter card** lists Alt + F, the wheel and reload, and names Alt + F in the card's foot.
- **Dock icons**: the hover says what click, right-click and the wheel do.
- The poe.ninja price you can click to switch chaos and divines gets a dashed underline. The Links tile gets small socket dots when a right-click adds the colour tiles.

No NEWS line. These are the in-game hints themselves, and a What's new line saying "tips exist" would have nothing to show.
