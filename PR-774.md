WOKEPOET-487. A Discord user's price check of Originator's Exalted Orb read "1 div each". On the live exchange, about 900 chaos offers ask 1 to 20c, and about 340 divine offers ask 1 div (~209c).

**Cause:** the first exchange search asks for both currencies. The trade site sorts it by the raw amount, so "1 div" ranks with "1 chaos". Only 15 of the first 100 rows were chaos, so the chaos side was held back as load-on-demand, and the view picked the divine side by offer count.

**Change**
- The held-back side is loaded quietly right away. A failure only marks it, and clicking it later retries with the usual error.
- `pickBulkMarket` (bulk-insight.ts) leads with the cheaper market in chaos, using poe.ninja's divine rate. A side with under 3 sellers loses to a busier one. Without a rate it falls back to the old count rule. A currency you click stays picked.
- The verdict says "Chaos offers" or "Divine offers", shows the currency icon, and gives a divine price's chaos value. A line names the other market and compares it, e.g. "Divine offers ask 1 div each (≈ 180c), 180× as much".
- Table prices carry the currency icon. The ratio column reads pay / get.
- Mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/1QJTgUJpZtA3NNXXtL1iCw (option A).

**Tests:** unit tests for `pickBulkMarket`, plus a fake-game spec for this exact case (mixed first search, then the chaos search).
