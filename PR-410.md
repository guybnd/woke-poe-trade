Adds a look that matches Path of Exile's own UI, plus custom app colours.

**Theme picker**
- One picker. **PoE 1** and **PoE 2** cards come first, each with a preview; the existing themes follow.
- Layout and button placement are the same in every look.

**Game art (PoE 1 and PoE 2)**
- Windows: the game's frame, floor and carved title bar, with a buffer inside the frame. The close coin sits in the title bar's socket.
- Panel titles sit in a centred crest with the window's icon in the dome. On busy title bars the crest is dropped so the controls fit.
- Settings is laid out like the in-game Options screen, with its header crest. It has checkbox coins, art sliders and the search at the top of the list.
- Buttons use the game's own art, lit on hover and darker when pressed.
- Tabs, slots, the rail, pop-ups and the dock are styled too.
- The scrollbar thumb lights up on hover and drag.
- The price check window uses the same frame. It grows by the frame's buffer, so the stat table keeps all its columns.

**App colours**
- Optional accent, background and text colours for Settings, the tab bar and the dock, with presets.

**Smaller changes**
- "Skip thin" and "Hide thin" now read "Skip low confidence" everywhere.
- The Disenchant footer now reads "N with no dust information".
- Fixed an item tooltip that could get stuck on screen once its item was gone.

**Known open items**
- "Skip low confidence" may not be vertically centred in one spot. Still to check.
- Headless tests can't show scrollbars, so the lit scrollbar has only been checked by eye.
- The art is GGG's, taken from the game files. Before shipping it publicly, read GGG's terms of use: get their approval, or load the art from the user's own game install.
- The price check layout itself hasn't been redesigned yet. That's next.

**Testing**
- 3220 unit tests pass; `vue-tsc` and eslint are clean.
- The e2e failures found during this work are fixed: the crest title now lets clicks through to buttons, and the price window regained its width.
- After the fixes, the affected specs pass. The full e2e suite was not rerun after the last fixes.
- One clipboard test is flaky while the dev app is running.

Try it in Settings → Appearance → Theme.
