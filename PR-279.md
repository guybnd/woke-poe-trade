## What changes
- **Split a pseudo total:** click a `Pseudo xN` chip. The mods behind the total are listed under it, indented and ticked, each with its own min and, where a swap fits, its own ⇄. The total gets unticked. Click again to fold them back; this restores the total's tick. Mods that already have their own row are skipped. A split is per item.
- **Right-click a stat row:**
  - Search only this
  - Exclude it (trade NOT group), or Include it again
  - Set the min: my roll, bottom of tier, or clear
  - Price as an element, on swappable rows only. One search each, same as the ⇄ buttons
  - Split, or Fold back
  - Copy the mod text

  Right-clicking inside a min or max box keeps the normal text menu. Nothing else in the menu searches.
- One `contextmenu` listener on the filter list serves every row. The `memory.listeners` budget goes 2260 → 2270 for it.
