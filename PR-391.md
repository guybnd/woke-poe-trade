## What
With the game's passive tree open, press **Alt+T** (or click the build's dock icon, its card button, its menu row, or the campaign card's "Draw on tree"). The nodes of the active PoB build that you haven't taken yet are drawn right over the game's tree: notables, keystones and jewels in gold, small passives and lines in cyan, plus an "N to take" counter.

## How
- `main/src/woke/tree-fit.ts` (pure TS, since the bundled opencv.js has no matchTemplate): one screenshot → half-size high-pass map → bright-blob peaks → Hough votes for the offset at each known zoom step (scaled by window height; falls back to scanning 0.1–0.9) → checks brightness under every node → refines to sub-pixel. It rejects non-tree screens by darkness, score and the share of nodes that land on bright spots. Lit (allocated) lines are read from the same shot.
- `main/src/woke/tree-overlay.ts`: follows a left-drag live through uiohook and re-fits after a drag, scroll or key press (using the last zoom as a hint). It hides when the tree closes. `tree-drag.ts` learns the tree's edges so the drawing stops where the tree stops.
- Renderer: `pob-builds/TreeOverlay.vue` is a canvas layer that hides itself (and the whole overlay on a fresh fit) before each capture. The shared on/off state lives in `tree-toggle.ts`.
- UX: hotkey and "dock icon click draws" settings in PoB builds. The card button is marked New until first used. There's a one-time tip in the PoB tree panel and on the first P press in game. README updated.
- Screenshots stay in memory only. No input goes to the game.

## Measured
On 10 of Guy's 1440p shots (fully zoomed out to fully zoomed in), every one fits within about 2 px. The fit takes about 0.1–0.35 s, and play screens are rejected. Fixtures: `testing/fixtures/screens/passive-tree-real-*` (64-colour PNGs). Startup JS grows by about 12 KB.

## Checks
Local runs: renderer lint, vue-tsc, typecheck:test, 3,092 unit tests and the build; main tsc and build; `testing/main/tree-*.test.ts`. Adversarial self-review fixes are included: the card covering the capture, focus loss from card clicks, stale on/off labels, learned edges being lost between fits of one zoom step, and a guard for a fit with no probes. Guy has tried it live on the dev build.
