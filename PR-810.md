WOKEPOET-515, part 2b. Guy picked K1 and H1 in the design artifact.

- **K1: the hold hotkey.** Hover an item and press **Alt + H** (you can change it in Net worth's settings).
  - The item joins the live run as *held outside the stash*, at poe.ninja's price, with Undo in the toast.
  - It counts under "Outside the stash" until a stash read sees its key come in, or a Faustus listing or sale of the same name shows it.
  - It refuses three kinds of item, with a toast: rares and magic items other than maps (no price, and their names don't match listings), currency (the stash and sales already count it), and items with no price.
  - The `item-text` listener is registered only while a run is going, so idle sessions have no extra event listener (perf budget).
- **H1: carry-over.** At Start, the previous run's leftovers come along but aren't counted:
  - Held items: if they reach the stash, that value comes off profit. Their listings and sales count as from before.
  - Sales not collected: when their currency arrives, it comes off profit.
  - Each run carries over once (`carriedFrom`). Carried rows can be removed.
- The stats label is now "Outside the stash" (Faustus listings, uncollected sales and held items). Ctrl+Space shows it too.
