Guy couldn't find the Wickerman module (#509) in game: it only showed with a Viscera in hand or in a campaign zone. From the round 2 mocks he picked E, F and G:

- **E, dock icon:** a bone icon joins the dock only in maps, unique maps, pinnacle arenas and campaign zones whose boss drops a Viscera. Its card shows the boss, the Viscera, its slot, and every version with its price.
- **F, League mechanics line:** that card gains a line in the same zones, "Boss drops X · slot", with the versions on hover.
- **G, Wickerman window:** a new launcher window lists every Viscera by slot, with search. The ones that drop in your zone come first, and clicking a row shows its versions, prices and drop sources.

Prices come from the poe.ninja prices the app already loads, with no new fetch. The names are "Lesser X" for Low, "X" for Med and "Greater X" for High; when a name has several rows, it picks the one whose effect matches. They appear once the Market thread's Viscera category (`woke/market-viscera`) is on master; until then the price column stays empty.

The module now reads Client.txt for the zone (`needs: ['log']`). It still runs without its window (`runsWithoutWidget`), so the dock icon, the price check strip and Ctrl+Space work with no window placed. `wickerman` is in the dock order and names, so hiding it with "Always" can be undone.

Checks run locally: eslint, `vue-tsc` (app and tests), the full vitest suite (194 files, 4,116 tests) and `vite build`. Startup JS is 1,540 KB against a budget of 1,640 KB. I also had Claude do an adversarial review; fixed from it: the "Always" hide couldn't be undone, and unique maps never matched. Still open from that review, all low:

- Blighted maps that share a regular map's name show that map's boss.
- The category-label entries never match a zone: Conqueror encounter, Elder Guardian map, and the Incarnations.
- I haven't checked the Nightmare map names against a real log.
