## Why
After "Apply N and save" in Re-tier from poe.ninja prices, a new batch of moves showed up with nothing changed. Each tier's price line came from the median of the items in the tier now, including the ones just moved in or out, so every apply moved the lines and pushed a few more items across.

## What
- `planRetier` takes the stored price moves and counts each moved item for the tier NeverSink put it in when working out tier medians. Which tier an item sits in now still comes from the filter.
- The store passes this league's active moves.

With fixed lines, a moved item already sits where its price says, so the next plan is empty until a price changes.

## Tests
New `filter-retier-settles.test.ts`: one apply leaves nothing to move; over 60 random drifted filters the new plan settles in one apply every time while the old one cascaded in more than 10; a price that falls still moves an item back. Full suite, lint, typecheck and build pass locally.
