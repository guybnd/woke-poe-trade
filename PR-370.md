## What
Net worth item rows get a right-click menu that sits under the usual item strip:
- **Set my price…** opens an input in the Each cell, in the column's unit, with a c/d toggle. Enter saves; Esc or clicking away cancels.
- **Reset to poe.ninja price** shows on rows that have your price.
- **Ignore item / Stop ignoring**, with Undo.

Your prices count in totals, categories, tab values and the chart from the next read on. Rows with your price show a pin, and hovering it says "Your price (poe.ninja: X)".

Ignored items leave every sum. The footer's "N ignored · show" lists them dimmed. "N items unpriced · show" lists rares so they can be given a price.

Everything is saved per league in the net-worth docs (`<league>-prices`).

## Accounting
- Ignoring, un-ignoring or pricing an item is never booked as a gain or a loss, either in "since last refresh" or in session gains.
- The baseline now also holds unpriced and ignored items.
- Magic and rare items get their own keys (`M:`/`R:`).
- Baselines saved before this change don't book a newly priced rare as a gain.
