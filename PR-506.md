WOKEPOET-314, first part. Guy asked for the build and character item tooltips to be as good as the price search ones, "like aspd total dmg etc".

A weapon from a PoB build or a character read now shows:
- quality
- physical damage
- elemental damage, by type
- chaos damage
- critical strike chance
- attacks per second

Values changed by the item's mods show in blue, as in the game. Below them is a DPS strip: total, physical, elemental and chaos.

These are worked out from the base in `tips/bases` and the weapon's local mods, using PoB's rounding. The new `weapon-stats.ts` does the work and has tests. The bases load lazily, the first time the tooltip shows an item.

Mod tiers and roll % are not in this PR. They need a mod-tier data file, which is ticket WOKEPOET-316.

Checks:
- Typecheck, lint and vitest `weapon-stats` (5) pass.
- `vite build` and e2e `pob-builds` (14/14) pass.
- An adversarial review found two problems, both fixed: magic weapons got no block, and attacks per second were rounded wrong.
