**Merge on hold until Guy has seen the redesign.**

The trade site can only search a Valdo map's Void mod. The Valdo strip now follows the map price check's layout (MapModStrip, pick A):
- a Trade search top line with what's set and hidden ("2 Not · 2 of 3 listings hidden");
- the Reward;
- one list of the map's mods, each with Any / Has / Not.

On the Void line, Any / Has / Not drives the trade filter, as before. On the other mods, Has and Not filter the **already-loaded** listings, so the check spends no extra searches.

## What changed
- `valdo/skips.ts` (new):
  - Mods are matched as keys, with numbers read as `#` and case ignored. A listing is checked on every line of its mods.
  - Not is saved on the active Map mods profile (`valdoSkip` / `valdoKeep`). It fills itself from that profile's "can't run" ratings, which show as red dots. Each build profile is its own template.
  - Has (`valdoNeed`) applies to this price check only. It wins over a saved Not without changing the profile.
- `ValdoStrip.vue`:
  - The strip is rebuilt as one mod list.
  - A minions line sits in the same row as its player mod.
  - The Void line stays up when the list is folded.
  - A map without the Void mod still shows its Void line, tagged "not on this map", set to No void.
- `TradeListing.vue` (Valdo maps only):
  - `fits` drops the hidden listings, and the foot says how many were hidden.
  - A hint shows when fewer than 5 listings are left.
  - The check isn't logged under the search's key.
- `widget.ts`: checks that `valdoSkip` / `valdoKeep` are arrays.
- Adds a NEWS line and a line to `release-next-notes.md`. Master is merged in, with its conflicts resolved.
