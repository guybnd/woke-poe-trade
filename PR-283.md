## What
The trade listing tooltip's roll % ignored catalyst quality. On a belt with `Quality (Attribute Modifiers): +20%`, `+51(30-50) to Dexterity` read 100% even though the raw roll is 42.5. The roll is now read against the range the quality raises it to (30-50 at 20% is 36-60, so 62%). The shown range stays raw, as in game.

## How
- The trade API sends no mod tags, so each catalyst kind matches lines by their words; prefix/suffix quality goes by tier.
- Each mod on a shared line is raised (truncated) before the mods add up, as the game does.
- If the raised range doesn't fit the numbers, the line falls back to the shown range rather than losing its roll.
- Price check rolls already used the item text's "20% Increased" note, so they needed nothing.
