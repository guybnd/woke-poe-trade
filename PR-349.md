Guy saw "Nothing matches." under the Currency chip, even though Where it is showed Currency at 7515 div.

**Cause:** the tab filter and the category filter applied together. The Fragment tab was still selected, so the table showed currency inside the Fragment tab, which is empty.

**Fix:**
- Filters are one at a time: picking a tab clears the category, and picking a category clears the tab. The logic is in `pickFilter` / `filterLines` in `ledger.ts`.
- An empty filtered table offers **Show all**.
- Tabs filter by id, so the two tabs both named "Currency" stay separate.

**Map and unique tabs:**
- pathofexile.com's stash page lists them but sends none of their items. Guy's MapStash and UniqueStash tabs both came back with 0 items.
- Templates now leave them out, the picker greys them out ("can't be read"), and their tab labels say "not readable" instead of 0.00.

Also:
- The tab picker button is now a layers icon instead of a second gear next to the window's own.
- ≈ chaos is shortened (6.77M).
