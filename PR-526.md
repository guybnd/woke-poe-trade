## Why
Rolled uniques (Forbidden Flame, Watcher's Eye, Impossible Escape...) drop unidentified. Guy's call: their unidentified price is poe.ninja's cached row, and a real trade search for unidentified copies runs only on a click.

## What
- **Price check** (from Search's Live listings or the item menu): a rolled unique is checked unidentified, with the Unidentified chip on; clicking the chip searches identified copies.
- **Search pane**: an "Unidentified on trade" row under poe.ninja's price, with a search button (one trade search, kept an hour). Never searches by itself.
- **Boss and "where it drops" cards**: rolled jewels show poe.ninja's price as unidentified (the trade price once looked up) instead of "Depends on the roll", and count in loot per kill.
- **Search cards**: item rows show their art.
- **Boss arena card**: shows a trade price when one was looked up, else poe.ninja. No lookups of its own.
- **Item tooltip**: an unidentified strip when a trade price is on hand. Hovering never searches.
- **Impresence** joins the rolled list (its element decides the price).
- The trade quote store moved to `woke/shared/` so Search, the tooltip and the price check don't load the boss tables. Names asked one at a time (`ask`) stay queued when the Boss runs panel replaces or stops its list.

Unchanged: the Boss runs panel still looks up its list on trade while it is open (as before this PR).
