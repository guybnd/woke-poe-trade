## Why
Rolled uniques (Forbidden Flame, Watcher's Eye, Impossible Escape...) drop unidentified. The Boss runs arena card showed poe.ninja's single row (Forbidden Flame 25c) because the unidentified trade lookups only ran while the Boss runs panel was open. Search showed only the poe.ninja row too.

## What
- **Boss arena card**: while you're in a boss arena, the arena bosses' rolled drops are looked up on trade (same queue: one search per 10 s, kept an hour). A poe.ninja fallback shows dimmed until trade answers.
- **Search**: a picked rolled unique gets an "Unidentified" row under its poe.ninja variants (one search after the pick settles for 0.8 s). Boss cards and "where it drops" cards show the unidentified price when known instead of "Depends on the roll".
- **Item tooltip**: adds an "unidentified" price strip when a quote is already on hand. Hovering never searches.
- **Impresence** joins the rolled list (its element decides the price: Chaos is far above the rest on poe.ninja).
- The trade quote store moved to `woke/shared/` (`trade-quotes.ts`, `unid-prices.ts`) so Search and the tooltip don't load the boss tables. Names asked one at a time (`ask`) stay queued when a panel replaces or stops its list.

Checked the boss tables against a poe.ninja snapshot: the other multi-row boss uniques differ by links (5L/6L), which show while unidentified, so they stay as they were.
