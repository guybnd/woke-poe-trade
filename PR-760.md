## What

The Market window's Prices tab gets a range picker (3h, 6h, 12h, 24h, 2d, 3d, 7d) beside c / div. The sparkline, the % change, the column header and sorting by change follow it, and the choice is saved in the widget config (`priceRange`). This is option A from the mockups.

## Where the data comes from

- poe.ninja's dense feed only has 7 daily values per item, so nothing under a day can come from it.
- The app already saves a full snapshot about every 30 minutes, kept 3 days. A new host op, `market-series` (`main/src/woke/market-series.ts`), picks up to 13 snapshots spread over the range (nearest within min(40 min, range/6)), reads them once, keeps only a Float32Array of prices per snapshot (about 60 cached), and returns one category's lines.
- If no snapshot sits near the start of the range (the app wasn't running), a row shows "Not enough data". 24h and 2d fall back to poe.ninja's daily line instead; 3d and 7d always use it.
- 1h isn't offered: poe.ninja re-prices currency about once an hour.

## Ctrl+Space search

When the Market range is 3h, 6h, 12h or 2d, the price table gains "<range> ago" and "<range>" columns. With 24h, 3d or 7d chosen it's unchanged.
