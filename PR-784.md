## Checker
- `testing/e2e/layout-audit.ts`: with `WOKE_LAYOUT_AUDIT=<folder>`, every fake-game test records text cut off by its box (with or without an ellipsis), controls spilling out of their window, sideways scrollbars, button rows that wrap or mix heights, text under 11px, overlapping text and a lone word on a paragraph's last line, each with a screenshot. `tools/layout-report.mjs` groups them. Off by default, so CI is unchanged; the gate is PR 3.
- `WOKE_VIEWPORT=2560x1440` and `WOKE_TEXT_SCALE=125` run the suite at 1440p or with bigger text. Run on all 456 tests at 1080p, 1440p and 125% text: about 430 distinct spots.
- `data-layout-ok="<why>"` exempts a deliberate case (the dock badge on the icon's corner).
