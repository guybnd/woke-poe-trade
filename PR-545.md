## What

Guy's questions from the loot filter editor, answered in the tool itself.

**Fated items (3.30):** a "Fated Lathi" showed even though its Tier 2 rule was off. Fated is a real 3.30 mark, and NeverSink gives Fated rares their own twin tiers (`t1f`/`t2f`).
- The parser now reads "Fated <base>" as the base plus an `isFated` mark. Before this, price check failed on these items with `item.unknown`.
- The filter matcher evaluates `Fated`, so Alt+F shows the right rule.
- Tiers are named in words: "Tier 2, Fated", "Fated gear", "Identified rares, not corrupted or mirrored", the tincture and flask tiers, and others.

**"Still showing" on hidden tiers:** when a tier is off or hidden, the side panel lists rules in the same group that still show its bases, such as its Fated twin, with a jump link and "Hide that tier too". It only counts rules above a still-live hidden tier, with matching rarity.

**Rules with no item list:** rules that pick items by class, item level or Fated used to show only a look editor. They now show a **Catches** section in words, for example "Not mirrored · Item level 68+ · Class: Amulets, Belts… +20". The row "Also needs" column uses the same wording.

**Pick which tinctures and flasks:** rules that catch a whole class (Tinctures, Utility Flasks…) list that class's bases. Click one to hide only it (an own rule), and click again to bring it back. The list isn't shown on a rule that's off or hidden. Rows with the same label ("item level 82+" twice) show their rule's name under it.

**Alt+F to the editor:** the card's Rule section has "Show this rule in the editor". It opens the editor on that tier and scrolls to it, as every other jump does.
