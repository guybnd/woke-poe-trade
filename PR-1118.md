Requested by **Guy** during his in-game walk. Part of NeverSink retirement M8 (WOKEPOET-721).

## What was wrong
- **The flask still hid after #1114.** Guy has Small and Normal turned off on his ruler. #1114's rows only guaranteed a Small pile (`free`), and a step that's turned off hides it. His screenshot showed "Hidden any (Small off)" on the identified magic 26% Divine Life Flask of Thawing (ilvl 85).
- **Blessing of God (a div card) had no row.** The `RETIRED` regex `^Blessing of ` skipped it.
- **Assembled Eye Jewel was missing.** The abyss jewel rows didn't list that base.

## Fix
- The fractured jewel rows (abyss, plain, cluster) and the "Life or mana flask, quality 21%+" row now carry a **Decent floor**. Turned-off steps don't hide a floor: this is the same mechanism that keeps 6-linked uniques at Big.
- `RETIRED` no longer applies to Divination Cards. Eternal Orb and Imprint are added to it, since they're legacy and no longer drop.
- `ABYSS_BASES` gains Assembled Eye Jewel.

## Proof
- The strict-line test now also turns Small and Normal off, and includes Guy's exact flask. It fails without this change and passes with it.
- New strict check: with real prices, no real drop may land in the skeleton's plain class looks. It skips scarabs (`ownType` null), `RETIRED` items, bases that only drop as uniques (Timeless Jewel, the unique-only contracts), gold piles under the Gold ruler, unscored rares and maps with no tier.

## Checks
Typecheck, lint and all 434 woke test files pass locally. One expectation in filter-loot-gaps changed: the 23% Divine Life Flask now lands on s3, the new Decent floor.
