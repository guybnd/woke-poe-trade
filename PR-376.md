Three Net worth window changes Guy asked for.

## Tab labels in the Tabs row
- The labels sit on the Tabs row itself, after "All tabs", on one line.
- If they all fit, that's the whole row: no chevron and no extra rows.
- If they don't fit, the row fades out at the right. The chevron or the faded area opens the full wrapped set below, and the chevron closes it again.
- Picked tabs come first when the row is folded. Labels stay clickable for multi-pick.
- A ResizeObserver re-measures on resize, and it measures again when labels change or fonts load.

## "Since last refresh"
- The newest 5 groups show. The 2 newest are open; older ones fold to one header row (time · N changes · net), and clicking a header opens or closes it.
- Next to "Where it is", the column is as tall as that column (at least 240px) and scrolls inside. "Show older (N)" adds 5 more inside the scroll.

## Value chips
- "Hide < 1 [d]" and "Hide < 10 [c]" filter on each row's total value.
- Clicking the label switches a chip on or off. The number (dashed underline) can be typed or scrolled, and changing it switches the chip on.
- When both are on, the higher limit in chaos wins and the other chip shows dimmed and dashed.
- Values and on/off are saved in the widget config. Both limits are also in Settings.
- The footer reads "N hidden by value · show".
