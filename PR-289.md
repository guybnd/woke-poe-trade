## What
Price check rows that the item's catalyst raised now show a small blue **Q20** tag (Guy picked mock C). Clicking any tag switches the item's catalyst quality filter (`pseudo_jewellery_*_quality`, min = the item's quality), which the price check used to keep hidden and off. The search then only finds items with the same catalyst at that quality or more. The trade site filters the item, not single lines, so all tags share one filter. It's off when the window opens, so prices don't change unless you click.

Without a catalyst filter (no provider, or no quality), rows keep the old "increased" icon.
