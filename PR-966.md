## What was wrong
Every Glorious Vanity failed to price check. The parser read it fine (the "Historic — Unscalable Value" line and the `(...)` text were not the problem). The stat data's Glorious Vanity `modFamily` still lists `…in the name of Mazcotl`, a ref that no longer exists (Mazcotl was renamed Ahuana). `filterTimelessJewelKeystones` called `findAndResolveByRef`, which threw `Unexpected stat shape`, killing the whole check. Same in en/ko/ru/cmn-Hant data. The other four Timeless jewels (and Heroic Tragedy) were fine.

## Fix
- `timeless-jewel.ts`: a family ref missing from the data resolves to the lines that share the same `modFamily` but aren't named in it (by family, not text, so it works in every language). Duplicates are skipped; nothing throws. The seed's own line is always included.
- `utils.ts`: non-throwing `tryFindAndResolveByRef`.
