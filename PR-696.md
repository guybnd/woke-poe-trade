## What

Buying something (a 50 d item) took currency out of the stash while the item went on the character or had no price, so the refresh read as a big loss. That tanked Net worth's profit per hour, the session total and the session story's "Farming profit".

- `spend.ts`: a refresh dropping 10 div or more, at least 75% of it currency, is guessed as a purchase and counts at most 0. Any losing refresh can be marked by hand (change list, or click a chart point and type the amount). A gain of 50–115% of an open purchase within 2 h (currency back, or the bought item stashed) cancels it. Everything is worked out in time order by `resolveSpends`, so flipping one call moves the ones after it.
- `diffStash` records the currency part (`cashChaos`); baselines keep each item's category.
- Gains and ledger points carry the guess and your mark; rates, `gainsBetween` and the session story use the counted value. Net worth's total and chart still show what's really there, with a dashed blue line at purchases.
