Those rules are shared by every section, so it labelled each step with its first block's conditions. The item read "S3 Corpses" or "S4 Blueprints, AnyEnchantment True".

The filter itself was right: the item matched its own Loot row. Only the card's label was wrong. This change skips the tier list (and its By strictness grid) for Loot tab items. The Loot card already moves them.
