# Loot filter guard: what is protected

Generated by `renderer/test/woke/filter-guard-report.test.ts` from the same checks the guard tests make. Don't edit by hand.

## The promise

Whatever presets you switch on, NeverSink's most valuable drops still **show**, still **make their sound**, and still get their **beam and map icon**, and their label never gets smaller. A preset may only take away what it says it does:

- **Quiet mode** takes the sound of rules NeverSink ranks below your line (it keeps tiers 1 to N).
- **Beams only for top tiers** takes the beam and map icon of rules ranked below your line.
- Nothing else may hide, silence or un-beam them.

Checked on NeverSink 8.21.0a, every strictness (Soft, Regular, Semi-strict, Strict, Very strict, Uber, Uber+) and one style (Vaal), through the same code that writes your filter. Prices are as hostile as they get: everything not listed below is priced at 0.01c, so the price presets hide as much as they can. (The dust preset gets a fixed list of bases, as the disenchant prices would give it.)

## Also checked

- With nothing switched on, the saved file is NeverSink's, byte for byte.
- Every setup's file passes the game's load check, and with every preset on it writes and reads back the same.
- NeverSink's rules are never removed or reordered; the presets only add rules and change actions.
- With no prices, or only thin (unreliable) prices, the cheap currency, card, unique and splinter presets hide nothing.
- If you hide NeverSink's top currency tier yourself, Divines go (your call), and the safety net brings them back with a sound and beam, even with every preset at its harshest.

## Open (not settled)

- **Quiet mode and beams at 1 tier** take NeverSink's second tier too, which wears a red beam. On Regular: Sacred Orb: sound off; 21 red-beam rules silenced. The default (2 tiers) keeps them. Should the lowest setting be 2?
- **A wrong price can hide a top drop.** The price presets trust poe.ninja: if Divine Orb were listed at 0.5c, "hide currency under 1c" would hide it (hidden in this check, safety net on too, since the net reads the same prices). The same goes for cards and uniques. A guard could skip anything NeverSink gives a red beam. Pinned as a known gap in `filter-guard.test.ts`.
- **Item tier from a real drop.** The tiered gear check gives the drop its item tier by hand: the copied item text doesn't show it yet (WOKEPOET-153), so the in-game preview can't judge those rules.
