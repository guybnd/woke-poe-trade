## What

- The campaign tracker now ticks off a boss kill step when Client.txt logs that boss's defeat line, for example "Sin: Abberath!" in Prisoner's Gate.
- Kill steps show only the boss's first name ("kill Ryslatha" instead of "kill Ryslatha, the Puppet Mistress").

## How

- `boss-lines.ts` holds 25 lines from Guy's real Client.txt (about 20 runs per act). Each line is pinned to its zone, speaker and exact text.
  - Acts 5-10: Sin naming each god as it goes free. Act 5 also has Bannon's line after Avarius.
  - Acts 3-4: Dominus, Daresso, Piety in the Bowels (4 known class lines), Maligaro and Malachai.
  - A line was kept only when it came once per kill, after the fight, with no death after it in that zone.
  - Fight-start and mid-fight lines are left out (Merveil, Brutus, Kaom, Shavronne, Piety's "Kill me!").
- `advanceOnKill` looks ahead only within the current zone. It never skips another boss's kill step. If you do the Harvest bosses out of order, the route waits and you tick them by hand.
- The same lines in maps (Kitava's line in MapWorldsLavaLake) don't count: the zone has to match, and only campaign areas count.
