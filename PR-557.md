## What changes

On a Viscera price check (for example Molten Core), the Low, Med and High rows are now clickable. A click prices that version:

- The item text is rewritten to hold that version: its mod line, its range (rolled at the bottom of the range, so the search finds the whole range) and its Lesser / Greater name.
- The rewritten text is parsed like any copy and replaces the window's item, so the stat filter, the slider bounds and the search all follow.
- The "yours" chip stays on your own version. The row being searched gets the highlight and a "searched" chip. A click on your row goes back to your item.
- A row is not clickable when the item's lines don't show which version it is, or when a named version has the same text as yours (Queen).

## Files

- `wickerman/viscera.ts`: `otherTierText()` rewrites the copy. Advanced copies keep the `14(14-18)%` form; plain copies get the bare number.
- `wickerman/VisceraStrip.vue`: clickable rows, remembers the original copy while another version is shown.
- `PriceCheckWindow.vue`: provides `price-check-replace-item` so a panel can swap the item.
- Tests: Guy's Molten Core (Med to Low and High), a plain-copy Skeletal Leg, and the no-match case.
