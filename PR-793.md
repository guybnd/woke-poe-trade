## What

"Where to get <gem>" rows (and any item row) further down Ctrl+Space showed no tooltip when hovered.

## Why

Any scroll hides the item tooltip (GameTooltip listens for scroll). When the list scrolled after the pointer reached a row (Ctrl+Space bringing a row into view, a wheel scroll still settling), the row stayed the hovered one, so no new mouseover came and nothing showed the tooltip again until you moved off the row and back.

## Fix

`item-tip.ts`: a scroll while an item row is hovered marks it stale. When the scroll stops (150 ms) or the pointer moves, the tooltip comes back only if that row is still under the pointer (the browser's own `:hover`) and the window has focus. So:
- a row that slid away from under the pointer doesn't get one,
- nothing pops up over the game after focus moves,
- a click or a menu still hides it for good (only a scroll marks it).

## Tests

`testing/e2e/search-groups.spec.ts`: new test hovers gem rows far down the Facts chip, checks the tooltip names the gem, then fires a scroll on the list with the pointer on a row: the tooltip hides and comes back. It fails on master's `item-tip.ts`. `openPalette` retries the hotkey until the box has focus.
