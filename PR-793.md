## What

"Where to get <gem>" rows (and any item row) further down Ctrl+Space showed no tooltip when hovered.

## Why

Any scroll hides the item tooltip (GameTooltip listens for scroll). When the list scrolled after the pointer reached a row (Ctrl+Space bringing a row into view, a wheel scroll still settling), the row stayed the hovered one, so no new mouseover came and nothing showed the tooltip again until you moved off the row and back.

## Fix

- `tooltip.ts` / `GameTooltip.vue`: the scroll hide goes through a new `hideTipOnScroll()`, so the hide hook knows a scroll caused it.
- `item-tip.ts`: when a scroll hides the hovered row's tooltip (shown or on its way), it comes back once the scroll stops (150 ms) or the pointer moves. It only comes back if the row is still shown, still under the pointer (the browser's own `:hover`), and the window has focus. Any other hide (click to price check, menu, blur, overlay closing) clears it and stays final.

Adversarial review findings addressed: a click or blur followed by a scroll no longer brings it back, a pending reshow is dropped by any other hide, and it won't anchor to a hidden row.

## Tests

`testing/e2e/search-groups.spec.ts`:
- hovers gem rows far down the Facts chip and checks the tooltip names the gem;
- fires a scroll with the pointer on a row: the tooltip hides and comes back (fails on master's code);
- after a blur, a scroll doesn't bring it back.

`openPalette` retries the hotkey until the box has focus. Release note line added.
