Personal-only stash reader. It reads Guy's own stash tabs with his pathofexile.com login. Picks A1 B1 C1 D2 ([mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/FYYuDFZxMptKQJCA38ykAC)). Ticket: WOKEPOET-206.

## Gate (C1)
- Main turns it on only when `personal.flag` is in `app.getPath('userData')` (`%APPDATA%\woke-poe-trade`, the same folder in dev and packaged builds).
- Without the flag:
  - no handlers are registered except `personal-flag` (which answers false),
  - no game-log hook,
  - the overlay never loads the code: `personal.ts` dynamically imports `net-worth/enable.ts`, which registers the module, widget, settings page, editor and hotkey.
- Tests cover this on both sides.

## Login (A1)
- Settings › Game & league gets a Stash reader section, but only with the flag.
- The section opens a pathofexile.com login window on the default session. Sign-in is detected through `get-account-name`.
- Sign out removes POESESSID.
- The app never reads the cookie's value.
- The proxy now strips `set-cookie` from replies it passes to the overlay.
- Crash reports scrub POESESSID values.

## Reading (main only)
- The first call (`tabs=1`) lists the tabs and returns tab 0. After that the reader goes one tab at a time.
- `RateLimiter` follows `X-Rate-Limit-*` and `-State`:
  - uses 60% of each window,
  - stops instead of waiting more than 90 s,
  - honours `Retry-After` on 429,
  - stops on 401/403 (signed out).
- Each read is cached per tab in `woke-personal/stash/<league>.json`.

## Net worth window (B1)
- A lazy-loaded widget with a dock icon and an optional hotkey.
- Shows the total in divines, change since yesterday and per hour today.
- Category bars (Currency, Fragments, Div cards, Gear priced, Other). Click a bar to list its items with prices.
- Prices come from the poe.ninja data already loaded. It never runs a trade search; rares stay unpriced.
- A "Since last refresh" list shows items added and removed, with the net change.

## Data layer (`net-worth/valuation.ts`, pure)
- Valuing, categories, the diff, daily snapshots and a gains log with a timestamp per refresh.
- `gains.ts` exports `stashGainsBetween(league, from, to)` for farming, session analytics and boss runs. Their UI is unchanged.

## Refresh (D2)
- A Refresh button.
- A quiet read when Client.txt shows the hideout being generated, at most every 10 minutes and at most 12 tabs. It uses the existing log watcher (an `onLines` hook), so no new timer is added.
- The window shows "read N min ago" and an error line when signed out or rate-limited.
