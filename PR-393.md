Guy stretched Atlas strategies wide on the left of the screen and minimized it; the bar landed on the far right.

`minimizeAnchor` chose the pinned side from the panel's middle. A wide panel starting on the left has its middle past the screen's, so the bar pinned to the panel's right edge. It now pins the panel edge nearer its own side of the screen. Shared by every panel (PoB builds and the rest). The anchor corner isn't used: a bar restored on the right half leaves the panel anchored right even after it's dragged back left.

Restoring an unmoved bar still brings the panel back exactly as it was; saved minimized panels are unaffected.
