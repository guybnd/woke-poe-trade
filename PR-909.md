## What
This follows up #894. Guy asked to dismiss single recents, to pin the ones he wants to keep, and to show them only in the empty box.

- Hovering a Saved or Recent line shows two buttons over the row's right edge, on a shaded backing like the profit lists' (`w-line` / `w-line-acts`). They take no room.
  - **Bookmark** saves the line, or unsaves it back to the top of Recent.
  - **×** removes it.
- **Delete** removes the picked line when the box is empty. The footer shows the key then.
- **Saved** is a section above Recent. It shows only when the box is empty and holds up to 10 lines, searches or items (`saved` on the widget config). A saved line isn't listed twice.
- It's called Saved, with a bookmark icon, not "pinned": "pin to compare" (Ctrl+P) already means the price strip at the top.
- The helpers `saveLine`, `unsaveLine`, `dropLine` and `sameLine` are in `search/widget.ts`.
- There's a NEWS line, `search-recent-save`. It has no Show me, because there's nothing to show before you've searched. A release note is included.
