Bug Guy reported: on a Primal Crystallised Lifeforce price check, "Drops from" listed Essences of Insanity, Horror, Delirium and Hysteria. "Drops in" listed Fractured Fossil, Golden Oil and Diviner's Delirium Orb.

**Root cause:** upstream's `item-drop.json` mixes real drop groups with groups of items that only go together (Harvest crafts, Blight oils, Eldritch currency). #875 read every group's `items` as drops.

**What poedb says:**
- Lifeforce has no drop link to the essences. It is used only at the Horticrafting bench.
- Reflective Oil isn't made from or dropped by the other oils.
- Embers have no Ichor link.
- Orb of Conflict is dropped only by The Maven.

**Fix:** `dropsAreReal(group)` in `related-family/recipe.ts` leaves out six upstream groups. Their items no longer show as "Drops from" or "Drops in". The siblings in each group still show as "Related to".
- Corrupted essences
- Lifeforce
- Golden Oil and the other oils
- Embers
- Ichors
- Timeless splinters (they make the emblems, which the splinter recipe already shows)

I checked every other currency group whose items are currency. They are boss or fragment drops: Shaper/Elder/Maven/Sirus fragments, Hivebrain Gland, Sacred Blossom, and reliquary keys.

Tests: I added a case to `related-recipe.test.ts`. vue-tsc and eslint pass. This is a bug fix, so there is no NEWS line.
