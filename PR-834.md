## What
Option A from the picks artifact: a **Cheapest?** column on Net worth › Listed.

- **Check** on a row runs one trade search for that item only: instant buyout (`securable`), sorted by price, then one fetch of 10. The row then shows your rank among other sellers: `1st ✓` (green), or `4th · 35 div` (red). Hovering shows the cheapest asks, the count and when it was checked. Clicking again checks again.
- Searches only run on a click. Nothing runs in the background.
- A check stays with the asking price it was made at, so a reprice brings Check back. Checks are saved per league (`<league>-cheapest` doc, newest 300 kept).
- An **Undercut N** chip filters to the listings a check found cheaper.
- Ctrl+Space "listed" shows "· 4th cheapest" on rows that have a check.
- Rare gear shows "—" because a search by name can't compare rares. Rare and magic maps are checked by base and tier, marked rough (`~`). Corrupted or rolled uniques are also rough.
- Searches by kind: uniques by name, base, corruption and 5 or 6 links; gems by level, quality and corruption; maps by tier; everything else by type.
- Adds a What's new line (`listed-still-cheapest`) and a release-notes line.
