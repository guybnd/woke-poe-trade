## What
Option A from the picks artifact: a **Cheapest?** column on Net worth › Listed.

- **Check** on a row runs one trade search for that item only: instant buyout (`securable`), sorted by price, then one fetch of 10. The row then shows your rank among other sellers: `1st ✓` (green), or `4th · 35 div` (red). Hovering shows the cheapest asks, the count and when it was checked. Clicking again checks again.
- Searches only run on a click. Nothing runs in the background.
- A check stays with the asking price it was made at, so a reprice brings Check back. Checks are saved per league (`<league>-cheapest` doc, newest 300 kept).
- An **Undercut N** chip filters to the listings a check found cheaper.
- Ctrl+Space "listed" shows "· 4th cheapest" on rows that have a check.
- Stacks are ranked by price each: a price note counts for the whole stack, the same way Listed's Suggest column reads it.
- Rare, magic and white gear shows "—" because a search by name can't compare it. Rare and magic maps are checked by base and tier, marked rough (`~`). Corrupted or rolled uniques are also rough.
- Searches by kind: uniques by name, base, corruption and 5 or 6 links; gems by level, quality and corruption (transfigured gems as their normal gem plus a discriminator, like the price check sends them); maps by tier; everything else by type.
- A Check that can't run says why: no price for the listing's currency yet, or no account to tell your own listings apart.
- Adds a What's new line (`listed-still-cheapest`) and a release-notes line.

## Review
An adversarial review by a separate agent found six issues: stacks ranked by whole-stack price, transfigured gems sent as a plain type, white bases ranked against rares, a silent no-op when the currency rate is missing, own listings counting as others' when the account is unknown, and a failed load never retried. All six are fixed in 4c9e4ef.
