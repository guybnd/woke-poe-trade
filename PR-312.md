## What
Opening **Settings > Maps & atlas > Map mods & map scan** froze the app.

## Why
The settings editor called `normalizeMapModsWidget` inside a `computed`, and the normalizer always assigned a fresh `weights` object. Every render dirtied the render again, and each pass also ran SettingsShell's deep watcher over the whole config. Caught over CDP on the dev overlay: the renderer was stuck in `flushJobs` → deep `traverse`. In a dev build Vue reports "Maximum recursive updates exceeded in component <SettingsShell>".

## Fix
- `normalizeMapModsWidget` writes `weights` only when a weight is missing, so normalizing a normal widget writes nothing.
- The editor normalizes in a pre-flush watcher, not inside the computed.
- Regression test: normalizing an already normal reactive widget triggers no effect.
