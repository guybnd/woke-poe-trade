Adds **Settings > Price check > Window style: Standard / PoE**. Standard (the default) is the window exactly as before. PoE draws the price check the way the A4 mocks (`design-mocks/price-check-poe-mode/` on `claude/project-thread-c5mri8`) show it. Ticket WOKEPOET-166. Based on master + `claude/ninja-chip-labels` (#288); merge that first.

## PoE mode
- **Unpicked mods** show as tooltip lines in Fontin SC, in the game's colours: explicit, crafted, fractured, enchant, implicit and pseudo each have their own colour. Influence, veiled, delve, incursion and essence mods get their icons, and corruption implicits get a red diamond. Hovering a line shows its kind. Gold-faded separators split the properties, enchants, implicits and explicits, and lines are ordered that way. A tier gutter sits on the left: T1 in gold, other tiers faint, blank when there's no tier.
- **Clicking a line picks it**, and it becomes the usual row: tick, text, min/max, roll line and Low/≈/High. Long text wraps (never an ellipsis), with everything top-aligned. The min box drags sideways. A picked fractured mod gets **Fractured only**, which switches the row to the trade site's `fractured.*` stat.
- **Pseudo** works the same as in Standard (`stat-actions.ts` toggleSplit). A small square "pseudo ×N ▾" tag splits the total into one line per mod, each with its own min/max and ⇄; "▴" folds them back.
- **Number fields** (links, item level, gem level, quality, map tier, stack size and the rest) become chips. Clicking the label switches the filter on or off. ‹ › arrows fade in on hover. The number changes by wheel, typing or a sideways drag (the #282 drag code), and changing it switches the filter on.
- **Header** is a 2x2 grid: name and poe.ninja chip on top, Generic/Base, Corrupted and logic chips below, with the number chips on the right. The ninja chip is #288's, which only shows where poe.ninja prices the exact item.
- **PoE parts** on the warm stone tokens: bronze bevel buttons, a pressed-in active segment, recessed input wells, square chips, 2px corners. Digits and ≈ stay in Inter.
- **Keyboard:** Enter searches from anywhere in the shown window (a button reached with Tab keeps its own Enter, and IME commits are ignored). Esc closes, or only leaves the box when one is being typed in. Tab works as before.
- Everything else stays: Improved verdict and drivers, poeprices, Collapse, Hidden filters, Item's mods, stack value, ⇄, the Flip tab and the right-click menu. Nothing searches without a click or Enter.
