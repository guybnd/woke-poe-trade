Guy picked C1 for the Net worth dock hover card.

## The card
- Header: icon, "Net worth" and pin, from the usual card frame.
- Total in divines, with "▲ +112 today" or "▼ … today" in the up/down colours.
- A 7-day sparkline, built from the same data as the window chart.
- "Since last read · ±N d", then the 3 biggest changes either way (art, "12 × Divine Orb" or "−2 × Maven's Writ", and a signed divine value). Ignored items are left out; your prices were already applied when the read was taken.
- Footer: "Read 3h ago" and a Refresh button.
  - Refresh is disabled with "in N min" for 10 minutes after a read, or until the retry time if pathofexile.com asked to slow down.
  - While a read runs, it spins and shows "reading 3/14".
- The shared Open row sits at the bottom, as on every other card.

## Empty states
- Not read yet: shows Refresh.
- Signed out (the reader's signed-out error): "Sign in in Settings" opens Game & league.
- No changes since last read.

## Cost
- `NetWorthCard` is an async chunk and the window's data loads the first time the card opens, never at startup.
- The countdown clock ticks only while the card is shown.
- Startup JS is 1588.2 KB against a 1640 KB budget.
