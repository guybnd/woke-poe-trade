## Why
The search palette (`WidgetSearch.vue`) is a lazily loaded widget, but main registers its Ctrl+Space hotkey from the saved config straight away. A press in between broadcast `widget-action` to no listener and was lost: the overlay came up with only the rail. That's the Ctrl+Space flake in forbidden-pair, search-settings and chip-settings-layout (WOKEPOET-569). `boot()` only waits for seeded widgets, and the palette is a default one. Players could hit it too, in the first second or two after the app starts.

## What
- `index.ts` (loaded at startup) notes the time of a search press while the palette isn't mounted (`earlySearchPress` in `search/widget.ts`).
- The palette opens on mount if that press is under 5 s old.
- No test-side wait: that would hide the app bug.

## Proof
Same 3 tests, 2 workers, in a cloud container:
- master: `--repeat-each 8` gave 2/16 fails (forbidden-pair). The snapshot shows no search box.
- this branch: `--repeat-each 10`, run twice, 60/60 pass.

## Checks
- Separate adversarial review found no blocking bugs. Its point that a 10 s window could open the palette late was taken: the window is now 5 s.
- lint, `typecheck:test` and `vitest related` (1157 tests) all pass.
