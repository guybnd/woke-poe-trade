WOKEPOET-205, part one. Guy wants PoB jewels in 0.4.3. He saved no picks on the [mocks](https://claude.ai/artifact/LjV41JaacDXq9eFeQtrtTH), so this follows the recommended options (A, A, A) as far as a first pass can go.

- **Parsing (main):** each tree's `<Socket nodeId itemId>` now keeps its node (`sockets` on `PobTreeSpec`). Items keep their `Radius:` line.
- **Socketed jewels:** a gem shape in the jewel's rarity colour sits in its socket. Hovering the socket shows the jewel's full item tooltip, the same one as the Gear tab.
- **Totals:** a plain jewel's mods count in the Totals list (`jewelStats`). The footnote now says Timeless and cluster jewels aren't counted yet.
- **Radius jewels:** a dashed ring at PoB's radius (`Modules/Data.lua`: Small 960, Medium 1440, Large 1800, Very Large 2400, Massive 2880). For Timeless jewels and the uniques that rewrite passives in their ring (Lioneye's Fall, Might of the Meek, the Dreams and Nightmares, the Tempered and Transcendent jewels and others), allocated passives inside the ring get an orange edge. Their tooltip says which jewel changes them. Only jewels in allocated sockets count.

Not in this PR, left on the ticket:
- **Thread of Hope** (a variable ring) and **Impossible Escape** (its ring is around a keystone, not its socket).
- **Jewels inside cluster sockets**, which aren't in the tree data.
- **Timeless jewels' new node names and stats.** These need PoB's lookup tables (zlib-packed). For now the tooltip says "its new stats aren't shown yet".
- **Cluster jewel subtrees.** These need PoB's cluster data. For now hovering the socket lists the cluster's mods (option B's content).
