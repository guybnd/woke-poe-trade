_Requested by **Guy**_

A Stacked Deck tracker module, built from Guy's picks on the mockup (A1 + A3, B1, C1 + C2 + C3, D1 + D2). Ticket WOKEPOET-161.

## What it does

- **A1 dock icon**: shows only while a session runs, badge = decks opened, glows while cards come in. Card: Opened, Spent, Got, Result, Without top card, running-result chart, cards by total value, New session. The dock's own "Open Stacked decks" link opens the window. A session starts with the first card and ends after 15 quiet minutes or New session.
- **A3 + C2 big hit**: one popup (card, value, deck number, session result) that fades after 6 s, plus an optional chime. Default 1 div; popup on, sound off.
- **B1**: in the dock card, the window and history.
- **History**: saved beside the farming sessions (`<userData>/woke-deck-sessions/<league>/<id>.json`, same record store as `woke-sessions`) and listed in a new **Stacked decks** tab in Session analytics (range and unit apply), with delete and undo.
- **C1**: a chip on a Stacked Deck price check: Open them, Open for luck, or Sell them. It uses your own decks once there are 100 (average and average without your luckiest 1% of draws, at today's prices). Before that it uses the community weights, marked `~`.
- **D1**: "Your decks give X each, Y without your luckiest 1%" in the window and the tab.
- **D2 + C3**: the Prohibited Library weights (3.29 column, 745,364 decks), bundled as `pl-weights.json` with credit in the UI and README. The sheet is public, view-only and has no terms or usage notes, and it keeps a tab for another tool, so reuse with credit looks fine. Expected value per deck and luck are shown as a percentile from a seeded Monte Carlo of sessions the same size. They're labelled as estimates. After 500 of your own decks (not counting the session shown), the weights are scaled by your own result, clamped to 0.7–1.6, instead of a fixed +25%.

Prices come from the poe.ninja table APT already downloads (`usePoeninja`), so there is no new poller. Cards come from the shared Client.txt stream (`followGameLog`), so cards drawn while the app was closed are counted at the next start.
