## What
Searing Exarch / Eater of Worlds implicit rows in the price check had no roll bar: the game's advanced copy gives those lines no `(min-max)`.

- `implicit-sources.json` gains a `shown` table: per trade stat, `[altar, rank, low, high, item class bits]` in the numbers the item shows (RePoE unit handlers applied: ms→s, /1000, per minute→per second). Built by `scripts/gen-implicit-sources.py`; the existing `eldritch` / `corrupted` tables are untouched (kept byte-for-byte from the current file).
- `eldritch-roll.ts`: a tier that rolls a range (Fortify 6-7%) spans that range like any mod; a tier with one fixed number (Endurance Charge every 14 s) spans every tier of its altar, so the bar shows Lesser → best. A roll outside the data's tier leaves the row as before.
- The data loads on the first check with an eldritch implicit; rows drawn before it arrives get their bounds afterwards (`eldritch-refill.ts`, watched in CheckedItem). Only bounds/goodness change, never the min/max boxes, and no search is re-run.
- Hover tooltip on an eldritch row: every tier, Perfect to Lesser, with the item's own lit.
