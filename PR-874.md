## What
Guy couldn't tell what the price bar's buttons do (from #839), and asked to see the actual listed item and which tab it sits in.

- **Labels:** the buttons now read **Set 19 div (under cheapest)** and **Set 20 div (match cheapest)**. Their hover says the price is saved on Net worth › Listed, for you to change at Faustus in game. The numbers in his screenshot were right: those asks were in divines.
- **The listed item and its tab:** the bar shows the item's icon and name, with the standard item tooltip, then "in Sell 1 · col 5, row 3". The tab comes from the trade fetch's `listing.stash`, now kept on `PricingResult.stash` and on a listing as `tab`. It shows only when the trade site sends it; listings read before this PR get it on their next read. When several copies are listed, their chips name the tab too.
- **Suggestions:** they copy the cheapest other ask as its seller priced it, when it's for your stack size (no rate in between). A sub-1c ask isn't copied.
- Review fix: asks come from the ungrouped listings. Grouped rows add up one seller's stacks under a single price, which made a stack's each-price too low.
- The price box starts empty, showing its "Your price" hint instead of 0.
