## What
Guy couldn't tell what the price bar's buttons do (from #839). His screenshot showed "19 DIV · UNDER CHEAPEST" and "20 DIV · MATCH". Those numbers were right: the cheapest ask was 20 **div** (the price icons are Divine Orbs). The labels were the problem.

- The buttons now read **Set 19 div (under cheapest)** and **Set 20 div (match cheapest)**. Their hover says the price is saved on Net worth › Listed, for you to change at Faustus in game.
- The suggestions copy the cheapest other ask as its seller priced it when it's for your stack size (20c stays 20c, 5 div stays 5 div), with no rate in between. A sub-1c ask isn't copied.
- Review fix: asks now come from the ungrouped listings. Grouped rows add up one seller's stacks under a single price, which made a stack's each-price too low for cards and currency.
- The price box starts empty, showing its "Your price" hint instead of 0.

## Tests
- `net-worth-listed-target.test.ts`: chaos asks stay chaos, divine asks are copied, a different stack size is priced from the each-price, and a fractional ask is handled.
- `net-worth.spec.ts`: the bar shows "Set 8 div (match cheapest)" and "Set 7.9 div (under cheapest)".
