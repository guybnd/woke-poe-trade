## What it does
- **Picker (L1):** a list with tick boxes, a search field and a tab-type filter (all, special, normal & quad, ticked). It sits in the Net worth window.
- **Templates:**
  - Reasonable: special tabs, plus normal/premium/quad tabs whose names start with `$`, `~price` or `~b/o`.
  - Currency only.
  - This league, all.
  - Save your own: name the current ticks; saved sets show as chips you can switch between or delete.
- **When it shows (F1):** before a league's first read (one tab-list request, Reasonable pre-ticked), and later from the **Tabs** button in the window header.
- **New tabs (N1):** a template follows the tab list as it changes. Your own ticks and saved sets stay exactly those tabs.
- **What gets read:** remove-only tabs are always left out, in every league. Reads, quiet ones included, only read the chosen tabs. With nothing chosen, a read makes no request at all and the picker asks. The footer shows "N of M tabs".
- **Storage:** choices and sets are kept per league in `woke-personal/tabs.json`, by main.

## Fixes from the bug review
- A different choice of tabs is no longer booked as a gain or loss. The baseline stores the tabs it covered, and a read of other tabs starts a new baseline.
- Tabs and "Read these tabs" wait until a read in progress finishes.
- Picker writes name their league, and are refused if the league has changed since.
- The picker asks again for each new league.
- A failed tab list shows why inside the picker.

- Renderer: the picker's starting state, templates, sets and ticks, the filters, and baseline tab sets.
