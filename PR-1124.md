Follow-up to #1104, two of Guy's asks.

- **Previews were far too loud.** They played at full volume, but the game plays filter sounds at its own Master × Item filter alert volume. Main reads `master_volume2` and `item_filter_sound_volume2` from production_Config.ini (new op `filter-sound-gain`, `main/src/woke/sound-volume.ts`), and every preview (game, pack and custom sounds) is scaled by it. A muted game says so instead of playing silence. The linear 0-100 scale is a guess until it's checked by ear.
- **No DLL decoding in the repo.** Guy picked shipping copies: the 26 sounds (1-16, ShAlchemy..ShVaal) are in `renderer/public/sounds/game/` as .ogg, about 1 MB, taken once from the game's ItemFilter.bank (see the README there). The FMOD/PowerShell decoder and its `filter-game-sound` op are removed.

Tests: `testing/main/sound-volume.test.ts` (gain parsing), `renderer/test/woke/filter-preview.test.ts` (URLs, every sound file present). Adversarially reviewed by a separate reviewer; fixes are in the last commit.
