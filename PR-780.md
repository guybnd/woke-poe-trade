## What
Right-clicking Links in the price check (Tooltip lines / PoE mode) adds R/G/B/W tiles, and the number row ran off the window's right edge (Quality cut off).

## Why
`.filter-name-tiles` had `flex: none`, so in the PoE-mode header (a wrapping flex row) it kept its one-line width. It now gets `flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: 0; max-width: 100%`, so it shrinks to the window and wraps its tiles onto a second row.
