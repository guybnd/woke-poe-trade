## Bug
Ctrl+Space search → a unique (Mageblood) → item menu → **Price**: the price check opened without the base, and the trade search failed with "Unknown item base type".

## Cause
The menu's Price button and the search's Live listings built a virtual unique with no `uniqueBase`. The price check's filters then fell through to the plain-item branch and sent the unique's name as the trade `type`.

## Fix
`virtualUnique(info)` in `item-menu-logic.ts` gives the item its base and the base's category, as the parser does for game text. Used by the item menu's Price, the search's Live listings, and the search's lookup tabs.

Other kinds from the same menu were already fine: gems carry their category, cards and bulk currency go through game text, plain bases and Foulborn uniques have no Price.
