Guy picked A (tinted card) on the mockup, and asked for a style picker in the maps category, so the Look row of each map section has a Style select: Tinted card (default), Dark card, White card. No sound change (Guy: off).

## Summary

Originator T16 looked like a normal T16: both black on white, and the plain T16's gold edge was nearly Originator's yellow edge.

```diff
 currencyLook(step, mapCat)                       # special maps
-  Normal/Decent/Big: bg grey/white, edge = tint
+  Normal: bg tint 65% to white, edge tint 35% darker
+  Decent: bg tint 45% to white, edge tint 45% darker
+  Big:    bg = tint, edge white
 MAP_BANDS T16                                    # plain maps
-  border 230 190 40 (gold)
+  border 150 150 150 (grey)
```

Text stays black (white on a dark colour of your own), icons stay tinted squares, beams, sizes and sounds don't change. Small (step 1) and Tink don't change.
