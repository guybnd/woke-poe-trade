Guy asked what the "5.4 [chaos] +113% [line]" at the bottom of a Vaal Orb tooltip meant, and whether it was the wrong way round.

**What it is:** one Vaal Orb's poe.ninja price in chaos, not a ratio. I checked against poe.ninja's Standard currency exchange: Vaal is 5.76 c (137.9 Vaal per divine at about 795 c). The +113% and the line are that chaos price over poe.ninja's 7 days, so they point the same way as the price.

**Now labelled** (shared item tooltip, every window):
- `5.4 [c] each`
- When the caller says how many, what they're worth together: `· 108 [c] for 20`. Net worth passes each row's count.
- Currency under 1 chaos also shows `(N per c)`.
- The change reads `+113% 7d`; the separate "7 days" text is gone.

The strip's numbers come from a new pure `marketFromRow` in `tooltip.ts`, which has a unit test. The two e2e specs that looked for "7 days" now look for "7d".
