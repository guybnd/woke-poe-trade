## What changes
- **One list for a map's lines.** The map's implicit, enchant and influence lines (`implicit` / `enchant` / `variant` tags) are now marked `mapLine` and sit on the strip. They are no longer duplicated in the "4 of 4" rows under it. Mods and Originator lines get Any / Has / Not, and implicit lines keep their default (searched).
- **Number tiles.** The chips become a 4-wide grid: game icon and name, the value, and a typed or scrolled minimum. Base map moves to a small button on the Kind row.
- **Map kind replaces No Elder.** A map's kind is its influence. The search now finds only this map's kind: its own influence lines are searched as has, and `pseudo/maps.ts` adds a "not" for each family it lacks (Elder/Shaper with options 1,2, a conqueror with options 1–4, the Originator). "Any kind" turns them all off. Base map keeps the kind.
- Behaviour change: a normal map no longer finds Originator maps unless Any kind is picked.
- NEWS line and release notes added.
