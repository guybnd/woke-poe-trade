## What
Following your level, the tree in use went only by its title, so a guide's "Level 33" tree (39 points) stayed "now" until level 54, though its points are spent by about level 36. Guy at level 39 still saw Level 33.

Now `loadoutOf` moves the tree past each tree your passive points cover. The points are level − 1 plus quest rewards from earlier acts, with the Bandits left out. The tree's cost is its nodes less the class start, with ascendancy nodes counted, so it moves late rather than early. Gems and gear still go by title, and a set picked by hand stays put.

The panel's Next, the pins and the hover card start the tree step from the title's tree when points moved it on. At level 39 that reads Next · Level 54 with what's left of it, matching the gems.
