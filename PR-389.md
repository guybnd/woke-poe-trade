## What

This adds a second batch of one-click rows to the loot filter's Presets tab. All 12 start off. Like the first batch, they are rebuilt on every NeverSink update and never touch your own rules.

| Row | How |
|---|---|
| Hide white and blue gear in maps | Edits NeverSink's own rules. Its catch-all hide stays on, and its early-map 4-link and lowest (t3) under-ilvl-86 crafting rules keep only rares. Ilvl 86 bases, higher crafting tiers, jewellery, 6-sockets, influenced and exotic bases are untouched. |
| Hide div cards under X c (2) | Top rules split by stack size, so a stack worth X still shows. Thin prices are skipped. |
| Hide maps below tier N (14) | NeverSink's per-tier map rules turn to Hide, but only for maps that aren't influenced and aren't above the zone you're in. Unique, blighted, Zana, enchanted and special maps have their own rules and still show. |
| Hide flasks in maps | Top rule for flasks under 20% quality, normal and magic only. |
| Hide uniques under X c in maps (1) | Only bases where every unique has a solid price under X. 5-links, 6-sockets, corrupted, synthesised, replica and foulborn copies still show, and so do dust bases (when the dust preset is on) and your PoB build's unique bases. |
| Hide splinter and shard stacks under X c in maps (1) | Value is the unit price times StackSize, by the same stack-size split as cards. |
| Movement speed boots while levelling | Magic and rare boots with Runner's or better, with a border, beam and sound. |
| Hide R-G-B items in maps | NeverSink's chromatic rules get `AreaLevel < 68`; its maps-only R-G-B rules turn to Hide. |
| 20% quality gems and flasks for the vendor | Up to area level 77. The gem rule sits just above NeverSink's quality-20 gem tier (even where that tier is switched off), so exceptional, special, 21/20 and 23% gems keep their own rules. The flask rule stops at item level 82, where NeverSink's endgame flask tiers start. |
| Drop sound volume X% (60) | Scales PlayAlertSound and PlayAlertSoundPositional. Works with quiet mode. Your own rules keep their volume. |
| Beams and map icons only for tiers 1 to N (2) | Like quiet mode, but it removes PlayEffect and MinimapIcon. Label colours and sounds stay. |
| While levelling, only weapons for: Auto / Caster / Melee attack / Bow / Minion | Hides white and blue weapons of other classes and dims their rares. **Auto uses the same weapon classes as Build rules › hideWeapons** (from the PoB build). This preset adds the dimmed rares and the fixed build types. |

## Skipped or changed

- **Stacked deck cards:** the Stacked Decks tool keeps no list of tracked cards, so the cheap-card hide doesn't keep any.
- **Atlas completion:** NeverSink has no rule for unbonused maps. The low-map hide instead keeps maps above the zone you're in, which NeverSink's map-up rule marks.
- **Not built:** tiered magic/rare drops wait for 3.30 launch day; there is a TODO in `presets.ts`. The regal recipe was dropped, as Guy said.

## Review

An adversarial review found four bugs, all fixed and now tested against the real NeverSink files at every strictness:

- Ilvl 84 jewellery bases were being hidden.
- Influenced maps below the tier were being hidden.
- On Uber Plus Strict, the vendor gem rule was taking over NeverSink's 21/20 and 23% gem tiers.
- The vendor flask rule was taking over NeverSink's ilvl 82+ endgame flask tiers.
