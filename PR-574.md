## What

Every row in the price check's side window can now be clicked to open its price check. That covers the family list, "Related to X" and "Drops in X". Before, the click was selective: only currency rows in the family list had it. Cards and uniques (for example The Nurse's group and its Headhunter drop) did nothing.

- **One row:** all sections render through one row and one click handler. The row model is in `related-items/side-row.ts`.
- **One price check path:** `ui/tooltip/item-price-check.ts` builds the price check. The item tooltip menu's Price button now uses it too, so cards price as cards, uniques get their mods as stat rows, and gems and currency work the same way as from the menu.
- **Correct base:** a row carries its own data item, so a unique listed on several bases opens on the base shown in that row.
- **Last click wins:** while a unique's mods are loading, a later click or a new check takes over from the pending one.

Still not clickable: rows that can't be priced by name, such as maps, invitations and captured beasts. Their behaviour is unchanged.
