## Why
Guy couldn't drag the in-map timer (the Session story glance bar). Since #744 it only took the mouse while the Settings window was open, so with the overlay key in a map it stayed click-through.

## What
- The bar takes the mouse and drags by any part whenever the overlay is open while it shows in a map. While you play it stays click-through.
- The grip shows on hover only. It is always rendered and only toggles visibility, so the bar doesn't shift when the overlay opens. Outside a map the bar still appears only with Settings open, as in #744.
- The drag reuses `ui/drag-spot.ts`. That now keeps a dragged pop-up's edges on screen, not just its centre, which also applies to the big-hit popup and the report dialog.
- New fake-game spec `glance-bar-drag.spec.ts` covers click-through while playing, the drag with the overlay open, the saved `glancePos`, and the bar coming back at its spot after a restart.
- Settings hint text updated, plus a release-next-notes line.

## Sweep
Every other pop-up that comes up on its own already drags and remembers its spot: the after-map toast, the big-hit popup, the report dialog, the run timer strip and the widgets. Hover cards, essence labels and the atlas/tree overlays are tied to what they point at, and toasts last a few seconds, so none of those need it.
