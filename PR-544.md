Price checking a skill gem now lists its other versions (level / quality / corrupted, plus the Vaal form) with poe.ninja prices from the already-loaded price data. Guy picked layout A from the mockups.

- One folded line under the name: "7 versions · 3c to 155c". The open/closed state is remembered.
- Open, it's a small table: Level, Quality, corrupted/Vaal, poe.ninja price, Trade.
- A trade search runs only when a row is clicked: one search plus one fetch of the 10 cheapest, and the row shows the price and how many are listed. The price check's own listings don't change. The copied gem's exact version is marked "yours" and isn't clickable.
- Hidden when the price check's league isn't the poe.ninja league, or poe.ninja has fewer than two versions.
