## What
Net worth > "Tabs to read" list (TabPicker.vue), from player feedback that it was too small to read and should show tab colours.

- Rows go from `--w-fs-sm` (11.5px) to `--w-fs-md` (13px), the app's row size; tab type label to `--w-fs-sm`.
- Tab names use `--w-fg`; unreadable (Unique etc.) rows dim the name.
- The 10px swatch becomes a 4px left border in the tab colour (`--tab`), faint line colour for tabs with none.

Option B of the mockups (size + colour edge). Coloured names and full-row tint were rejected as unreadable for dark tab colours / too noisy. WOKEPOET-524.
