Draft: a visible redesign, so it stays unmerged until Guy has seen it in the app and picked a scoring model (the critique thread's options are still open).

## What
A separate way to decide unidentified endgame rares (item level 68+), switched on per profile, and off by default.
- `weights.ts`: the model and a compiler to filter rules. Base rank (NeverSink's lists, as Guy picked), size and old base, drop tier, item level, corruption and mirrored all multiply. Of the other marks (links, influence, fractured...), only the biggest counts, and only on rares that aren't corrupted or mirrored. That keeps the rules to about 2,300 (+2.6 MB) at the default line; exact multiplying needed 10k+ rules and 15+ MB. The rules go above everything NeverSink has and end with their own hide, so no NeverSink rare rule reaches these rares. Build rules, presets and your own rules still go on top.
- `weight-settings.ts`: the multiplier table, defaults (Guy's values), line and stages. `presets.weights` holds them per profile.
- `WeightsTab.vue`: the switch, a line slider with NeverSink-style stage names, the share of a made-up rare pile at each loudness, 28 fixed test rares (chips showing what counts, weights vs NeverSink side by side) and the multiplier table in points (+10 doubles). The tab shows with the experimental tools (Settings, Modules: click Economy 5x), or while a profile has weights on. Because the tab is hidden for everyone else, there's no NEWS line yet. Add one when it leaves beta.
- `weight-samples.ts`: the test rares, the pile and their filter facts.

## Checks
- `filter-weights.test.ts`: 7,500 random rares on NeverSink Semi-strict at 5 lines. Each one lands on exactly the step its weight gives, through the real matcher. The tests also cover: identified, magic and below-68 items untouched, the game validator, the size cap and normalize.
- Filter guard: `HARSHEST` has weights on at the default line. Nothing precious is hidden. Rares that weights decide may change look (the tier 5 Two-Stone Ring and Stygian Vise come out Big rather than Beam at Guy's defaults). `docs/loot-filter-guard.md` refreshed.
