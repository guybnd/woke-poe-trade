## What changes
- **Cost kits (K1).** The run's name is its kit. Typing or picking a name shows the last same-name run's per-map costs under Start, and any of them can be removed for this run. Per-map lines priced from the stash or poe.ninja are marked `live`: they take the price at each stash read (`relive`, priced by `run-kit.ts kitPrice`, which uses the valued stash line with Net worth's holds first and poe.ninja by name second) and freeze at Stop. Typing a price pins the line. A `live` chip on cost rows switches it.
- **Past runs compared (C2).** The past runs list is now a sortable table: maps, profit, per map, per hour (follows the Per hour setting; `RunFinal.time` is now saved at Stop), cost a map, and per map without the top drop. A "By strategy" switch adds up runs that share a name.
- **Small sample (S1).** A mark appears beside Per map, and in the table, when a run has under 30 maps or one drop is over half its profit. Hovering it gives the per map without that drop. The top drop leaves out currency sums and map restocks (Mirrors still count).
- **Ctrl+Space.** "compare runs" or "best strategy" lists strategies best per map first, from a small saved `-run-board` doc. The live run answer gains "per map without X" and a small-sample note.
