## Why
The filter preview said Orb of Alchemy was hidden in currency t8. The preview was reading the filter correctly: price re-tiering had moved Alch from NeverSink's `t5alchemy` (shown) to `t8trans` (hidden at Strict), at 0.008c. It also moved Chaos Orb, the price unit, out of `t4chaos`.

## Change
- `economy.ts`: a currency tier's namesake orb (Chaos Orb in `t4chaos`, Orb of Alchemy in `t5alchemy`, Orb of Transmutation in `t8trans`...) is never moved by prices, and stored moves of one are skipped when the filter is built.
- `store.ts`: stored namesake moves are dropped from config, so the Prices tab stops listing them and the filter counts as changed and gets rewritten.
- Test covering the plan, the stored-move drop, and the match result.
