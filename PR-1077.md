- The Loot tab's Uniques rows come from the game's own unique list (every base with a unique on it, `shared/uniques-on-base`) instead of NeverSink's plain unique tiers. A base gets a row while one of its uniques is priced now or was last league (`item-guesses.json`). Until that table has been filled once, NeverSink's plain unique bases also keep their rows.
- Sections come from our own data. Boss drops and Chase are unchanged and come from the drop data. High variance means a unique on the base hides its roll until identified (`variable-uniques.ts`). Early league (Guy's pick) means the base's uniques sold for at least 3x their usual price, and 5c or more, in last league's first days (`day1-prices.json` vs `item-guesses.json`).
- An unpriced base is guessed from last league: its uniques' usual price, or their first days' price for Early league. NeverSink's tier guess stays only when there's no table.
- NeverSink's special unique rules (links, Foulborn, corrupted implicits, special rolls) stay.
