Guy expected a click on a gem version row to set the price check to that version. It used to run a separate inline search instead.

- A row click now sets the price check's Level, Quality, Corrupted and Vaal filters to that version and runs the price check's own search. Still one trade search, and only on a click.
- The clicked row reads "searched" while the filters stay as it set them. Clicking it again does nothing.
- Your own row ("yours") is clickable and puts back the filters the gem was copied with (corrupted any, imbued, quality waiting at 20).
- Empower, Enlighten and Enhance don't get a Quality box added.
- The inline search (`gem-versions/search.ts`) and the now-unused `useOnDemandQuote` row helper are removed. `askOnce`/`inLane` stay; Harvest swap and trade quotes use them.
