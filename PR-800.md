## Why
Guy's 7D chart still showed a block at about 1,960 div after #798. I ran `priceGlitches` on his saved ledger (390 points). It correctly found the 11-22 Rain Tempter glitch, but after skipping it the loop resumed with the last *glitched* read as the base. The read that came back then looked like a 300k-chaos dip, and that "dip" was "undone" by the next real glitch on 11-23. So 17 hours of real reads drew at about 1,960 div.

## Fix
- After a glitch, the next run starts from the read that came back (`i = end`). A glitched read is never a base.
- On Guy's real ledger this leaves only the real glitches, which were all card fakes: 11-19 08:38–09:26, 11-22 05:00, 11-22 13:56–14:41 and 11-23 07:59–08:10. The 24H and 7D lines now stay between 440 and 575 div.
- Regression test: two glitches with real reads between them.
