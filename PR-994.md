Guy wants an easy way to lower the rank of bases he doesn't care about, so even a tier 5 drop stays under the line.

- **New rank row:** "Don't care" (×0.1, about −33 points), below No rank.
- **Changing a rank:** on a test or pasted label, click the rank chip to lower that base one rank. Right-click raises it.
- **Stored per profile:** your ranks are saved in `weights.ranks` and override the filter's ranks (Base types or NeverSink).
- **Your ranks list:** every changed base is listed with its item tooltip, its old rank → new rank, a reset per base, and Reset all.
- **Tests:**
  - A Don't care t5 ilvl 86 base hides, and a fractured one still shows.
  - Random own ranks still put every item on its step with the right colours.
  - Moving 40 bases to Don't care adds under 30% rules (2,952 → 3,291).
