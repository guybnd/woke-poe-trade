A toolkit user asked for the campaign tracker to show the areas behind you, with a configurable count. Guy picked layout A from the mocks (https://claude.ai/artifact/JMz5fQ8shijAHzEFRn421J): faded rows above the current step, with time spent in each.

## What changes
- The card lists the last finished route steps, struck through and faded, above the current step. Each row shows how long that step took.
- New setting under Campaign › Display: "Finished steps on the card", 0 to 5, default 1. 0 hides them.
- Step times are stored as `state.stepStarts` (step index → when it became current). They come from log timestamps for automatic advances, and the click time for Step back or Step done.
- A time is hidden if it is over an hour (a break or the game being closed), zero, or unknown (steps skipped in one jump, old saves).
- Times reset when you enter the Twilight Strand (new character) and when route options change.

- Adversarial bug review done. It found a new character inheriting old times, stale times after a route option change, and breaks counted as step time. All three are fixed here.
