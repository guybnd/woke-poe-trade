Alt+F on a unique (say an unidentified Imperial Claw hidden by All uniques › Low) now shows a **Can drop as** list on the filter card: every unique on that base with its cached poe.ninja price, dearest first. That way you can decide whether to show or hide the base.

- **sets tier** tags the unique whose price moved the base, shown only when a price tier move put it there. NeverSink's own tier gives no reason, so nothing is tagged then.
- An identified copy's own row is highlighted.
- Each unique gets a faint "Foulborn up to X" line, marked "few listings" when that price is thin.
- Shows 6 rows, then "N more".

It reuses the filter's per-base price breakdown (`uniquesOnBase`, the same item data as the Ctrl+Space base uniques). No new data, no trade searches. The item data has no drop-restricted or league-only flag, so those uniques are listed without one.
