Phase 2 of the shared profit line item (picks A4, S1, E2, N1; phase 1 was #861).

## What changes
- **Stacked decks**: each card row is now `CardLine.vue`. Click its value to set your price for one card; hover for ⊘ (leave out) and ⋯; right-click opens the same menu. A left-out card stays in the list, struck. Got, Result, luck and the best card skip it, and a card you priced yourself is never folded into the cheap cards. Edits are saved on the live session and on saved sessions (`DeckHistory`), with Undo toasts.
- **Run summary, Best drops**: `DropTile.vue`. Click a drop's value to set your price for one; ⊘ leaves it out of the run, through the same run edits as the Runs columns. Hand-added drops can be removed but not left out. Left-out drops keep their place by value and don't take a counted drop's slot. The last-run record for Ctrl+Space uses the best drop that still counts.
- **Session analytics › Sessions**:
  - A map's eye toggle is now the shared ⊘, with Undo, a right-click menu and keys.
  - Loot chips (`LootChip.vue`) get a right-click menu: your price (F2), Back to poe.ninja's price, Leave out, Move to another map. A left-out chip is struck; one you priced yourself shows a gold pin.
  - `lootValue` skips left-out lines, and `read` keeps poe.ninja's price from the session's end.
- NEWS lines for Stacked decks and Session analytics, plus a release notes line.
