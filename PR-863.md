The bottom now comes from the stash line and the top from the band's edge.

- About 30 slots across the chart: 10 min at 3H, 1 h at 24H, 6 h at 7D.

- Coins are a touch smaller and plain gold (they read as white before).
- The hover card still lists each sale.
