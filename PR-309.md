Uniques used to start with every mod ticked (any unique with three or fewer visible rows, and every scalable mod on a corrupted one). Most of a unique's rolls barely move its price, so this hid the cheap copies.

Now a unique starts from its name, plus what sets this copy apart:
- corruption implicits, and any total that holds one (a corruption, foulborn or vestigial mod swallowed into total resistances)
- foulborn, vestigial and variant mods, as before (Watcher's Eye auras, Forbidden Flame, Split Personality...)
- explicit, implicit, pseudo and property rows rolled in the top 10% of their range (`UNIQUE_HIGH_ROLL`)
- on a foulborn unique, the other explicit mods keep the earlier rule, since which mod was replaced sets the price

The Dark Seer from Guy's screenshot now starts with only the corruption implicit and the 2/2 ES per level ticked.
