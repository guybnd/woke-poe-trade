## What
`listingWeight` weighted every listing by seller status and age: an offline seller counted 0.4, and 0.24 once the listing was over a week old. Merchant tab (instant buyout, `hasFee`) listings can be bought while the seller is offline, so they're real offers. The weighted median then pulled the fair price toward online whisper sellers, who usually ask more.

Now instant buyouts always weigh 1. Whisper listings are weighted the same way as before.

Found in the veteran critique (finding "offline-buyout").

## Changes
- `price-insight/insight.ts`: `listingWeight` returns 1 for `hasFee` listings.
- New test `insight-offline-buyout.test.ts`. Three offline 100c buyouts under three online whispers at 130 to 140 used to give a fair price of 130. It's now 101.
- `verdict-collapsed.test.ts`: one case depended on the old down-weighting, where a single 5c bargain became the fair price over three old offline 100c buyouts. The fair price is now 100, and the 5c bargain is still flagged.
