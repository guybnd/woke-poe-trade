## Why
"Compare to your amulet" compared against the active PoB build's item set. With a levelling guide active (Elemental Hit Slayer, "Level 68" set: Agate Amulet, no rings) it compared Guy's real Warped Timepiece against the guide's amulet, and called his ring slot empty.

## What
- With the stash reader on, main reads the character you last played (`get-characters`, `lastActive`, the game log's name as fallback) and its equipped items (`get-items`) with the pathofexile.com login. Gear is cached a minute, failures a minute, sign-outs are passed on to the reader. `main/src/woke/personal/worn-items.ts`
- The compare uses that gear; otherwise, or when the read failed or is over ten minutes old, it falls back to PoB and the tooltip says "your PoB build's "<set>" set, not what you wear in game".
- PoB-shaped item text gives each mod line its own heading, so two lines of one stat (120% + 42% increased Energy Shield) both count. An item compared to itself now shows no rows.
- Header: the compare is a small scale icon top right of the header (no third row), the "Your colours" chip is gone, the corrupted chip reads Any / Corr / Not.
