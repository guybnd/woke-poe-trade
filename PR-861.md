Phase 1 of one shared line item for every profit list. Mockups and picks: https://claude.ai/artifact/U7S1cPER5L6sX2ki2PNDJz (Guy picked A4, S1, E2, N1).

## What changes
- New shared pieces in `woke/ui`: `line-item.ts` (`lineMenu`, `lineKeys`, `lineToast`), `WLineNum` (click a number to change it; yours in gold with a pin) and `WLineActs` (hover ⊘ leave out and ⋯ menu). CSS in `patterns.css` (`.w-line`, `.w-line-out`, `.w-line-tag`).
- **Runs** rows (`RunRow`): click the count or value to edit, ⊘ and ⋯ on hover, right-click opens the same menu, Enter and Delete on a focused row. Left-out rows show "left out". Removing a cost, an added line or a held item, and leaving a row out, toast with Undo.
- **Holdings** (`ItemTable`): no longer right-click only. The Each price is clickable, and there's a ⊘ / ⋯ column. A left-out row stays where it was, dimmed and struck. "Ignore" is now "Leave out".
- A **Changed N** chip on Holdings and Runs shows only what you edited or left out (Guy's ask: a way to audit edits).
- NEWS line `profit-line-item`, release notes line, ui README.

## Tests
