This follows up #713 and #714. Guy: "vaal loss should count as lvl 1 corrupt … we should just indicate vaal it always assumes from a 3/20 or 20/20".

- A level drop (20 to 19, or 3 to 2) used to count as no loss. It's now priced as the corrupted level-1 gem at 20% quality. When poe.ninja doesn't list that version, it uses the cheapest version at or below that level and quality, and says which one it used, e.g. "priced as 1/0 clean". The quality-down outcome uses the same helper.
- The hover card and the Ctrl+Space card now say they always assume a clean 3/20 (or 20/20) gem, whatever yours is, and that a lost level counts as a corrupted level 1.
- A separate reviewer checked the change. Its fixes are in: the fallback takes the cheapest version, not the dearest; the price note says clean or corrupted; and the tests now check where each price came from.
