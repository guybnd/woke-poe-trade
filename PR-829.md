Second bug hunt, on Net worth and run tracking (WOKEPOET-522). Four area reviewers found the bugs, each confirmed with a repro before the fix. Two separate adversarial reviews of the fixes found 5 more problems, fixed here too. Skips what #824 fixed.

## Net worth
- Ignoring an item while its poe.ninja price was a fake made the fake its trusted price (`ref`), so un-ignoring it counted the fake with no hold.
- A divine rate swing that came back within a day was "repaired" as a price glitch, so a divine-heavy stash charted a fake dip. A glitch now has to show in chaos and in divines alike.
- Picking more or fewer tabs showed as profit or loss in "today" (panel, dock icon, card ▲). Only per hour left it out.
- Faustus listings dropped gem level, quality, map tier and links, so the Listed tab and At Faustus priced them wrong. Held maps also matched listings of any tier.
- Guy's pick: the At Faustus box now applies to the dock card's total and the goal (line and ETA), not only the headline. The card goes by the listings glance, with the window's saved worth where it has one. The total's hover says a sold listing counts again once collected. Guy chose to live with sold-but-uncollected sales not being counted, since they can't be matched reliably.

## Strategy runs
- A stash read that landed while Stop was reading Faustus listings went into the tally but not the stash change. Maps bought after Stop came off the profit.
- A past run's profit fell to about 0 when it was edited or opened after its reads left the 3000-point ledger. Stop now keeps the stash change.
- The dock card and Ctrl+Space cost per map left out maps, scarabs and fragments used up. The tally now marks map-cost items itself, so one used up reads ago still counts.
- A sale collected, or a held item stashed, between one run's Stop and the next Start was carried over and taken off the next run's profit.

## Session story, Map history
- A game crash mid-map split the run in two: a wrong toast time, two nights each with half the map, and a missed Map history row. Seen twice in Guy's real Client.txt. A quick return to the same instance now continues it, and the closed time isn't map time or hideout time.
- Party members' deaths counted in the night card and Ctrl+Space.
- The night's best drop and Ctrl+Space's last maps had no item tooltip. The best drop now uses the item's tooltip name, not the stash label ("Headhunter", not "Headhunter 6L").

## Also
- Rarity chip: the hover said Magic finds bases to fracture, but a Fracturing Orb needs a rare with 4+ mods.

## Left open
- Runs tallied before this build: map costs used up before the update aren't marked, so the dock card's cost per map can read lower than the Runs view for those runs.
- If Stop is aborted (league changed, failed save), reads that came in while it was pending stay out of the tally.
- Night deaths follow the character MapLog last saw level up, so after a character switch with no level-up your deaths can be undercounted.
- A global "per hour" setting across Runs, Session story and Ctrl+Space goes to its own thread (Guy).
