Part 2 of the layout audit (epic WOKEPOET-491, subtasks 500 and 501; the price check listings come from Guy's request in the project chat).

## What changes
- **One control height**: `--w-ctl-h` is 28px and scales with text size (24px in compact). Every button, field, select and segment in a `.w-toolbar` (WPanel's toolbar) or `.w-actions` row uses it, and so does a chip that sits directly in the row. A wrapping cloud of chips keeps its own height, and so do lists and menus that open inside a toolbar.
- **Rows that stay on one line**:
  - Loot filter's picked-items bar: Hide, Own look and Take off are now icon buttons with `aria-label`s, and Clear is a ghost button.
  - PoB build toolbar: Passive tree is now an icon button, and the panel is 30rem wide so its 6 tabs fit.
  - Lab gems toolbar: shorter option label.
- **Price check listings** (from Guy's screenshot):
  - The ×N count keeps its slot on every row.
  - When a Twice Corrupted row shows, every Corrupted tag takes its width (a hidden copy of the longer text sizes the box).
  - Prices are right-aligned, so the coins form a column.
  - Bulk and the currency chip are one height.
- **No text under 11px**: the price check's Bulk chip, add-a-mod kinds, hints and section heads, dock badges, "Vendor recipe" and the cluster picker's "yours" now use `max(var(--w-fs-xs), …)`. The new `renderer/test/woke/font-floor.test.ts` fails on any px font size under 11. Icon glyphs are allowed only from a list with reasons, and the list can only shrink.
- **Layout checker**:
  - Counts icon-only buttons and small fields in rows.
  - No longer reports a wrapping cloud of chips as a wrapped button row.

## Tests
- `twice-corrupted.spec.ts` has a new test. It checks that tags share their left edge and width, coins share their right edge, the ×4 slot holds, and Bulk and the chip have the same top and height.
