## What
A page opened in the item lookup panel (wiki, poedb, Craft of Exile, bookmarks) disappeared when you left the overlay (Shift+Space) and came back.

## Why
The panel lives in the item check widget, which had `hide-on-blur`: losing focus hid the widget, and hiding it clears the lookup.

## Fix
- While a lookup is open, the widget swaps `hide-on-blur` for `invisible-on-blur` (same as the price check browser), so the webview stays mounted and the page comes back as you left it. The flags reset on every launch.
- The item info card (no lookup) still closes on blur.
- The item check key pressed over something that isn't an item no longer wipes a hidden page (found in review).
