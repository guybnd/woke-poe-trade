NeverSink retirement M8 follow-up. This closes the last loud white and magic items that the weights still hid.

- **Exotic bases never count as Large** (`baseTerms`). Before, a large magic exotic at area 70 scored 8 × 0.5 × 0.7 = 2.8, under the line of 4. Examples: Psychotic Axe, Foundry Bow, Magmatic Tower Shield.
- **The top 5 mods are counted per class** (`classSeed` in id-mods). The top 5 now come from the entries on the shared valuable mod list that the class can roll. For example, a bow's top 5 are socketed gem levels, crit multiplier, crit chance, attack speed and % physical. Before, they were movement speed, life and other mods a bow can't roll, so a Merciless bow only graded Good.
- **Flasks:**
  - Iron Flask (on NeverSink's exotic list) now defaults to Top.
  - The flask line drops from 8 to 4, so Good flasks always show and Low ones show with 20% quality or item level 85.
  - Migration: `WEIGHTS_V` 3 moves a saved flask line of 8 to 4 once.
- Tests are added for each change. The equip-flask tests are updated for the new line.
