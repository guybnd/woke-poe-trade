## What
Guy's follow-up to #834 (R1 on the picks artifact). He also said (2026-11-24) that the rough Check is near useless for tuning a price: he expected it to open a real price check with the item's own data.

- **Cheapest? column**: the main button is now **Price check**. It opens the full price check on that listing with its own item data (mods and rolls, the same game text the row's tooltip uses), so you can tune the filters. Items without item data open by name.
- **The rank comes from that price check**: the listings it loads rank your price ("2nd · 8.0 div"), with no extra search. Tuned filters count; narrowed results count only what fits. A rank saves only for the listing Price check opened, or one you pick on a chip, because a match by name may be another copy with other rolls. Nothing saves before a listing loads, and by-name checks stay marked rough.
- The old search by name stays as a small **bolt** (quick rank, instant buyout), shown on row hover or when it failed.
- **Bar at the foot of the price check** (`net-worth/ListedPriceBar.vue`, lazy, only while Net worth is on). It has suggestions from the loaded listings (1 step under the cheapest, or match), your own price, and Save. A saved price shows as "9 div → 7 div" with a **To reprice N** chip, and clears once a Refresh sees that price.
- Ctrl+Space "listed" shows "· set 34 div".
- `TradeListing` publishes its loaded asks to `price-insight/last-asks.ts`, tagged with the item that was searched.
- Adds a What's new line and a release-notes line.
