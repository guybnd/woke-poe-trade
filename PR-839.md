## What
This is Guy's follow-up to #834 (option R1 on the picks artifact). He asked to price check an item as normal, then set a suggested or manual price on its Faustus listing, so he can reprice it in game later.

- **Bar at the foot of the price check** (`net-worth/ListedPriceBar.vue`, loaded lazily, only while Net worth is on). It shows when the checked item is one you have listed. Matching is by unique or rare name and base, otherwise by type, and by tier or gem level where those apply. The bar shows your current price and two suggestions taken from the listings the price check already loaded, so it costs no extra search: one step under the cheapest (1c, 0.1 div, or 1 div from 10 div up), or matching it. There is also a box for your own price. If you have several copies listed, a chip picks one.
- **Listed:** each row has a tag button (shown on hover) that opens the price check on that listing. Rares open with their mods; uniques and everything else open by name. A price you set shows as "38 div → 34 div" with a **To reprice N** chip. It clears by itself once a Refresh sees the listing at that price.
- Ctrl+Space "listed" shows "· set 34 div".
- `TradeListing` publishes its loaded asks to `price-insight/last-asks.ts`: your own listings left out, chaos each, tied to the checked item so a stale search never feeds a new item.
- Targets are stored in the `<league>-cheapest` doc next to the checks.
- Adds a What's new line (`listed-reprice`) and a release-notes line.
