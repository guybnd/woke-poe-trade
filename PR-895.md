An unidentified Forbidden Flame showed poe.ninja's 100c in big gold. For uniques whose roll is hidden until they're identified, that single number is a guess. Guy picked A1, B2 and C1 in the [mockup](https://claude.ai/artifact/M7fCxC7KmoLHGaStjphY3E).

**The list**
- 72 variable uniques and 99 Foulborn uniques with 2+ possible mutations. They're generated from Path of Building's data by `scripts/gen-variable-uniques.mjs` into `shared/variable-uniques.ts`, which replaces the hand list of 15. Bases come from PoB too. A unique on several bases (Grand Spectrum, Precursor's Emblem, Doryani's Delusion) is searched by name alone.

**C1: one saved unid trade price**
- Kept for 7 days instead of 24 h, and it shows when it was checked.
- The tooltip, Search, Boss runs and Net worth all use it. Net worth recognises unid uniques in the stash by their art.
- Without a saved price, these screens say "depends on the hidden roll" and leave the item out of totals. No search runs without a click.

**Boss runs**
- Unid rolled drops count as 0 until checked. The ranking marks those estimates with *, and "Check them" looks them all up with one click (Guy's note).

**A1: price check card**
- The headline is the unid trade price, or "Depends on the hidden roll" with a Check unid price button (1 search). The ninja price is struck through and the identified range is small.
- The art guess is one slim line: the name, a * whose hover says "Guessed from the art", and Change, which stays after the check.
