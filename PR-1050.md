## Bug
`CurrencySettings.off` was one list for every Loot page, so unticking Show on Normal under the Currency ruler hid Normal on Uniques, Gems, Cards and the rest too.

## Fix
- `off` → `offBy: Record<string, number[]>`, keyed by a class page (`currency`, `uniques`...) or a section id. `stepsOffIn(c, cat)` = section's own, else its page's. `offSteps` uses it (keeps PR 1045's Always line).
- Old saved `off` migrates to `offBy.currency`.
- Ruler step card: "Show in all currency" (per page), with "N sections set their own".
- Each section body: **Steps** row (Small/Normal/Decent/Big, "as the ruler" or undo).
- StateChip bias menu: "Price bias, whole section".
- Piles hidden by an off step keep that step's colours and size (no sound/beam/icon), so Alt shows them labelled. No extra rules.

## Own section ruler (Guy's pick A)
- `rulers[cat]` = a section's own Small..Tink starts before Strictness; `stepStarts` uses it in place of the main ruler × bias. `scaled()` applies Strictness to it.
- Section body: Prices row with **Own ruler** (starts at the section's current steps) and undo; the section head says "own ruler"; the bias slider is hidden while it has one.
- Top ruler labelled **Main ruler · every Loot page**, counting sections with their own.

NEWS lines `loot-filter-section-steps`, `loot-filter-section-ruler` (spots on Orbs); release notes lines.
