## Why
This follows up #963. Since that PR, a tooltip from a window at a big Size takes that window's zoom. From the instant card at Size 150% that's 2.1×. A tall listing or unique tooltip can then be taller than the screen. The tooltip has `pointer-events: none`, so the part below the screen can't be scrolled to.

## What
- `GameTooltip` measures itself. If it's bigger than the screen (less an 8px edge), it shrinks its zoom by a `fit` factor until it fits.
- It never goes below 100%. Tooltips at a zoom of 1 or less don't change.
- `fit` resets on each new tooltip and is worked out again whenever the tooltip changes size (for example when async mod groups finish loading).
- Release note line added.

## Tests
- New fake-game test: Size goes to 150% from the palette on an 800px-tall screen. The listing tooltip stays on screen, with a zoom between 1 and 1.5. The test fails without this fix and passes with it.
- Typecheck and lint are clean. The tooltip, size, item-tip, mod-tip, pob-builds, beast-altar and card-drops specs pass.
