The app had no data on where div cards drop. This adds it from the community Divcord sheet (via divicards' generated JSON), options A1 + B1 + C1 from the [mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/VXdS2HK5bDKMwk8GQitSBQ).

- **Data**: `npm run card-drops` (renderer/scripts/build-card-drops.mjs) writes `public/data/card-drops.json` (42 KB, 470 cards: atlas maps for 339, other verified sources for 144, area level, 1-in-N rarity from 3.29 stacked-deck weights). Fetched on first Ctrl+Space open / first card check, not in the JS bundle.
- **Ctrl+Space, a card's price detail (A1)**: "Drops in" section: map chips with tier, other sources, area level and rarity, Divcord button. Each map chip's tooltip is the map with every card in it and its price.
- **Ctrl+Space, a map name (B1)**: "Div cards by map" result: the map's cards, dearest first, each a card row with its tooltip.
- **Price check (C1)**: one "Drops:" chip line on the small poe.ninja card and the full window for div cards.
- Tests: unit (card-drops.test.ts) and fake-game e2e (card-drops.spec.ts, in scope.json), which the tooltip gate covers.

Open (flagged to Guy): divicards-site's data has no stated licence; it's the public Divcord sheet. The app credits Divcord.
