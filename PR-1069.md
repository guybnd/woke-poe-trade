## What
This is the levelling filter, parts 1 and 2. It follows Guy's picks on the levelling design page: gate A, currency B, uniques A, gems A, switch A, home A, links A, boots A, weapons A, rares A and jewellery A, plus his notes on templates and quivers.

### Part 1: where the Loot looks apply, and floors below maps
- **Gate:** the Loot tab's rules no longer carry `AreaLevel >= 68` by default. They now apply everywhere, so the hideout (always area level 60) stops showing NeverSink's levelling looks. The new **Levelling** page on the Loot tab offers Everywhere (the default), From the hideout (60+) and Maps only (68+).
- **Levelling rules:** one switch, on by default. Below maps it adds floor rules that sit just above the first block that would hide an item or show it quieter:
  - Supplies show at least Normal. A pile of 3 or more orbs, or 5 or more Scrap or Whetstones, is one step louder.
  - Every unique shows at least Normal.
  - Gems show Normal up to area 33, and gems with quality show Small until maps.
- **Per item:** each currency has a checkbox on the page, and Alt+F on a currency, fragment or card has a Levelling row.
- **Storage:** settings live in `presets.currency.catMode` (`lvgate`, `lvon`, `lvess`, `lv:<name>`, `lvdef`).

### Part 2: gear by build template (`levelling-gear.ts`)
- **Template:** pick a build type (Auto from PoB, Caster, Melee, Bow or Minion). Weapon type chips are shared with the Presets tab's "only weapons for" switch. Armour chips cover Armour, Evasion and Energy shield; the defaults are Caster ES, Melee AR, Bow EV, Minion ES, and Auto follows the build's own armour.
  - Shields show only for builds with one-handed weapons, and quivers only for bows.
  - Other builds' white and blue gear hides. Their rares show Small, and so do rare armour pieces in other defences after Act 3.
- **Links:**
  - 5- and 6-links show Big, 4-links Decent, and 3-links Normal until Act 3.
  - Small R-G-B items show Small until Act 3. Your build's colours are already louder on the Build tab.
- **Movement speed boots:** Decent with a sound and a cyan edge. 10 to 15% shows until Act 5, 20% or more until maps.
- **Weapons:** white and blue weapons of your type show Normal when the base drops within 5 levels of the area. This is written per 5-level area band with `DropLevel`. There is no socket requirement (Guy's note).
- **Rares:**
  - Every rare shows until Act 3. After that, rares on a base within 10 levels of the area show Normal, and older ones Small.
  - Big rares (2x3 and up) are one step quieter.
  - Rare boots and jewellery always show Normal.
- **Jewellery:** every white and blue piece shows until Act 2, then the useful bases until Act 6.
- **Placement:** the rules go over NeverSink's levelling gear, and under the build rules, presets and your own rules.
