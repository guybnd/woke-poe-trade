_Requested by **Guy** · [project thread](https://claude.ai/code/project/chan_01TFAVQEVFL8KKQ8fhVfpfJo?thread=cmsg_01TFAVQEVFL8KKQ8fhVfpfJoW9FtdVATJw6MNwjadWuQzn)_

In towns the campaign card showed a wrong XP penalty, e.g. "Lv 33 −7 · 81% XP" in Highgate.

**Cause.** Towns log an area level far above their act's zones. Highgate (1_4_town) logs 40 while the Aqueduct is 33, and Act 9's Highgate logs 67. The card compared your level with the town's level.

**Fix**
- `route-parse.ts` `nextFightZone`: the first zone with monsters ahead on the route.
- `WidgetCampaign.vue`: in a town, the band compares against that zone, and the tooltip names it ("the next zone, The Aqueduct").
- The band is hidden in a town when there's no zone left on the route, or when the route is asleep (past the campaign).
- Hideouts and other hubs were already hidden.

**Checks:** lint, renderer typecheck, all unit tests (2 new) and the build pass.

**Adversarial review:** it found nothing that shows a wrong band. Minor notes:
- If you portal to town in the middle of a zone, the band names the next zone on the route, not the zone you'll go back to. The level is usually within 1-2.
- The dock's level pin uses the same number without naming the zone.
