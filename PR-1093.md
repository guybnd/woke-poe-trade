NeverSink retirement M8 finding: Rare weights hid 296 kinds of gear that NeverSink shows loud in maps (`/mnt/project-files/neversink-retire/m8/weights-hides-loud-gear.json`). The weights do count exotic bases, item level, strands and links, but at the defaults these items scored +2 to +15, under the +20 line, because white and magic took ×0.1 (−33 points). Guy (09:53Z): cut that penalty, be critical about whether it should exist, and add a workaround if it should.

## What changes
- **White and magic go to ×1.** Their worth is their base, item level and marks, which have rows of their own. A rare's hidden mods are already counted by its bigger drop tier factor.
- **New row, Plain crafting base (white or magic), ×0.25.** It applies only to non-exotic white or magic items with no counted mark. It stops plain crafting bases from flooding the screen: the best ones show from item level 86, and plain no-rank whites never show.
  - In the compiler, the main walk is split into a rare walk and a white/magic walk. The white/magic walk's rank split has r0–r3 and rj scaled by `wc`.
  - The mark walks are unchanged, so marked white and magic items count in full.
- **Identified magic goes from ×1 to ×1.5,** matching identified rares, so one Great mod shows the item.
- **Migration (`WEIGHTS_V` 2):** saved white, magic and identified-magic values still at the old defaults move once. Values you tuned yourself stay.
- **Special gear over the weights' Hide:** the Special gear rows (M4b) sat below the weights, which hide everything in their scope, so the rows never saw that gear. `floorOverWeights` copies the shown rows that can meet gear:
  - white and magic copies go above the weights;
  - identified rare copies go just above the weights' identified-rare Hide, so the mod rules above can still show those items louder;
  - unidentified rares stay the weights' call.
  
  The original rows stay at their anchor.
- **Cheaper pins ("always show"):**
  - `joinConds` drops a pin copy that can't meet the Hide, intersecting base lists, rarities and number ranges.
  - A pinned mark gets one Small rule after its own walk, instead of a copy at every later Hide.
  
  Together this is about a quarter of the rules it cost before: all 14 pins went from +1,764 to +779 rules. The `ig1` (Great mod) pin is new, off by default.

## Before and after (white and magic cases from the M8 list, unidentified at drop tier 1)
- Before, 0 of 214 showed; after, 150 of 214.
- The ones still hidden are large exotic bases at area 70 (the item level penalty) and a few magic items with one Good mod.
- Plain no-rank white or magic items that show: 0 out of 13,824 base, item level and tier combinations.
- Crafting-rank whites now show at item level 86 only (33 rank 1 bases, 20 rank 2).
