WOKEPOET-82, built to Guy's picks 1A, 2A and 3A from the [mocks](https://claude.ai/artifact/Gghx9jLJVkozMHRYqmrggX):
- **1A, the tag:** the same ! icon Lab gems and the tracker already use. Its hover says why the price is thin.
- **2A, the filter:** a Skip thin chip on each panel, saved per panel.
- **3A, the rule:** the Lab gems rule. poe.ninja had no price on 3 or more of the last 7 days (`isThin`).

Panels:
- **League mechanics:** price rows get the !. Skip thin starts on, and drops thin rows from the lists and from the Best pick, in the full panel and on the dock card.
- **Market tracker:** the Movers toolbar gets the Skip thin chip. It drives the existing "show thin" setting, still default hidden, so nothing changes until it's clicked.
- **Boss runs:** drops get the !, judged on the poe.ninja row that prices the drop. A drop priced from the spread of its variants gets it only when every variant is thin. Skip thin starts off, because it's a money total. When it's on:
  - a drop priced from a thin row counts as 0;
  - thin variants leave a drop's min/median spread;
  - entry costs are never touched;
  - so loot and profit are a floor.

Two adversarial reviews found these problems, all fixed:
- Skip thin used to pick a sibling variant's price.
- The tag was keyed by name only.
- A 5L/6L could become a mechanic's Best.
- A thin entry fragment hid the boss.
- Rows were filtered after a group's limit.
- Thin variants still counted in a spread.
