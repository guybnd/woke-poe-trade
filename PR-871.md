## What
New tool **Scrying** (Economy). A Scrying Orb names a map; used on an Atlas map, that map's own divination cards become the named map's (poedb, Map Scrying). Only non-unique maps with bonus objectives.

- Ranks maps by **gain per map** = cards per map (3, editable) × (map's card value − your map's card value). Card value = each own card's price × its drop share (Divcord / Prohibited Library weights, `valuePerCardDrop`). Card prices from the price index, so the fake-price guard applies.
- **Price orbs**: trade lookups for the top 8 maps' orbs (poe.ninja doesn't price them), one every 10 s via `createTradePrices`, only on a click. **Pays back** = orb ÷ gain, in maps. **Buy orb** opens that trade search.
- Maps with no Scrying Orb on trade (guardian maps, Vaal Temple, Ziggurat...) are left out.
- Ctrl+Space "scry" opens it; a map's card list gets "Scry it".
- NEWS line, release note, README/docs row, Mapper preset, Config single (off for anyone with Boss runs off).
